 Throughout the summer and fall of 2026, the MICHELIN Guide announced nominees for its second annual hotel awards. Chosen by our Inspectors, the nominees for each award — Architecture & Design, Wellness, Local Gateway, and Opening of the Year — represent the very best across four aspects of hospitality. 
Today, in conjunction with the announcement of the latest hotel Keys, we're thrilled to announce the four winners for 2026.
 A decidedly creative, high-end version of an ancient form. © Desert Rock Resort
 MICHELIN Architecture & Design Award
 Celebrating hotels where architecture and interior design do not just support the experience, they are the experience.
 Winner: Desert Rock Resort — Umluj, Saudi Arabia
 Just like the ancient Nabatean civilizations that came before, the hoteliers utilize surrounding Arabian rock as their foundation — for one of the most landscape-integrated hotels in the world.

 MICHELIN Wellness Award
 Celebrating hotels that make wellness their central ethos, where facilities and experiences may prove transformative even beyond the stay.
 Winner: Schloss Elmau Luxury Spa Retreat & Cultural Hideaway — Elmau, Germany 
 Located in an isolated valley in the Bavarian Alps, six spas provide extraordinarily comprehensive facilities in an extraordinary natural landscape.

 MICHELIN Local Gateway Award
 Celebrating hotels that facilitate the most remarkable and authentic local experiences for guests.
 Winner: Londolozi Game Reserve — Kruger, South Africa
 Perhaps the original luxury camp remains among South Africa’s premier safari lodges — where Big Five sightings are standard and an associated foundation invests in conservation and education.

 Celebrating the brand-new hotels whose fresh visions made an immediate impact in their first year.
