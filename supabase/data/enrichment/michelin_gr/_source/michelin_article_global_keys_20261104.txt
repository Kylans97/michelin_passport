Use this guide to explore the best hotels on every continent, country by country. MICHELIN Keys Editor's Pick Hotels Share The new MICHELIN Keys are here. After a year-long search for new additions, Inspectors have awarded nearly 500 hotels with their very first Key distinctions and more than 80 with upgrades. 10 countries earned their very first representation in the Key selection. And 18 new Three-Keys take their place at the very top of the selection. With total numbers now spanning 155 Three-Keys, 657 Two-Keys and 2,020 One-Keys, Inspectors find a hotel scene in 2026 ready to meet an increasingly flexible and open-minded modern traveler. No longer do hotel guests hit the road strictly for business or leisure, but increasingly for both, laptops in hand. Rough-luxury experiences, from tented rooms to trailers in the wilderness, are increasingly popular. And deeply private accommodations, from tiny, five-room-or-less hotels to private villas, are in high demand. The hotel equivalent of the MICHELIN Star for restaurants is now entering its third year. Each of the more than 9,000 hotels in our selection meets Inspectors’ rigorous criteria. The Keys are the most outstanding. The best way to get to know them is to explore them yourself. North & Central America + Caribbean Updated MICHELIN Keys in the United States, Mexico, Canada — and 100-plus brand-new Key hotels and promotions across nearly a dozen countries and territories. The United States, Mexico and Canada now boast nearly 500 Key hotels between them, while Inspectors also added many special properties across Central America and the Caribbean — with the hotel scene in Costa Rica a particular standout for 2026. This small country in Central America earned the only new Three-Key hotel in the Americas, as well as four new One-Key and three new Two-Key hotels. In this cluster of the Western Hemisphere, the best hotels are often surprising in form and function. This is where you'll find plenty of wilderness glamping and jungle eco-lodges, in addition to sleek city boutiques and affordable countryside gems. In this section, too: more than twenty Three-Key hotels. Hotel Sevilla, a new One-Key hotel in Mérida, Mexico, that restores a 16th-century colonial villa. © Hotel Sevilla Editor’s Picks: Highlights from the New Selection Nekajui, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve, Costa Rica : Central America's latest Three-Key hotel is set on Peninsula Papagayo, some 1,400 acres of tropical forest and pristine beaches where you'll find some of the greatest hotels in the world. In 2026, Inspectors name Nekajuj the very best of them: a central lodge orbited by a constellation of rooms, suites and villas — some of which are reached via a wooden suspension bridge that hangs across a ravine. Cataloochee Ranch, North Carolina, USA : With its sister hotel the Swag , Cataloochee ranch is one of two hotels on the outskirts of the Great Smoky Mountain National Park to earn Two Keys this year. Fitting for its gorgeous wilderness setting, the Ranch — with a century of history and a recent renovation to its name — provides many chances for hiking and horseback riding, with upscale, modern-rustic cabins and a newly opened spa. Hotel Sevilla, Mérida, Mexico : A 16th-century villa in Mérida's historic center that was salvaged through brutalist design. A new One-Key in Mexico and one of the most creative historic restorations you’ll ever see. In South America's flourishing hotel scene, the variety of styles, experiences and prices matches the continent's equally rich diversity of cultures and landscapes. Inspectors distinguished the best hotels here with 94 Key hotels across seven countries, including five Three-Key hotels — each retaining its top distinction first earned in 2025. You'll find MICHELIN Keys in the major cities, in the small towns along the coastlines and even in the farthest reaches of the continent, a stone's throw from Antarctica. For regions with both MICHELIN Keys and MICHELIN restaurants, look to Argentina and Brazil. Las Casitas retains its Three-Key distinction in Peru. © Las Casitas Editor’s Picks: Highlights from the New Selection Las Casitas, Peru : Machu Picchu has its own Key hotels, but the country's only Three Key, for a second consecutive year, is an awe-inspiring 20-acre estate in the Colca Valley. Las Casitas makes this a must-visit destination, a more under-the-radar way to discover the Andes that's by no stretch any less unforgettable. explora Rapa Nui, Rapa Nui, Chile : The Explora brand is known across the Southern Hemisphere for adventure hotels that pair wide varieties of on-the-ground explorations with all-inclusive experiences. On Rapa Nui, formerly Easter Island, their now Two-Key retreat is an unmatched way to visit the moai statues and endless more archaeological and cultural sites. Cinco Quintas Hotel Boutique, Cartagena, Colombia : One of five new Keys and promotions in Colombia, this One-Key sits inside the walled city of Cartagena's historic center, a beautiful mix of stylish modern design and historic architecture where larger parties might rent full floors or even the entire, 21-room boutique for privacy or celebration. A strong 2026 for hospitality in Europe: nearly 200 new Keys and promotions, including eight new Three-Key hotels. Inspectors also, for the first time, uncovered Key hotels in Slovakia and Liechtenstein. These brand-new additions join a continent with the highest count of Key hotels in the world. Well represented are all the biggest countries in Europe, including Austria, France, Germany, Ireland, Italy, Spain, Switzerland and the UK. And you'll find Inspector picks in nearly every category of travel here: from ski gems in Andorra, to beautiful wine estates in Georgia, to futuristic glass boxes in Norway. As of Sept. 18, 2026, Italy leads the continent with 224 Keys, followed by France with 220 and the United Kingdom with 131. One of Europe's newest Three-Key hotels: the Amalfi Coast's Borgo Santandrea. Editor’s Picks: Highlights from the New Selection Borgo Santandrea, Amalfi Coast, Italy : Promoted from Two-Keys, this Sixties modernist structure, thoroughly renovated and set directly into a seaside cliff, is one of eight new Three-Key hotels named in Europe in 2026. Les Sources de Vougeot, Burgundy, France : This extraordinary hotel in Burgundy was once a monastery building. After a recent renovation, it becomes one of a crop of excellent new Two-Key hotels in France, complete with a Caudalie spa crafted from the château’s historic vaulted cellars. Park Hotel Sonnenhof, Vaduz, Liechtenstein : A brand-new One Key and the first Key hotel in Liechtenstein. Family-owned and operated, come for the mountain views, the culinary program run by the chef-owner, and for this Alpine corner of one of the world's smallest countries. With the addition of the very first Keys in Benin, Botswana, Rwanda and Zimbabwe, Inspectors' best hotels in Africa now number over 100 in 15 countries. In the selection below, you'll find the expected luxury safari experiences of South Africa and Kenya, the historic riads of Morocco, and the beach resorts of Mauritius and the Seychelles. But the best hotels in Africa show too some of the boldest, most creative hospitality experiments in the world, from hotels that make their homes on high-altitude suspended train lines to ghostly, shipwreck-dotted coastlines . A new Three-Key joins this selection as well, for a total in 2026 of seven Three-Key hotels in four different countries in Africa. Riad Mayfez, a new One-Key riad in Fes, Morocco. © Riad Mayfez Editor’s Picks: Highlights from the New Selection Delaire Graff Estate, Stellenbosch, South Africa : Africa's newest Three-Key hotel is a wine estate in the Cape Winelands, its beautiful grounds decorated with the contemporary art and sculpture collection of its owner. This is the place to be for premium tastings and vine views. One&Only Gorilla's Nest, Kinigi, Rwanda : One of three Two-Key hotels to inaugurate the Rwanda Key selection, this one's for trekking in the foothills of the Virunga Mountains — the same setting where famed conservationist Dian Fossey spent years studying the mountain gorillas. Riad Mayfez Suites & Spa, Fes, Morocco : Inspectors have a deep respect for the purposeful architecture of the historic riad. But in Fez, Morocco, they find a superlative new One-Key that offers another take on the proven style — one that incorporates qualities of Art Deco as well as stylistic nods to the owner's beloved London. While most new Three-Key hotels in 2026 represent promotions from properties awarded with Two Keys in last year's reveal, Asia features the rare hotel to debut with Three Keys: Soneva Secret in the Maldives. This tremendous hotel joins a class of 34 Three-Key accommodations in Asia this year. The Three Keys may grab the most headlines, but on a continent home to everything from the most iconic cultural landmarks to the most understated beachside treasures, Keys across all distinctions lead the way to both bucket-list destinations and unsung discoveries, from the beaches of Thailand to the mountains of Bhutan and the foothills of the Himalayas. The City of Dreams - Morpheus in Macau, among the new Three-Key hotels in Asia. Editor’s Picks: Highlights from the New Selection City of Dreams - Morpheus, Cotai, Macau : Of the many futuristic buildings hosting phenomenal accommodations across Asia, the Three-Key City of Dreams - Morpheus may be the most futuristic and phenomenal of them all. The crisscrossing steel exterior is the work of the late, visionary architect Zaha Hadid, and its spaces are home to a rooftop pool, spa and plenty of high-end restaurants. Shinta Mani Wild, Ou Bak Rothed Commune, Cambodia : In order to preserve the indigenous forest, this Two-Key hotel sets its 15 tented suites and all of its public spaces on elevated platforms. Some more elevated than others — as of this writing, Shinta Mani Wild is the only hotel we know of where guests have the option to arrive via zipline. Ayan Zalaat, Ulaanbaatar, Mongolia : The first, and for now the only, Key hotel in Mongolia, is this 32-room accommodation in a protected valley at the edge of Ulaanbaatar. Mongolia is one of four countries to earn its first Keys in 2026 in Asia, along with Myanmar, Armenia and Kazakhstan. The MICHELIN Keys in Oceania span the best hotels in Australia, New Zealand, Fiji and French Polynesia. But in this short list of destinations are 92 Key hotels — an increase of nearly a third from last year — and some of the most extraordinary experiences in the world. The Three-Key and Two-Key selections tend toward the remote island resorts of Bora Bora, the sublime architectural gems on the Australian coast or the spectacular rural estates of New Zealand. But you'll find too a wonderful range of hotels throughout the major cities, including a welcome collection of historic properties, underrated hidden finds and plenty of representation from all the world's renowned luxury brands. On Australia's Kangaroo Island, Three-Key Southern Ocean Lodge. © Southern Ocean Lodge Editor’s Picks: Highlights from the New Selection Southern Ocean Lodge, Kangaroo Island, Australia : One of the two new Three-Keys in Oceania along with Saffire Freycinet , the Southern Ocean Lodge is a shining example of creative modern architecture. In neutral tones and ample glass, it's set parallel to the ocean coastline in a remote and rugged locale excellent for wildlife and nature excursions. Rosewood Matakauri, Queenstown, New Zealand : The intimate New Zealand lodge is one of the greatest hotel forms in the hemisphere, and this Two-key boasts some of the best views in the country to go with everything from humble nature walks to helicopter tours — or, simply, visits to the private spa. The Porter House Hotel, Sydney, Australia : A mid-size hotel in Sydney that preserves the arresting exterior of an 1876-vintage tobacco warehouse, while also connecting it to a new, 36-storey tower. Today, a One-Key hotel with a bevy of modern comforts.
