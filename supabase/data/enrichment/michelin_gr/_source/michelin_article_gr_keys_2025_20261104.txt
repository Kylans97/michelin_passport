The Four Seasons Astir Palace is massive, both in physicality and reputation. With a legacy dating back to 1958, the guest list includes names like Nelson Mandela and Jackie Kennedy. But from the very moment of arrival, our Inspectors felt a welcoming, laid-back, leisurely vibe at this historic beachfront site that more or less invented the modern Athens Riviera, a glamorous stretch of coastline just outside the capital. Astir Palace is a true, self-contained getaway. Expect: many restaurants, three on-site bays ensconced in trees, yacht excursions, visits to the temple of Poseidon, olive oil tastings, on-site tennis, pottery classes, and nearly any other diversion, luxury, or convenience you might imagine.
