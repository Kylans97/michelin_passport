The conclusions drawn from the inspectors' deliberations following their travels throughout the country once again highlight Greece's strong commitment to gastronomy as a defining element of its tourism offering. Indeed, this has led to the creation of a selection featuring 62 restaurants, representing a genuine revolution in the country's culinary landscape. The historic city of Athens, cradle of Western civilization, remains the country's main attraction. However, far from solely concentrating Greece's gastronomic wealth, it serves as the driving force behind a far broader and more diverse culinary scene. At the same time, two other remarkable gastronomic hubs are gaining prominence: the cosmopolitan city of Thessaloniki, where Eastern and Western influences converge, and the spectacular volcanic island of Santorini, which brings the flavours and distinctive culinary character of the Cyclades to the table. Greek gastronomy is enjoying a moment of remarkable international prominence, driven by a new generation of chefs who are reinterpreting the country's culinary heritage through a contemporary lens while remaining true to their roots. In major cities such as Athens and Thessaloniki, innovation coexists with tradition in a vibrant and creative dining scene, while destinations such as Santorini have turned gastronomic excellence into one of their key attractions. Greek cuisine is far more than a cultural expression; it embodies a philosophy of life based on balance, simplicity and respect for local produce. Taken as a whole, Greece offers an admirable balance between avant-garde cuisine and respect for centuries-old culinary traditions, supported by the extraordinary quality of local ingredients. With these foundations in place, the country has successfully combined flavour, identity and wellbeing Gwendal Poullennec International Director of the MICHELIN Guide Lycabettus (Oia, Santorini) Shines with Two MICHELIN Stars Its achievement is particularly remarkable, as there are only a handful of cases worldwide where a restaurant has entered the Guide and been awarded Two MICHELIN Stars immediately. Located within the Andronis Luxury Suites, this exclusive restaurant boasts one of Oia's most iconic terraces, offering breathtaking views overlooking Santorini's caldera. Under the direction of chefs Christos Karagiannis and Petros Brandenburg , the culinary proposal combines contemporary elegance, local produce and French-inspired techniques. Available both as a tasting menu and à la carte, the cuisine includes standout creations such as Squid with Red Mullet Sauce and Lobster with Carrot Purée. The experience is complemented by truly exclusive service, an impressive cocktail selection and a wine cellar featuring more than 1,000 labels, many available by the glass, firmly establishing the restaurant as one of the island's most attractive gastronomic destinations. Seven New One MICHELIN Star Restaurants The inspection team has awarded Seven additional restaurants with One MICHELIN Star (four in Athens and three in Santorini). Following these additions, a total of 19 establishments now hold the prestigious distinction. Particular recognition should be given to chef Ettore Botrini , who has earned the distinction in two restaurants simultaneously. Already awarded One MICHELIN Star for Botrini's in Athens, he is now recognised for his work at Botrini's Santorini and Selene, both located in Santorini. Aleria, Athens Set within an elegant 19th-century neoclassical mansion in the heart of Metaxourgeio, time seems to stand still in dining rooms complemented by atmospheric courtyards. Chef Gikas Xenakis reinterprets Greek-Mediterranean cuisine with sensitivity and creativity, highlighting local seasonal ingredients. His tasting menus, supported by a cellar of more than 400 wine references, provide a refined experience featuring vibrant dishes such as Crab with Fennel, Peach and Chamomile. Botrini's Santorini, Santorini Perched above Santorini's caldera amid cascading terraces and unforgettable views over the Aegean Sea, this elegant restaurant turns every dinner into a sensory journey. Chef Ettore Botrini , supported by resident chef George Skopelitis, elevates Greek tradition to fine dining through refined techniques and subtle Italian influences. Dishes such as Bottoni Octopus and the intensely flavoured Bourdeto are highlights of a soulful menu best accompanied by a pairing celebrating the richness of Greek wines. Lauda, Santorini Nestled on Santorini's caldera cliffs, Lauda, the island's oldest restaurant, offers an experience where Greek identity meets the precision of French haute cuisine. Inspired by French chef Emmanuel Renaut and executed by resident chef Orestis Sotiriou , its tasting menus celebrate local flavours with impeccable elegance. Highlights include Aggelos' Scorpion Fish & Lemon Verbena and a refined reinterpretation of traditional Kakavia featuring lobster gnocchi and vegetables. see|ds, Athens In the refined district of Ilisia, see|ds showcases chef Dimos Samourakis ' creative vision through contemporary cuisine of remarkable elegance. The seasonal tasting menu, inspired by local ingredients and subtly influenced by French gastronomy, stands out for its technical precision and minimalist aesthetic. The experience becomes even more personal when the chefs themselves present and finish dishes tableside, including memorable creations such as Cod with Artichokes, Courgette and Lobster-Pepper Sauce. Selene, Santorini Located in the heart of Fira's old Venetian quarter, among historic alleyways and the tranquil courtyard of an 18th-century monastery, this iconic restaurant reaffirms its place as a pioneer of fine dining in Santorini. Under the direction of chef Ettore Botrini and day-to-day leadership of Thomas Mposmos, its tasting menus reinterpret local traditions with elegance, technical refinement and delicate textures. Highlights include a reinvented Spanakotiropita and locally raised pigeon, complemented by an outstanding wine selection. SENSE, Athens High above the rooftops of Athens, offering one of the most spectacular views of the Acropolis, SENSE transforms Greek culinary memories into a sophisticated contemporary experience. Chef Alexandros Charalabopoulos reimagines traditional recipes and childhood flavours with creativity, technique and aesthetic sensitivity. His tasting menus celebrate the richness of Greek terroir through surprising dishes such as the delicate Vine Leaf trompe-l'œil. Simul, Athens In the vibrant neighbourhood of Kolonaki, Simul presents a gastronomic concept that is both bold and sophisticated. Chef Nikos Thomas combines seasonal Greek produce with fine dining techniques and international influences to create a single tasting menu full of character, creativity and precision. In a relaxed contemporary setting, dishes such as Prawns with Fermented Honey and Powdered Foie Gras shine alongside a carefully curated wine selection. 7 New Bib Gourmand Celebrate Excellence at an Affordable Price Few distinctions generate as much enthusiasm among food lovers as the Bib Gourmand, symbolised by the famous licking Bibendum and reserved for establishments offering outstanding value for money. This year, the category welcomes seven new additions, bringing the total number of Bib Gourmand restaurants in Greece to 10. The inclusion of Thessaloniki in the Guide has played a decisive role in the growth of this category, as most of the new Bib Gourmand restaurants are located there. Athens: UPON Zohós Thessaloniki: Deka trapezia Menu me… nou Sintrofi Three Little Pigs To Manitari 21 New Recommended Restaurants Greece's culinary vitality is once again reflected in the continuous opening of new restaurants. Of the 32 Recommended establishments featured in the 2027 selection, an impressive 21 are new additions, demonstrating both the strength of the country's gastronomic development and its considerable growth potential. Among the new Athens additions, particular highlights include the innovative gastronomic concept developed by talented chef Tasos Mantis at Amphibian ; the reinterpretation of classic Greek cooking proposed by chef Nikos Karathanos at Ateno Greek Cuisine; the strong focus on seafood championed by chef Georgianna Hiliadaki at IODIO ; and the Italian flavours with an international touch showcased at Sole Giaguaro Athens Italian Dining House . In Thessaloniki, the inspectors were particularly impressed by 7 Thalasses , which pays tribute to the richness of the seas; Charoupi , where the essence of traditional Cretan cuisine comes to life; L'Albero , with its blend of Italian and Greek influences; and Akti Ntovil , where guests can enjoy spectacular sunsets overlooking the Thermaic Gulf. Four Special Awards Created to celebrate the talent and expertise that transform dining experiences into unforgettable memories, the MICHELIN Special Awards recognise several outstanding professionals in the 2027 edition. MICHELIN Young Chef Award At just 29 years of age, Theodoros Kaloidis leads the kitchen at To Manitari in Thessaloniki, working closely with chef Dimitris Pamporis, who serves as culinary advisor. His reinterpretation of Greek culinary traditions stands out for its technical skill, successfully updating classic dishes while adding a distinctive personal touch. MICHELIN Service Award Christian Potelle , the experienced French-born Director of the acclaimed restaurant Spondi in Athens, is constantly attentive to every detail in the dining room. Viewing guests as "invitees" rather than customers, he elevates the concept of hospitality to the highest level while maintaining impeccable professionalism. MICHELIN Sommelier Award At Aleria in Athens, sommelier Despina Tokalakis shines through her extensive wine knowledge and her ability to communicate her passion to guests, enhancing the dining experience and making it truly memorable. MICHELIN Award for Opening of the Year This award goes to Amphibian in Athens. Led gastronomically by chef Tasos Mantis, who is also behind the acclaimed Soil, the restaurant impresses both through its rooftop location atop the Onassis Stegi Cultural Centre and its innovative culinary concept, described as "transitional gastronomy". Mindful Voices / Voix Engagées Created in 2026, the new Mindful Voices platform brings together inspiring personalities from across the hospitality industry. It serves as an editorial space where chefs, hoteliers and winemakers can share their stories and innovative practices with both their peers and the wider public. Gwendal Poullennec, International Director of the MICHELIN Guide, announced: "Mindful Voices / Voix Engagées will give a platform to those who are driving change within their respective fields. This new initiative reflects what our inspection teams encounter in the field every day: inspiring people and experiences that are transforming practices and deserve to be shared. Faithful to its DNA and values, the MICHELIN Guide seeks to amplify the reach and resonance of these voices from the worlds of gastronomy, hospitality and wine so that they may be heard around the globe." About Michelin: Michelin is building a world-leading manufacturer of life-changing composites and experiences. Pioneering engineered materials for more than 130 years, Michelin is uniquely positioned to make decisive contributions to human progress and to a more sustainable world. Drawing on its deep know-how in polymer composites, Michelin is constantly innovating to manufacture high-quality tires and components for critical applications in demanding fields as varied as mobility, construction, aeronautics, low-carbon energies, and healthcare. The care placed in its products and deep customer knowledge inspire Michelin to offer the finest experiences. This spans from providing data- and AI-based connected solutions for professional fleets to recommending outstanding restaurants and hotels curated by the MICHELIN Guide. Headquartered in Clermont-Ferrand, France, Michelin is present in 175 countries and employs 122,600 people.
