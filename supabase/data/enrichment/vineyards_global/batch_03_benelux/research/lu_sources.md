# Luxembourg: official sources (researched 2026-11-25)

## Source used
**Vins & Crémants Luxembourg, "Nos vignerons"**: https://vins-cremants.lu/fr/nos-vignerons/
(paginated as /fr/nos-vignerons/all/all/0 … /4, 12 per page; producer pages at /fr/vigneron/<slug>/).
Its mentions légales (https://vins-cremants.lu/fr/politique-de-confidentialite/) name the publisher as the
**Fonds de Solidarité Viticole p.a. Institut Viti-Vinicole**, B.P. 50 L-5501 Remich, i.e. the sector's
promotion fund administered by the government's IVV. Treated as the official source. Each producer page
gives name, address, phone, website and a map pin (the pin is the source's, not the estate's, so not used).

## Sources checked and not used
- IVV (ivv.public.lu / agriculture.public.lu): no producer list found; the IVV-administered list is the one above.
- OPVI (vignerons indépendants): no public member list found online (only CEVI membership mentions). Not used.
- Domaines Vinsmoselle (domainesvinsmoselle.lu): lists its vinothèques (Remerschen, Wellenstein, Wormeldange,
  Grevenmacher, Vinocity). Used only to verify the four caves the official list names separately.

## How the 45-row cap was applied
Taken in list order; 45 rows reached at #64. **Stopped after #64 Pundel vins purs**; #69 Winery Jeff
Konsbrück (www.winery-jeffkonsbruck.lu) is the only remaining eligible entry and was not researched.

Skipped, no website on the official listing (not searched independently): #1, 4, 7, 13, 16, 17, 18, 39, 40,
42, 45, 55, 56, 60, 62, 65, 68.
Skipped, not an estate: #57 Fédération des Associations Viticoles (federation), #58 Kanecher Wéngertsbierg
(listing links to visitmoselle.lu, a tourism site), #63 Pavillon Desom (venue of Caves Desom),
#67 Wäistuff "Am Wäibuer" (wine bar of Schumacher-Lethal, same address and site).
Merged duplicates: #53 into #52 (Schumacher-Lethal, same address/site); #66 into #50 (Schlink, same address/site).
Vinsmoselle: the official list publishes four caves separately, so four rows.

## Coordinates
Only taken from a pin the estate itself publishes (a Google Maps place/address link with !3d/!4d on its own
site, or its own static-map marker). Map embeds, address-search links and the official list's pins were not
used. Two estate links were rejected because they point elsewhere (René Bentz, Le Vignoble). 10 rows have coordinates.
