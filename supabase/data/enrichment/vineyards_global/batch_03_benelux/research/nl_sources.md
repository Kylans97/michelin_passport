# Dutch BOB wine: official producer sources

Researched 2026-11-25. Only official sources count here: RVO product dossiers, Staatscourant notices (officielebekendmakingen.nl), the EU register. Press, guides and Wikipedia were not used to name producers.

## Method and caveats

- Index of current dossiers: https://www.rvo.nl/onderwerpen/landbouwproducten-verwerken-verhandelen-produceren/kwaliteitsaanduidingen/wijn (lists the 9 BOB dossiers below, plus 12 BGA dossiers).
- **The current RVO dossiers (republished 2026-08/09) black out the applicant lists** in Achterhoek-Winterswijk, Rivierenland, Twente and Vijlen, and leave them out of Voerendaalse Bergen. So producer names come from three places: figure captions and history text the redaction missed, the Staatscourant notices of the national applications (these print the applicant's name and address), and **earlier RVO versions of the same dossiers, read as Wayback Machine snapshots of the rvo.nl URL**. A Wayback copy is an archive of the official file, not a separate source. Snapshot timestamps are given so a human can reopen them.
- Staatscourant notices were found with the overheid.nl SRU search (`repository.overheid.nl/sru`, query = BOB name + "oorsprongsbenaming" + "wijn").
- **Not opened: eAmbrosia (EU register).** The EU single documents I saw republished in the Staatscourant (Mergelland 2018-10807) name no producers, and the eAmbrosia entries were not checked one by one.
- An official source naming an applicant shows who applied. It does not prove that the estate still bottles under the BOB. Two named applicants have since stopped making wine (see Twente/Achterhoek/Rivierenland below).

## Per BOB

### Achterhoek-Winterswijk
- RVO dossier (current): https://www.rvo.nl/sites/default/files/2026-09/Productdossier-BOB-Achterhoek-Winterswijk.pdf. §1.3 "Aanvragende wijngaarden" is blacked out, but the Fig. 2B caption names **Wijngaard Hesselink** and **Wijngaard Reeborghesch**.
- Staatscourant 2017-32298 (national application): https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2017-32298.html. Applicants: **Wijngaard Hesselink** (Gerhard Ensing, Vredenseweg 168, 7113 AE Winterswijk-Henxel) and **Wijngaard De Reeborghesch** (Henk Marmelstein, Sikkingweg 4, 7107 BD Winterswijk-Kotten).
- Staatscourant 2022-3477 (amendment, two grape varieties added): https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2022-3477.html. Names no producers.
- Result: Hesselink verified. Reeborghesch held_back, because the current site says winemaking stopped in 2026.

### Maasvallei Limburg (NL/BE)
- RVO dossier: https://www.rvo.nl/sites/default/files/2026-09/Productdossier-BOB-Maasvallei-Limburg.pdf, plus earlier copies (2021-01 and 2022-05 via Wayback). The applicant is the association **"Wijnbouwers Limburgse Maasvallei"** (an IVZW in Maaseik, BE, with an Aldeneyck e-mail address). No Dutch estate is named. "Thorn" appears only as a place name in the history section.
- Staatscourant 2015-15085 (application): https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2015-15085.html. Same association, no estates named. Staatscourant 2018-1156 is the registration notice.
- **No official source names a Dutch producer.** Wijngoed Thorn (already in the catalogue) states the BOB on its own site: that is estate-stated only. A Flemish government producer list (lv.vlaanderen.be) surfaced in search for the Belgian side. It is out of scope for NL and was not used.

### Mergelland
- RVO dossier (current, 2026-09): https://www.rvo.nl/sites/default/files/2026-09/Productdossier-BOB-Mergelland.pdf. Earlier copy: https://www.rvo.nl/sites/default/files/Productdossier%20Mergelland.pdf (Wayback 20180215023533). Neither names an applicant or producer. They name only the municipalities in the zone and the NVWA as control body.
- Staatscourant 2018-10807 (EU registration, Reg. 2018/215): https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2018-10807.html. No producers. No national application notice for Mergelland turned up in the SRU search.
- **No official source names producers.** Apostelhoeve and Wijngaardsberg (already in the catalogue) are estate-stated only. The earlier research's "nine applicants" came from trade press (Wijnkronieken) and is not used here.

### Oolde
- RVO dossier: https://www.rvo.nl/sites/default/files/2026-09/Productdossier-BOB-Oolde.pdf. §1.1.3 names the single vineyard **Wijngoed Gelders Laren**.
- Staatscourant 2015-46321: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2015-46321.html. Applicant: **Wijngoed Gelders Laren B.V.**, Sekmaatsteeg 1, 7245 PS Laren (GLD).
- Result: partial. The estate site looks unmaintained (footer © 2015).

### Rivierenland
- RVO dossier (Apr 2025, current): https://www.rvo.nl/sites/default/files/2026-08/Productdossier-BOB-Rivierenland.pdf. §1.5 is blacked out, but the Fig. 1-4 captions name **Betuws Wijndomein, Wijngoed Montferland, Wijnboerderij 't Heekenbroek**. The amendment https://www.rvo.nl/sites/default/files/2026-08/Gewijzigd-enig-document-BOB-Rivierenland.pdf names no producers.
- RVO dossier, Apr 2022 (https://www.rvo.nl/sites/default/files/2022-04/Productdossier-BOB-Rivierenland.pdf, Wayback 20221219183607). §1.5 lists all three in full: Betuws Wijndomein (correspondence address Burensewal 5, Erichem), Wijngoed Montferland (Munsterweg 4, Gendringen) and Wijnboerderij 't Heekenbroek (Tellingstraat 7, Drempt).
- Staatscourant 2021-3962: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-3962.html. The single document names no producers.
- Result: Betuws and Montferland verified. Heekenbroek held_back: its own site says the vines were grubbed.

### Schouwen-Duiveland
- RVO dossier (current): https://www.rvo.nl/sites/default/files/2026-09/Productdossier-BOB-Schouwen-Duiveland.pdf. Earlier copies 2021-01 and 2022-05 (Wayback) name the single applicant **Wijnhoeve De Kleine Schorre B.V.**, Zuiddijk 4, 4315 PA Dreischor.
- Staatscourant 2021-3964: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-3964.html. Names no producers.
- Only producer: De Kleine Schorre, **already in the catalogue**. No new rows.

### Twente
- RVO dossier (current): https://www.rvo.nl/sites/default/files/2026-09/Productdossier-BOB-Twente.pdf. §1.5 is blacked out.
- RVO dossier Apr 2022 (https://www.rvo.nl/sites/default/files/2022-05/Productdossier-BOB-Twente.pdf, Wayback 20230104221543). §1.5 names: **Wijngaard Hof van Twente** (Suetersweg 2A, Bentelo), **Wijngaard Bi'j de Regge, van de Wijnmakers** (Almeloseweg 202, Mariaparochie) and **Wijngaard de Tappenmars** (Leertendijk 5a, Den Ham).
- Staatscourant 2022-14239: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2022-14239.html. Names no producers.
- Related: BOB Ambt Delden, which the Twente dossier says is absorbed into Twente. Its applicant in Staatscourant 2015-18403 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2015-18403.html) is also Wijngaard Hof van Twente. The registration is Staatscourant 2019-67200.
- Result: Hof van Twente and Tappenmars verified. De Wijnmakers partial, because its current address differs from the dossier's.

### Vijlen
- RVO dossier 2015: https://www.rvo.nl/sites/default/files/2015/12/Productdossier%20BOB%20Vijlen.pdf (still live). §1.1.3 names **Wijngaard St. Martinus** (Rott 21, Vijlen) and **Wijndomein Holset** (Holset 34, 6295 NC Lemiers).
- RVO dossier (current): https://www.rvo.nl/sites/default/files/2026-09/Productdossier-BOB-Vijlen.pdf. §1.1.3 is blank, but the history section names Wijndomein St. Martinus and Domein Holset.
- Staatscourant 2015-15052: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2015-15052.html. The applicant named is Wijngaard St. Martinus (VOF, Rott 21, Vijlen) only.
- St. Martinus is **already in the catalogue**; this is the official confirmation the earlier research lacked. New row: Domein Holset (verified).

### Voerendaalse Bergen
- RVO dossier (current, Dec 2021 text): https://www.rvo.nl/sites/default/files/2026-09/Productdossier-BOB-Voerendaalse-Bergen.pdf. The applicant section is omitted.
- Earlier RVO copies: https://www.rvo.nl/sites/default/files/2021/01/Productdossier%20BOB%20De%20Voerendaalse%20Bergen.pdf (Wayback 20210430012335) and https://www.rvo.nl/sites/default/files/2022-05/Productdossier-BOB-De-Voerendaalse-Bergen.pdf (Wayback 20250717182320). §1.6 names the single applicant **Wijngoed FROMBERG V.O.F.** (Marcel Soomers, Hunsstraat 42A, 6367 JL Voerendaal). §8 calls it the "beheerder (Wijngaard Fromberg)".
- Staatscourant 2021-3965: https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2021-3965.html. Names only the sub-areas De Fromberg and De Vrakelberg, not producers. EU protection: Reg. (EU) 2023/889.
- Result: Wijngoed Fromberg verified.
