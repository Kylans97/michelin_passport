# Belgium: official producer sources per designation

Researched 2026-11-25. Rows in `be.csv` (45, the cap).

## Flanders

**Official producer list (all six Flemish designations):**
https://lv.vlaanderen.be/media/10031/download?attachment
(`WD_2026_02_10_DeelnemendeWijndomeinen.pdf`, Agentschap Landbouw & Zeevisserij,
"Vlaamse Wijnproducenten met appellatiewijnen in 2025", dated 10/02/2026). Linked
from https://lv.vlaanderen.be/landbouwbeleid/kwaliteitssystemen/beschermde-oorsprongsbenamingen-bob-en-beschermde-geografische,
which says the listed estates are not themselves recognised, but at least one of
their wines may carry an appellation. The list drops the prefixes Wijndomein,
Wijngaard, Wijngoed.

The list has two groups. The first is producers of BOB Vlaamse mousserende
kwaliteitswijn, some also producing still BOB/BGA wine. The second is producers of
still BOB/BGA wine only. Producers per designation, as the list names them:

That is 41 producers: 35 with at least one BOB, and 6 with BGA only.

**Product specifications (productdossiers):** 9155 Hageland, 9153 Haspengouw,
9150 Heuvelland, 9156 VMK, 9154 Maasvallei Limburg, 9152 Vlaamse landwijn, all at
https://lv.vlaanderen.be/media/<n>/download, plus the 2024 amendment decisions 9145,
9147 and 9148. They name only the applicant, VZW Belgische Wijnbouwers, and no
individual producers. The Maasvallei Limburg dossier is shared with the Netherlands
(RVO); its NL producers are out of scope here.

## Wallonia

**No current official producer list was found.** The arrêtés ministériels
(Côtes de Sambre et Meuse, https://wallex.wallonie.be/nl/contents/acts/2/2879.html;
Vin mousseux de qualité / Crémant de Wallonie,
https://wallex.wallonie.be/de/contents/acts/5/5739/1.html?doc=10718&rev=10093-5125)
say the Commission d'agrément keeps "la liste des producteurs agréés", but that list
is not published there. The SPW "Liste Produits de qualité" (updated 27/04/2026,
https://agriculture.wallonie.be/files/accueil/Aides/AII/outils/Liste_ProdQual.pdf)
names the four wine designations and their certifier (SPW ARNE, Direction Qualité),
but no producers. Its 2024 edition (20240523_Liste_PQ.pdf) noted that for Côtes de
Sambre et Meuse neither the operator nor any producer was yet certified.

**APAQ-W, the regional public promotion agency:**
https://www.apaqw.be/fr/labels-qualite-appellations-des-vins lists vineyards holding
an AOP or IGP **in 2016**. That list is stale, so every Walloon row is at most
`partial`.

| Designation | Producers named (2016) |
|---|---|
| AOP Côtes de Sambre et Meuse | Celiandre, Château Bon Baron, Clos Bois Marie, Coteaux de Dame Palate, Château de Bioul, Domaine du Ry d'Argent, Domaine du Chenoy, Vignoble de la Mazelle, Vin de Liège |
| AOP Crémant de Wallonie | Château Bon Baron, Domaine du Ry d'Argent, Domaine de Mellemont |
| AOP Vin mousseux de qualité de Wallonie | BGP (Baele-Granzamy-Porignaux) SPRL, Domaine du Ry d'Argent |
| IGP Vin de pays des jardins de Wallonie | Domaine de Mellemont, La Ferme Bleue |

The Vignerons de Wallonie lists of AOP/IGP wines and members
(vigneronsdewallonie.be) come from a producers' association, not an official body.
They were used only to find websites and check whether an estate is still trading.
No row relies on them as evidence.

eAmbrosia: the single documents restate the specifications. Producers are named only
through the national sources above. The EU register was not opened per designation.

## Cap and what was not finished

The cap is 45, and BOB/AOP came first. Researched: all 35 Flemish BOB producers and
the 10 Walloon AOP producers from the 2016 list, excluding BGP SPRL.

Not researched:
- BGP (Baele-Granzamy-Porignaux) SPRL, AOP Vin mousseux de qualité de Wallonie. It is
  a company name, not an estate name, so its identity is unclear. It was the 46th
  AOP producer.
- The BGA/IGP-only producers: Brothers in Wine, Domein van Krayenberg, Jerom Winery,
  Mérula, Mikken Urban (BGA Vlaamse landwijn) and La Ferme Bleue (IGP Vin de pays
  des jardins de Wallonie).

The Vlaamse landwijn and jardins de Wallonie designations are therefore unfinished.
