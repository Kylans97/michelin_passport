## Data-quality correction applied downstream (p2_006)

The original World's 50 Best 2025 extended-list source (`worlds_50_best_hotels_raw.csv:156`, cited there as "Official primary source") lists "Four Seasons Tamarindo, La Manzanilla, **Costa Rica**." Direct verification against the hotel's own website (fourseasons.com/tamarindo) confirms this property is actually on "the Costalegre coastline...Jalisco...south of Puerto Vallarta" — i.e. La Manzanilla, Jalisco, **Mexico**. This is very likely an editorial error in the original World's 50 Best source itself (conflating the Four Seasons Tamarindo brand name with the unrelated Costa Rican beach town also called Tamarindo, where Four Seasons has no property — its actual Costa Rica hotel is the separate "Peninsula Papagayo" resort).

**Correction applied:** p2_006's country is corrected from Costa Rica to Mexico in `new_hotels_for_deployment.csv` and all downstream counts. This removes Costa Rica from the new-country list entirely (it had no other candidate) and moves this hotel into Mexico's count (7 → 8). See `phase8_production_readiness_report.md` §7 for the corrected arithmetic.
