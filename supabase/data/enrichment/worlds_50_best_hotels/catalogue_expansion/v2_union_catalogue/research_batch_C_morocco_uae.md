## Oman & Sweden country-level MICHELIN Key coverage — RESOLVED

Both countries are **confirmed covered** by the MICHELIN Key programme, from the 8-9 October 2025 global launch:
- **Oman**: 6 MICHELIN Key hotels confirmed (1 Two-Key property — Alila Jabal Akhdar — and several One-Key properties). Source: guide.michelin.com's own global Key article; michelinkeyhotels.com/hotels/in/oman.
- **Sweden**: confirmed as part of the Nordic countries' first-ever Key selection (Denmark, Finland, Iceland, Norway, Sweden), announced as part of the 8 October 2025 global launch. Source: guide.michelin.com Nordic-countries Key article; Grand Hôtel Stockholm's own press release confirming it as Sweden's only Two-Key hotel.

Both p2_044 (Six Senses Zighy Bay, Oman) and p2_050 (Ett Hem, Sweden) individually confirmed with 1 Key each.

**This resolves the "Oman and Sweden — unresolved" item carried since the earlier catalogue-expansion report.** Both countries now have confirmed MICHELIN Key programmes; both candidate hotels in this batch hold a Key.
