| Award category | Confirmed markets | Annual award or ongoing label? |
|---|---|---|
| Chef of the Year | France, Belgium, Switzerland, Croatia, Netherlands, Luxembourg, Hungary, Turkey | Annual, one winner/market |

| Restaurant of the Year | UAE, Turkey | Annual |
| **POP (casual/trend guide)** | Switzerland (gaultmillau.ch/pop), Croatia | **Ongoing category/label, NOT an annual award** — a parallel, continuously-updated guide for lifestyle/casual venues that don't necessarily score in the main guide |
| Vegetable/vegetarian recognition | France, Belgium | **No confirmed dedicated award** — editorial curation/features only, not a formal category |
| Culinary Innovators Awards | Belgium (2022) | Possibly annual/occasional — not investigated in depth, flagged for follow-up |

**Key takeaway:** award category names are **not standardized globally** — each market sets its own slate and terminology. A data model should treat award category as an open, market-scoped vocabulary, not a fixed global enum. Chef of the Year and some form of young-talent recognition are the closest things to cross-market universals.

## Part C — Historical / annual edition mechanics

- **Annual cadence confirmed, but per-market timing varies** — no single global "G&M day." France: gala 17 Nov 2025 → guide in shops 20 Nov 2025 for the "2026" edition. Belgium/Luxembourg (Belux): launched 3 Nov 2025 for 2026. Japan: 2026 edition released 17 Mar 2026. Netherlands: 2026 reporting compares to 2025 (908 vs 865).
- **No visible historical score archive found anywhere.** Directly checked a live restaurant profile — [The Jane, Antwerp](https://www.gaultmillau.be/en/restaurants/the-jane-antwerpen) — shows current toque rating (4) plus named award badges tied to specific past years ("Sommelier of the Year 2018"), but **no chart/table of the restaurant's own past scores across editions**. No dedicated public archive site found on gaultmillau.org/.com either — the France "our history" page narrates brand milestones only, not a queryable data archive.
- **Implication for Chasing Stars:** historical snapshots are not retrievable as a built-in feature from the live sites today. They would need to be assembled going forward (recording each annual edition at its launch) or reconstructed from press coverage (aggregate only, not per-restaurant) or possibly the Wayback Machine (not checked this pass).
- **Caveat:** only one restaurant, one market (Belgium) was directly checked for history — not exhaustive across all 20 markets.

## Key uncertainties flagged
