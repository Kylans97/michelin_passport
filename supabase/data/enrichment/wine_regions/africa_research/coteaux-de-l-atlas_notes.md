# Les Coteaux de l'Atlas (AOC) — point only (commune shapes held back)

## Official text
- Arrêté du ministre de l'agriculture, du développement rural et des pêches maritimes n° 1957-98 du 8 octobre 1998 (BO n° 4662, 4 Feb 1999, p. 104). ONSSA PDF: https://www.onssa.gov.ma/wp-content/uploads/Reglementation/R%C3%A9glementation%20Sectorielle/Produits%20Alimentaires/Boissons%20Alcoolis%C3%A9es/Vins/ARR.1957-98.FR.pdf (raw/onssa/ARR.1957-98.FR.pdf); WIPO Lex MA011.
- Art. 1: red, rosé, gris and white wines from the area inside the AOG Béni M'Tir bounded west by route principale 21 (Meknès-El Hajeb), south by its intersection with the Oued Defali, east by the Oued Defali, north by the Fès-Meknès motorway. "Partie de commune de Sidi-Slimane; Partie de communes de Mjat; Partie de commune de Boufekrane." Limits are drawn on cadastral plans held by the fraud-control service (not published).
- Arrêté 1955-98 (general AOC conditions) Art. 1 requires every AOC arrêté to list "communes ou parties de communes" — so all three are partial by text.
- Art. 2 grapes: reds/rosés/gris — Cabernet-Sauvignon, Merlot, Syrah, Tempranillo ("minimum 2/3 dans l'assemblage"); Carignan, Cinsault, Grenache (the text also says "minimum 1/3"; read literally that is a floor, likely meant as a cap — reported as printed). Whites — Chardonnay, Sauvignon blanc (minimum 50%), Vermentino; Ugni blanc and Clairette (maximum 50%).
- Art. 3-6: reds ≥12.5% alc., rosés/gris ≥12%, whites ≥11.5%; ≤60 hl/ha; ≥3,000 vines/ha; irrigation to 30 June (gobelet) / 31 July (trellis).
- Crémant de l'Atlas (arrêté 352-09, 12 Feb 2009, BO 5732 p. 745): sparkling AOC produced inside the same Coteaux de l'Atlas area.

## Units — held back
The text lists three communes, all partial. The only openly downloadable commune boundaries are GADM 4.1 level 4 (no official HCP commune layer is published: the HDX COD-AB from HCP, downloaded 2026-11-20, stops at admin 2, 10 regions/69 provinces). GADM's polygons failed a position check: GeoNames' Boufakrane, Majjate and Sidi Slimane Moul Al Kifane points each fall in a *different* GADM commune, and GADM's El Hajeb, Sebaa Aiyoun, Benslimane and Boufakrane polygons sit ~3-5 km north of the towns they are named after. Under the never-guess rule the shapes are not used. The rejected candidate is kept for review in work/HELD_BACK_gadm_coteaux_communes.geojson (union 125 km², ~0.6% GADM simplification change). Even if correct, drawing three whole communes would overstate an AOC the text bounds by a road, a river and a motorway.

## Point
33.83781, -5.44462 — GeoNames 6546145 "Sidi Sliman Mol Kifa" (A.ADM3), one of the three listed communes. Not verified as inside the AOC's cadastral limits (not published). Stands for the AOC, not an estate.

## Importance
- Morocco's first AOC (official date 8 Oct 1998; some secondary sources say 2001 — they are wrong against the BO).
- Château Roslane (Celliers de Meknès) labels as "Les Coteaux de l'Atlas" and "Premier Cru": Jancis Robinson tastings https://www.jancisrobinson.com/tastings/227969 and /tastings/166846; Wine-Searcher listing "Chateau Roslane Premier Cru Blanc, Coteaux de l'Atlas".

## Style
- Summary (draft; official text + Jancis Robinson + Wine-Searcher): "Morocco's first AOC, a small area of the Meknès plateau east of the El Hajeb road. Reds are Bordeaux- and Rhône-variety blends led by Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot and Syrah; whites from Chardonnay and Sauvignon."
- Main grapes in order: from the official text (legal fact, one official text suffices): Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot, Syrah, Tempranillo (≥2/3 of the red blend), then Carignan, Cinsault, Grenache. Producer-specific blends not verified from two sources.
- Typical red body/acidity/tannin: held back (no accessible review text).
- Pairing: held back.
- Label: 869-75 Art. 6 — "Les Coteaux de l'Atlas" with "appellation d'origine contrôlée" in very apparent characters under it. "1er cru" is allowed only for an AOC wine from a terroir classified by the Commission nationale viti-vinicole (Art. 6, prohibited mentions 4°) — the basis for "Premier Cru" on Château Roslane labels. Château Roslane's own "château" right rests on arrêté 815-04 (14 Oct 2004).
