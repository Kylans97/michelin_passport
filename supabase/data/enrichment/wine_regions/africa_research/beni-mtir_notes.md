# Beni M'Tir (AOG) — point only

## Official text
- Arrêté 869-75 (15 Aug 1977, BO 3388 p. 1091), Art. 1 7°: "Beni-M'Tir : pour les vignobles du plateau de Meknès situés à l'est de la route de Meknès à El-Hajeb." Source as for Guerrouane (ONSSA PDF, raw/onssa/ARR.869-75.FR.pdf). Grapes and rules: Art. 2 as completed by arrêté 1956-98 (same list as Guerrouane).
- Three AOCs lie inside it: Les Coteaux de l'Atlas (1957-98), Crémant de l'Atlas (352-09, same area as Coteaux de l'Atlas), Côtes de Sebaa Ayoun (3477-22, 16 Dec 2022, BO 7166 p. 296: part of the commune Sebaa Ayoun, between RN6 Meknès-Fès (west), RP7048 (south), RP7040 (east), Oued Bou Gnaou (north)).

## Units
None. Defined only by a road ("east of the Meknès-El Hajeb road"); it has no eastern or southern limit in the text. Point only.

## Point
33.90313, -5.37814 — GeoNames 2537654 "Sebaa Aioun" (P.PPL). Why: arrêté 3477-22 places Sebaa Ayoun commune inside the AOG Béni M'Tir; the town is east of the Meknès-El Hajeb road. Note: GeoNames also has a tribal-area point "Beni Mtir" (2555703, 33.63333, -5.15) far to the south-east near Ifrane; not used, since it marks the tribe's territory, not the Meknès-plateau vineyards the text describes.

## Importance
- Wine-Searcher (snippets): Meknès/Fès group; Celliers de Meknès "Beauvallon" and rosé listed as Beni M'Tir; Château Roslane "at the heart of" the producer's Beni M'Tir vineyards.
- Jancis Robinson: Celliers de Meknès, ~30 million bottles a year from ~2,500 ha owned plus ~1,400 ha bought in (https://www.jancisrobinson.com/articles/from-priorat-to-morocco, via search snippet; 403 to direct fetch).

## Style
- Summary: same evidence as Guerrouane (shared Meknès-plateau description); no source separates Beni M'Tir from Guerrouane in style. Draft: "Meknès-plateau vineyards east of the El Hajeb road, at around 600-700 m below the Middle Atlas; home to Morocco's AOCs and mostly red and rosé wines." (Jancis Robinson; Wine-Searcher.)
- Grapes, structure, pairing: held back (no region-specific source).
- Label: as Guerrouane ("Beni M'Tir" + "appellation d'origine garantie").
