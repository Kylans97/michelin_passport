# Zenata (AOG) — point only

## Official text
- Arrêté 869-75 (15 Aug 1977, BO 3388 p. 1091), Art. 1 12°: "Zenata : pour les vignobles de Tit-Mellil, Oulad-Ziane, El Gara, El-Louizia, El Bahir, Sidi-Larbi, Fedalate, Ben-Slimane, Bessabès, Ben-Nabet, Oulad-Taleb, Beni-Amar et Mansouriah." ONSSA PDF as for Guerrouane.
- Grapes/rules: Art. 2 as completed by 1956-98 (same list as Guerrouane).

## Units — held back
The text names 1977 localities ("vineyards of ..."), not administrative units. Several share names with today's communes (Tit Mellil, El Gara, Benslimane, Fdalate, El Mansouria), but matching a 1977 locality list to 2009/2015 communes by name alone is exactly the inference the project forbids, and no reliable commune boundaries exist (see Coteaux de l'Atlas notes). Point only.

## Point
33.61601, -7.12153 — GeoNames 2555519 "Benslimane" (P.PPL). Why: Ben-Slimane is a locality the text names, and both wine references below place Zenata wine there. Stands for the AOG.

## Importance
- Domaine des Ouled Thaleb (Thalvin), Morocco's oldest operating winery near Benslimane, labels Syrocco and Tandem Syrah under Zenata: Wine Spectator, James Molesworth, 10 Aug 2009, "Domaine des Ouled Thaleb Syrah Zenata Syrocco 2007" (https://www.winespectator.com/articles/a-ripe-red-from-a-surprising-region-40456); Decanter review, Christelle Guibert, Thalvin Tandem Syrah 2011, labelled "Benslimane, Casablanca" (https://www.decanter.com/wine-reviews/morocco/benslimane/thalvin-tandem-syrah-benslimane-casablanca-morocco-2011-2575/); Wine-Searcher listings "Syrocco Syrah ... Zenata".
- Conflict logged: one search snippet attributed a French producer note saying Syrocco comes from "Meknès hillsides at 500 m". Not resolved; the label appellation (Zenata) is used.
