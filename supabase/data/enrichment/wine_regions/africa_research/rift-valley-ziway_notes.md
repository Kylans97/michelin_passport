# Ethiopian Rift Valley (Ziway/Batu) — point only

## Official status
No wine appellation or wine GI exists in Ethiopia. Until 2026 GIs had no specific legislation (only trademark rules, Proclamation 501/2006; Ethiopia's coffee names were protected as trademarks). Capital Ethiopia reported on 19 Apr 2026 that a first GI legal framework had been finalised, with registration "to begin soon" (https://capitalethiopia.com/2026/04/19/ethiopia-finalizes-first-legal-framework-for-geographical-indications/). No wine GI found. "Rift Valley" is a brand of Castel Winery, not an appellation.

## Producer and vineyard
- Castel Winery (BGI Ethiopia, Castel Group). Vineyard near Ziway, also called Batu, East Shewa, Oromia, on Lake Ziway in the Central Rift Valley.
  - Ziway, ~160 km south of Addis: Ethiopian Embassy to Belgium, "The wine sector in Ethiopia", 12 May 2015 (https://ethiopianembassy.be/?p=6802); "163 km south": How We Made It In Africa, 13 Dec 2016 (https://www.howwemadeitinafrica.com/p/ethiopia-s-emerging-wine-industry-luring-consumers-locally-and-abroad).
  - Batu = Ziway: Wikivoyage "Ziway" ("also called Zway or Batu"); Wikipedia "BGI Ethiopia PLC" (vineyard in "Batu (Oromia)"); a 2025 BGI job advert names the site "Castel Winery – Batu".
  - Still operating: BGI Ethiopia post on X, Feb 2025 (Q4 2024 distributor event at Castel Winery); Birr Metrics Dec 2025 calls it the country's largest wine producer (claim conflicts with Awash's market share — see below; not used).
- Size: 120 ha (Embassy 2015), 162 ha (How We Made It 2016): conflict, reported as 120-162 ha.
- Altitude ~1,600 m; ~650 mm rain; average ~25 °C; sandy soils: Embassy 2015 only. FurtherAfrica (Aug 2025) gives 1,600-2,000 m for Ethiopian vineyards generally. Treat altitude as one-source for this site.
- Grapes: Syrah, Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot, Chardonnay — How We Made It 2016 and Embassy 2015 (two sources). Order by area from Embassy 2015 only: Syrah 55 ha, Cabernet Sauvignon 38 ha, Merlot 14 ha, Chardonnay 12 ha (plus 42 ha Sangiovese "planted in the 1980s by the government", attribution to Castel unclear).
- First bottle January 2014 (How We Made It 2016).

## Distance and drive time from Addis Ababa
- Road: ~160-163 km (two sources above); Wikivoyage: 162 km via Modjo and the Addis-Adama Expressway, "2 hours 30 minutes"; 180 km / ~3 h via Butajira. The Modjo-Batu expressway opened in 2021 (Addis Fortune; 2merkato).
- Straight line Addis Ababa (GeoNames 344979) to Ziway (GeoNames 325579): 121 km (computed).
- Not ~1 hour: about 2.5 h by road.

## Point
7.93333, 38.71667 — GeoNames 325579 "Ziway" (P.PPL, pop. 90,500), GeoNames ET dump downloaded 2026-11-20 (raw/geonames/ET.txt). Stands for Castel's vineyard area by Ziway/Batu (the vineyard is "near" the town; its exact location is not sourced).
