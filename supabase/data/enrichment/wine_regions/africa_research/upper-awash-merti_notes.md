# Upper Awash (Merti/Jeju, Arsi) — point only

## Official status
No appellation (see rift-valley-ziway_notes.md).

## Producer and vineyard
- Awash Wine S.C., Ethiopia's oldest winery (wineries at Lideta and Mekanisa in Addis Ababa). Founding year conflicts on its own site (1936 and 1956); Wikipedia gives 1956. Privatised 2013 (8 Miles LLP with Blue Nile Investments); 8 Miles sold 89% to an investor consortium in 2024 (Africa Private Equity News; Kenyan Wall Street, via snippets). Active in Dec 2025 (Capital Ethiopia, agreed acquisition of Komari Beverage).
- Vineyard: "Merti Jeju" farm, 517 ha with 225 ha of vineyard (awashwines.com/history, producer). Location: "Merti wereda, Arsi zone, Oromia Regional State" (The Reporter, Kaleyesus Bekele, 31 Aug 2019, https://www.thereporterethiopia.com/8531/; 517 ha). Two sources agree on Merti (Jeju) and 517 ha.
- Market: ~80% of the local wine market (Addis Fortune, 9 Feb 2019).
- Grapes: no source names Awash's varieties: held back. Altitude: Embassy 2015 says "a plateau rising to 1,200 m" for Awash's estate without naming the place: one source, held back.

## Distance and drive time from Addis Ababa
- The Reporter 2019: "about 180 km east of Addis Ababa". The Drinks Business 2014 (snippet; 403 to fetch): vineyard at "Awash Merti Jersu, only 115 km southeast of Addis Ababa". Conflict — not resolved; the 115 km may be a straight-line figure.
- Straight line Addis Ababa to Abomsa (Merti woreda seat): 131 km (computed from GeoNames).
- Drive time: no source: held back. At 115-180 km it is not ~1 hour.

## Point
8.58333, 39.85 — GeoNames 345703 "Ābomsa" (P.PPL, Oromia), administrative seat of Merti woreda (Wikipedia "Merti" and "Abomsa"). Stands for Merti woreda, not the farm, whose exact position is not sourced. Trap: GeoNames has a second Abomsa (345704) in Amhara, and a "Mertī" (330876) in western Oromia at 9.5, 36.98 — both wrong for this; and "Merti Jeju" may straddle the neighbouring Jeju woreda (seat Arboye), so the farm could sit west of Abomsa.

## Style
All held back: no variety, altitude or tasting source for Awash's vineyard in two sources. Awash's brands (Gouder dry red, Awash dry white, Axumit semi-sweet red, Kemila semi-sweet white) come only from the producer.
