# Setúbal (DO, PDO-PT-A1457): source notes

**Boundary source:** eAmbrosia caderno de especificações, §4 (citing Portaria 793/2009): https://webgate.ec.europa.eu/eambrosia-api/api/v1/attachments/16888. Area: the municipalities of Montijo, Palmela and Setúbal, and the freguesia of Castelo in Sesimbra.

## Counts
- Montijo 5, Palmela 4, Setúbal 5 units, plus "Sesimbra (Castelo)" PT_151101. 15 rows, 15 distinct IDs. Partials: none. Held back: none.
- Montijo is included whole, as the text names the whole municipality.
- The DO Setúbal is for the fortified Moscatel de Setúbal and Moscatel Roxo only. Palmela (table wines, PDO-PT-A1460) overlaps it and is not covered here.

## Style sources
Official: spec (grapes, 67% and 85% rules, Moscatel Roxo less known). Independent: Wines of Portugal Península de Setúbal page, The Wine Society Portugal guide, Wine Folly Portugal by region. Red ratings null (fortified Moscatel).
