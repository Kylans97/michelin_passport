# trentodoc

## Official text
- Trento DOC (header: last listed change Provvedimento Ministeriale 12.07.2019). Art. 3: "particelle fondiarie, di sicura vocazione viticola, ubicate, in provincia di Trento, nei comuni amministrativi di" 74 names.

## Counts
74 printed names, 63 rows, 4 partial; by province: Trento 63.
- 74 printed names give 63 LAU units, because many have since merged (Altavalle, Castel Ivano, Cembra Lisignago, Madruzzo, Vallelaghi, Terre d'Adige and others). Partial: Altavalle (Grauno not listed), Altopiano della Vigolana (Bosentino, Centa San Nicolò, Vattaro not listed), Comano Terme (Lomaso not listed), San Lorenzo Dorsino (San Lorenzo in Banale not listed). The text restricts the zone to suitable vineyard parcels inside the listed comuni; that wording is not marked as partial on every row, the same reading used for other whole comuni lists. Region name Trentodoc is the collective brand; the official denomination is "Trento".

Method: comune names taken verbatim from the disciplinare in the MASAF archive (https://www.masaf.gov.it/flex/cm/pages/ServeBLOB.php/L/IT/IDPagina/4625, archives downloaded 2026-11-20, text extracted to `raw/txt/`). Each name resolved within its named province only with `it_tools/istat.py` (ISTAT current list, suppressed comuni and variations since 1991), then mapped to GISCO LAU 2024 by ISTAT code (`IT_` + 6 digits). Build: `../../it_tools/build_round5.py`; style assembly: `../../it_tools/style_round5.py`. A merged comune is marked partial when it also absorbed (since 1991) a comune the text does not list.

## Style sourcing
- Summary, ratings and pairing: see `sources` in style.json. Sparkling, so red ratings null. Pairing held back: Cellar Tours suggests speck and alpine cheeses, Opening a Bottle only says the acidity suits a wide range of food; no shared dish. Upper altitude given as about 800 m (disciplinare and trentodoc.com); Cellar Tours says 900 m, so no lower or upper figure beyond 'about 800' used. Porphyry and volcanic soils (Cembra) mentioned by two sources but left out for length. 'Best sparkling wines in the world' (consorzio) not used.
- Label note, grapes and appellations: from the disciplinare (Art. 1, 2, 5, 7), cited as the MASAF archive page.
