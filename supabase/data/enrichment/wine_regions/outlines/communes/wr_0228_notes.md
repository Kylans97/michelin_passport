# Paros (GR): boundary notes

**Official texts:** PDO Paros, PDO-GR-A1570, https://ec.europa.eu/geographical-indications-register/eambrosia-public-api/api/v1/attachments/11101 ("όλα τα δημοτικά διαμερίσματα της νήσου Πάρου και της νήσου Αντίπαρου", PD 93/2011); PDO Malvasia Paros, PDO-GR-A1607, https://ec.europa.eu/geographical-indications-register/eambrosia-public-api/api/v1/attachments/11567 ("τις νήσους Πάρος και Αντίπαρος", PD 93/2011). The two areas coincide.

**Resolution:** all GISCO LAU units of the municipalities of Paros (EL_6801xxxx, 7) and Antiparos (EL_68020000). **Rows:** 8; partial 0. Small islets belonging to these municipalities come with them.

**Style:** summary from Wines of Greece (pdo-paros, pdo-malvasia-paros), The Wine Society and Wine Traveler (Mandilaria co-vinified with the white Monemvasia on Paros). Mandilaria's 35% minimum (Wines of Greece only; spec table did not extract) is not stated. label_note from the Malvasia Paros spec (24 months oxidative ageing in oak; optional age in steps of four years).

**Near-miss:** the Paros spec PDF text lists "Monemvasia B, Mandilaria N" only; no percentages extracted, so the 100% Monemvasia white claim is not repeated.
