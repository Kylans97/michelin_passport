## Matching notes
- Felsberg (Schwalm-Eder-Kreis, northern Hessen) and Frankfurt am Main are listed by the text but lie far from the Rhine; each draws as a detached exclave, and the Frankfurt polygon is a whole city. Kept because the text lists them; flagged for a display decision.
- The text says "Landeshauptstadt Wiesbaden"; mapped to the single LAU "Wiesbaden, Landeshauptstadt".
- Every match was made by name inside the Kreise the region lies in (Kreis codes checked against each Kreis seat in the GISCO LAU 2024 index), and each name resolved to exactly one LAU.
