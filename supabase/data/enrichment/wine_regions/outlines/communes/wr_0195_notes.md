## Official text

Weingesetz 2009 § 21 Abs. 3 Z 1 lit. i: „Wien: das Bundesland Wien“. DAC-Verordnung „Wiener Gemischter Satz“ (BGBl. II Nr. 236/2013 idF BGBl. II Nr. 250/2024) § 1 Z 2: grapes from vineyards 100% within the Weinbaugebiet Wien; § 2 Abs. 2 lists seven Gemeindeteile (by Katastralgemeinden) for smaller origins on the label.

Source URL: https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/Dokumente/Bundesnormen/NOR40254626/NOR40254626.html

Municipality IDs: GISCO LAU 2024 (AT_ + Gemeindekennziffer), checked against Statistik Austria Gemeindeliste 2026 and politische Bezirke (alps_hr/at/raw/). For every whole Bezirk, the LAU 2024 and Gemeindeliste 2026 code sets were asserted identical.

## Judgement calls

- Drawn as the whole city: Wien is one Gemeinde and one LAU (AT_90001). The DAC does not restrict the area; its Gemeindeteile only govern smaller label names and are listed as appellations, not drawn.
