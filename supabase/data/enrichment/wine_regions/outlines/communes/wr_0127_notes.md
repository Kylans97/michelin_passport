# Hagelandse wijn (BOB/PDO, Belgium, Flanders)

- Flemish: "Het geconsolideerde enig document (update juli 2024)", Departement Landbouw en Visserij. https://lv.vlaanderen.be/media/9155/download (raw/lv_9155.pdf). Approval decision: https://lv.vlaanderen.be/media/9145/download

## Units: 24 LAU (GISCO 2024), about 997 km2
- 23 named municipalities matched by name within their province: 22 in Vlaams-Brabant, plus Halen, which is in the province of Limburg (matched there, BE_71020).
- Budingen, Dormaal, Helen-Bos are former municipalities of Zoutleeuw (OJ C/2024/1490 amendment 2: "Zoutleeuw currently consists of five formerly independent municipalities: Budingen, Dormaal, Halle-Booienhoven, Helen-Bos and Ossenweg ... Zoutleeuw has been deleted from the Hagelandse wijn PDO and replaced by Budingen, Dormaal and Helen-Bos"). All three go to BE_24130 Zoutleeuw, marked partial.
- Wording conflict, recorded not resolved: the Flemish consolidated text (July 2024) instead reads "... Tremelo en Zoutleeuw (met uitzondering van de deelgemeenten Booienhoven en Ossenweg)" and also lists Budingen, Dormaal, Helen-Bos. Both versions give the same LAU result: Zoutleeuw partial, its Halle-Booienhoven and Ossenweg parts belong to Haspengouwse wijn.
- The Flemish government listing of Zoutleeuw's deelgemeenten is "Zoutleeuw, Budingen, Dormaal, Halle-Booienhoven, Helen-Bos" (https://www.vlaanderen.be/organisaties/stad-zoutleeuw); it does not name Ossenweg. The OJ texts call Ossenweg a former municipality of Zoutleeuw.
- Bertem and Oud-Heverlee were added by the 2023 amendment (same OJ communication).
- No 2025 Flemish merger touches these municipalities as far as checked; LAU 2024 predates the 1 January 2025 mergers in any case.
