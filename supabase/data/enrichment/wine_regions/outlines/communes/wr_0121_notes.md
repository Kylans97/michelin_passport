# Somló (HU) — boundary notes

**Official text:** A Somló oltalom alatt álló eredetmegjelölés termékleírása, 2. változat (grapes harvested after 1 August 2024), section IV. https://boraszat.kormany.hu/download/a/d5/43000/SOML%C3%93%20term%C3%A9kle%C3%ADr%C3%A1s%20(2%20v%C3%A1ltozat)%20kn.pdf (raw/hu_somlo_v2.pdf/.txt), linked from https://boraszat.kormany.hu/somlo.

**Wording:** "Borgáta, Borszörcsök, Celldömölk Doba, Kemeneskápolna, Kissomlyó, Mesteri Somlójenő, Somlószőlős, Somlóvásárhely településeknek a szőlő termőhelyi kataszter szerint I. és II. osztályú területei".

**Units:** 10 settlements → 10 LAU (Vas: Borgáta, Celldömölk, Kemeneskápolna, Kissomlyó, Mesteri; Veszprém: Borszörcsök, Doba, Somlójenő, Somlószőlős, Somlóvásárhely).

**Judgement calls:**
- The text has two missing commas ("Celldömölk Doba", "Mesteri Somlójenő"). Read as four settlements: Celldömölk, Doba, Mesteri, Somlójenő — each is a separate KSH settlement and there is no settlement called "Celldömölk Doba" or "Mesteri Somlójenő"; the spec's own section VII names the three witness hills Somló, Ság-hegy (Celldömölk area) and Kis-Somlyó, which fits.
- v2 widened the Somló PDO: bor.hu (https://bor.hu/en/somloi-wine-district/) says the district merged Somló and Nagy-Somló into one Somló PDO in 2024 and dropped the Nagy-Somló name. eAmbrosia still lists both Somlói/Somló (EUGI00000005799) and Nagy-Somló (EUGI00000005798) with 2018 data; the national v2 text is used.
- Class I/II restriction treated as the general formula; `partial` empty. Celldömölk is a sizeable town whose LAU includes much non-vineyard land; it is named in the text, so it is kept.

**Held back:** none.

**Style notes:** grape order: Juhfark first (bor.hu: Somló its home, about 20% of the district; Wine Folly), Furmint next (bor.hu: Furmint is most widespread in Tokaj "followed by Somló"); Hárslevelű and Olaszrizling in the spec's Superior order, not ranked by area (no area figures found).
