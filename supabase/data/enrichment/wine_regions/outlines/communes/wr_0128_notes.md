# Haspengouwse wijn (BOB/PDO, Belgium, Flanders)

## Defining text
- EU: Communication of approval of a standard amendment, OJ C, C/2024/1485, 16.2.2024, single document point 6. http://data.europa.eu/eli/C/2024/1485/oj (raw/oj_1485.pdf)
- Flemish product specification: https://lv.vlaanderen.be/media/9153/download (raw/lv_9153.pdf). It does not yet show the 2023 addition below.
- eAmbrosia EUGI00000003723 (PDO-BE-A1492).

EU text, verbatim: "comprising the towns and/or municipalities of Alken, Bilzen, Borgloon, Gingelom, Halle-Booienhoven, Heers, Herk-de-Stad, Herstappe, Hoeselt, Kortessem, Nieuwerkerken, Ossenweg, Riemst, Sint-Truiden, Tongeren and Wellen."

## Units: 14 LAU, about 676 km2, all in the province of Limburg, all whole
Alken, Bilzen, Borgloon, Gingelom, Heers, Herk-de-Stad, Herstappe, Hoeselt, Kortessem, Nieuwerkerken (LAU "Nieuwerkerken (Hasselt)"), Riemst, Sint-Truiden, Tongeren, Wellen.

## Left out
- Halle-Booienhoven and Ossenweg: "two formerly independent municipalities of Zoutleeuw" (OJ C/2024/1485, amendment 1), added because of similar subsoils. They are two of Zoutleeuw's five former municipalities and Zoutleeuw (Vlaams-Brabant) is drawn in Hageland, so they are not drawn here. Their area share of Zoutleeuw was not measured.

## Mergers
- LAU 2024 still has the pre-merger units. On 1 January 2025 Bilzen and Hoeselt, Borgloon and Tongeren (Tongeren-Borgloon), and Hasselt and Kortessem merged (https://www.vlaanderen.be/lokaal-bestuur/overzicht-fusies/borgloon-en-tongeren ; https://www.vlaanderen.be/statistiek-vlaanderen/nieuwe-nis-codes-voor-gefuseerde-gemeenten-vanaf-1-januari-2025). Only Kortessem of the new Hasselt is in this PDO, so a later LAU vintage would make Hasselt a partial.

## Relation to Maasvallei Limburg (already in the app as a point)
- The Belgian side of BOB Maasvallei Limburg is strips of Kinrooi, Maaseik, Dilsen-Stokkem, Maasmechelen and Lanaken east of the N78 and the Zuid-Willemsvaart (lv_9154 annex 4). None of these municipalities is in Haspengouwse wijn; Lanaken borders Riemst and Bilzen, so the two areas touch but do not overlap. Not redrawn.

## Overlaps
- Vlaamse landwijn and Vlaamse mousserende kwaliteitswijn zones include all 14 ("Zuid-Limburgse Fruitstreek").
