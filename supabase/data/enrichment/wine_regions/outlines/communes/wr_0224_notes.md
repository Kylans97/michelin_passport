# Mantinia (GR): boundary notes

**Official text:** technical file PDO-GR-A1554 (EUGI00000007282), https://ec.europa.eu/geographical-indications-register/eambrosia-public-api/api/v1/attachments/11314, section 5. RD 625/1971 as amended (latest PD 92/2011), written in Kallikratis units: the Δημοτική Κοινότητα Τρίπολης and named Τοπικές Κοινότητες of the ΔΕ Τρίπολης, Τεγέας, Μαντινείας, Κορυθίου and Λεβιδίου, plus the settlement Κούβλι of the ΤΚ Δολιανών (Δήμος Βόρειας Κυνουρίας), "excluding marshy land".

**Resolution:** every named community resolves to a unique ELSTAT 2021 / GISCO unit within the stated ΔΕ (codes in tools/build_gr.py). Κούβλι is settlement 4002000704 of EL_40020007 Δολιανών, marked partial.

**Judgement calls:** marsh exclusion not drawn and not marked partial (Amyndeon precedent). Wines of Greece's own list of communities matches the spec.

**Rows:** 27; partial 1.

**Style:** summary from Wines of Greece (pdo-mantinia), Decanter's Moschofilero grape page (Arcadian plateau, 650 m, cool, harvest in early October), Wine Folly map page (near Tripoli), Karakasis MW (public "at a glance"). The Wine Society says Moschofilero is the "single permitted variety"; the spec allows up to 15% Asproudes, so that phrase was not used. Alias "Mantineia" from Wine Folly and Karakasis. Appellation colour given as white (still and sparkling white only).
