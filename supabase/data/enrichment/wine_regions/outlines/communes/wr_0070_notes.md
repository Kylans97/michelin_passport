# Neuchâtel (AOC Neuchâtel)

## Official text
- Arrêté concernant l'appellation d'origine contrôlée des vins de Neuchâtel, RSN 916.120.1, of
  20.06.2016 (Feuille officielle 2016 n° 25). It repeals the arrêté of 15.09.2010.
- Art. 4 al. 3: "Les limites de l'AOC Neuchâtel sont définies par les limites politiques du canton."
- Retrieved via the Wayback Machine because ne.ch now returns 410 for the original link:
  https://web.archive.org/web/2020/https://www.ne.ch/legislation-jurisprudence/pubfo/ArrRegCE/Documents/2016/FO25_04_ACE_DDTE_A_Vins_NE_AOC.pdf
  (`raw/ne_FO25_04_2016_AOC_wayback.pdf`).
- Later amendments are known from search results only: 19.08.2020 and 18.08.2021 (annex 3, maximum
  yields) and 12.05.2021 (tasting commission). According to their titles, none of them touches
  art. 4. The consolidated RSN version could not be downloaded: every rsn.ne.ch PDF URL tried
  returned 404. This was not verified further.

## How the area is defined
Whole canton. All 27 communes (BFS 31.12.2023); `partial` empty.

## Counts
27 rows, all names identical to LAU. Excluded: 9150 Bielersee (NE), 9155 Lac de Neuchâtel (NE).

## Judgement calls
- Grape order (Pinot Noir, Chasselas, Pinot Gris, Chardonnay) is the Swiss Wine Promotion
  Three Lakes table, which also covers Lac de Bienne (BE) and Vully. Neuchâtel is 606 of its
  944 ha. The federal statistics confirm Chasselas as Neuchâtel's main white (≈138 ha of ≈220 ha
  white). The Pinot Noir column for NE could not be read without ambiguity, and no Neuchâtel-only
  table was found (the cantonal reports on ne.ch did not download).
- The commune and locality names in art. 4 al. 2 are optional label mentions on plans held by the
  agriculture service. They are not sub-appellations, so they are not drawn.

## Held back
- The claim that Œil-de-Perdrix rosé originated in Neuchâtel. Only Swiss Wine Promotion says so;
  the other sources were producer pages. The 2016 arrêté does not define Œil-de-Perdrix.
- Red ratings and a pairing (no two sources).
