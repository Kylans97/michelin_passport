# Friuli Colli Orientali (IT): boundary notes

**Official text:** Single document after the standard amendment PDO-IT-A0953-AM02, Official Journal C/2026/988, 11.2.2026, section 9 (http://data.europa.eu/eli/C/2026/988/oj; raw/oj_c2026_988_it.txt): the zone "comprende in tutto o in parte il territorio comunale di" 19 comuni in the province of Udine. The disciplinare (MASAF, classics/it/raw/txt/Friuli Colli Orientali.txt) Art. 3 defines the zone only by a boundary line, and its Art. 8 gives the same 19 comuni "in tutto o in parte".

**Units:** 19 GISCO LAU 2024 (IT_030xxx), each name an exact LAU match in the province of Udine. All rows partial: the text does not say which comuni are wholly inside, and several (Tarcento, Nimis, Cividale, Faedis, Prepotto) include mountain or plain land outside the line. The drawing therefore overstates the zone.

**Not found:** no DOC geometry from Regione FVG (IRDAT) or any other official geodata was found.

**Unresolved:** Art. 3 runs "sopra gli abitati di Fragielis e Stregna"; Stregna is also a comune (IT_030111) but is not among the 19, so it is not drawn.

Style: grapes Verduzzo and Refosco dal Peduncolo Rosso held back (taste sources too thin); Picolit and Schioppettino added to new_grapes.json. See work/notes_hu_gr_it.md.
