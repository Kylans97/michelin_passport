# Lisboa (PT): boundary notes

**What is drawn, and why:** the IG (PGI) Lisboa, not its DOs. Every DO inside it (Bucelas, Alenquer, Arruda, Torres Vedras, Óbidos, Encostas d'Aire, Carcavelos) is defined in its caderno partly by *parts* of pre-2013 freguesias and by named lugares (e.g. Bucelas: "parte das freguesias de Fanhões (lugares de ...) e de Santo Antão do Tojal (lugares de ...)"; Carcavelos: lugares of Alcabideche and Oeiras, plus a line description). They cannot be drawn without tracing, so the honest unit is the IG. The DOs stay as appellations in style.json.

**Official text:** IG Lisboa caderno, sec. 4 (Portaria 426/2009 art. 2), eAmbrosia attachment 22309 (https://ec.europa.eu/geographical-indications-register/eambrosia-public-api/api/v1/attachments/22309; same id on webgate), restated unchanged in the 2024 single document (OJ C/2024/1215, sec. 6): the district of Lisboa except Azambuja; in Leiria district the concelhos of Alcobaça, Batalha, Bombarral, Caldas da Rainha, Leiria, Marinha Grande, Nazaré, Óbidos, Peniche, Pombal (except the freguesias of Abiul, Pelariga, Redinha and Vila Cã) and Porto de Mós; in Santarém district, Ourém.

**Units:** GISCO LAU 2024 freguesias (PT_ + DICOFRE), selected by concelho code; concelho codes and names from DGT's CAOP concelho layer (scratchpad/classics/pt/raw/conc_cont.json). 15 Lisboa concelhos, 11 Leiria concelhos, Ourém. The four excluded Pombal freguesias exist unchanged in LAU 2024 (PT_101501 Abiul, PT_101508 Pelariga, PT_101510 Redinha, PT_101514 Vila Cã) and are left out; 9 Pombal units remain. Script: tools/pt_build.py.

**Rows:** 227; partial 0. Whole concelhos are named, so the 2013 freguesia unions do not matter here.

**For review:** the area includes the city of Lisbon and the whole existing Colares region (Sintra), which will overlap the app's Colares outline. "Estremadura" as an alias is also a current official sub-region name of this IG (style notes). Lourinhã (wineries call it a DOC) has no wine PDO in eAmbrosia; only a spirits GI, so it is not an appellation here.
