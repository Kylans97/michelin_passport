# Santorini (GR) — boundary notes

**Official texts:** EU single document of the amendment to PDO Santorini, OJ C 143, 31.3.2022, item 6: "The demarcated production area for PDO wines covers the islands of Santorini and Thirasia." https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=uriserv%3AOJ.C_.2022.143.01.0012.01.ENG (raw/eu_santorini_2022_143.txt). Technical file on eAmbrosia (PDO-GR-A1065, attachment 10656; raw/gr_santorini_ts.pdf/.txt): zone set by Royal Decree 539/4.8.1971 (ΦΕΚ 159/A/14.8.1971), amended by Ministerial Decisions 13850/1572/11.2.1972 and 235308/7.2.2002, "περιλαμβάνει τα νησιά Θήρα και Θηρασία". The ministry copy on minagric.gr refused automated access (Akamai "Access Denied").

**GISCO LAU (Greece):** CNTR_CODE "EL", GISCO_ID "EL_" + 8 digits = RR MM UU CC: regional unit (2), municipality within it (2), municipal unit (2, "00" where the municipality has none), local or municipal community (2). Every LAU name is "Δημοτική Κοινότητα <genitive>" (68 "Ψευδοδημοτική"), i.e. LAU does not distinguish Τοπική from Δημοτική Κοινότητα. Regional unit of a code group was identified by its seat community (e.g. EL_60010101 Θήρας).

**Units:** the islands = Δήμος Θήρας (EL_6001…), 14 communities in two municipal units: Θήρα (Θήρας, Ακρωτηρίου, Βόθωνος, Βουρβούλου, Εμπορείου, Έξω Γωνιάς, Επισκοπής Γωνιάς, Ημεροβιγλίου, Καρτεράδου, Μεγαλοχωρίου, Μεσαριάς, Πύργου Καλλίστης) and Οία (Οίας, Θηρασίας). All whole.

**Judgement calls:** the text names islands, not communities; every community of the Thira municipality lies on Santorini or Thirasia (Thirasia has its own community), and no community of another municipality does, so the 14 together equal the text. Uninhabited islets (Kameni) are within these LAU and not separately excluded.

**Held back:** none.

**Style notes:** Vinsanto and Nykteri are traditional terms of the PDO, not separate appellations. Grape: Assyrtiko ≥85% (dry), ≥51% (sweet) per OJ 2022 and winesofgreece.org. Assyrtiko taste "stone fruit" rests on The Wine Society only.
