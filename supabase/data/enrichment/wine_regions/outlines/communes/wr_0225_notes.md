# Goumenissa (GR): boundary notes

**Official text:** technical file PDO-GR-A1251 (EUGI00000007109), https://ec.europa.eu/geographical-indications-register/eambrosia-public-api/api/v1/attachments/11560, section 5. Ministerial Decision 344530/7792/1979 as amended by PD 13/1981: the communities Γουμένισσα, Φυλλυριάς, Στάθης, Γοργόπης, Κάρπης, Γρίβας, Πεντάλοφου, Πολύπετρου, Γερακώνας (Kilkis), excluding land inside irrigation networks and land below 150 m.

**Resolution (ELSTAT 2021 / GISCO):** Γουμένισσας EL_09020301, Φιλυρίας EL_09020307 (text spells it Φυλλυριάς), Στάθη EL_09020306, Γοργόπης EL_09020202, Κάρπης EL_09020303, Γρίβας EL_09020302, Πενταλόφου EL_09020305, Πολυπέτρου EL_09020404. Γερακών is today a settlement (0902030702) of the Φιλυρία community, which is listed whole, so it adds no unit.

**Judgement calls:** the irrigation and altitude exclusions are not drawn and not marked partial (same rule as Amyndeon). Wines of Greece lists the same places.

**Rows:** 8; partial 0.

**Style:** summary from Wines of Greece (pdo-goumenissa and Negoska pages) and GuildSomm. red_body 4: GuildSomm "more fruity and full-bodied", spec taste "γεμάτο" (full). The 20% Negoska minimum is from Wines of Greece and GuildSomm; the spec PDF's percentage table did not extract. Tannin not rated (Negoska: soft per GuildSomm and The Wine Society, medium per Wines of Greece).
