# greco-di-tufo

## Official text
- Greco di Tufo DOCG (header: last amended D.M. 13.10.2020). Art. 3: "l'intero territorio amministrativo dei seguenti comuni della provincia di Avellino", eight names.

## Counts
8 printed names, 8 rows, 0 partial; by province: Avellino 8.
- All whole.

Method: comune names taken verbatim from the disciplinare in the MASAF archive (https://www.masaf.gov.it/flex/cm/pages/ServeBLOB.php/L/IT/IDPagina/4625, archives downloaded 2026-11-20, text extracted to `raw/txt/`). Each name resolved within its named province only with `it_tools/istat.py` (ISTAT current list, suppressed comuni and variations since 1991), then mapped to GISCO LAU 2024 by ISTAT code (`IT_` + 6 digits). Build: `../../it_tools/build_round5.py`; style assembly: `../../it_tools/style_round5.py`. A merged comune is marked partial when it also absorbed (since 1991) a comune the text does not list.

## Style sourcing
- Summary, ratings and pairing: see `sources` in style.json. White, so red ratings null. Sulphur in the soil held back: contested (World of Fine Wine cites a geologists' study finding none in the root zone). Body held back: Wine Traveler says full, Wine Enthusiast (via search, 403) medium to full. Pairing: Wine Traveler (seafood) and Irish Times (shellfish and seafood, said of the region's whites, with a crab salad for a Greco).
- Label note, grapes and appellations: from the disciplinare (Art. 1, 2, 5, 7), cited as the MASAF archive page.
