# thermenregion: municipality mapping notes

Prepared 2026-11-21. Source of truth for the boundary: the official text below.

## Official text

Weingesetz 2009 § 21 Abs. 3 Z 1 lit. a: „die Stadt Wiener Neustadt sowie die politischen Bezirke Baden, Mödling, Neunkirchen und Wiener Neustadt“. DAC-Verordnung „Thermenregion“ § 2 Z 1 (BGBl. II Nr. 191/2023 idF 250/2024): DAC area equals the Weinbaugebiet.

Source URL: https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/Dokumente/Bundesnormen/NOR40254626/NOR40254626.html

Municipality IDs: GISCO LAU 2024 (AT_ + Gemeindekennziffer), checked against Statistik Austria Gemeindeliste 2026 and politische Bezirke (alps_hr/at/raw/). For every whole Bezirk, the LAU 2024 and Gemeindeliste 2026 code sets were asserted identical.

## Judgement calls

- Stadt Wiener Neustadt (AT_30401) plus all Gemeinden of Bezirke 306, 317, 318 and 323: 130 rows. Includes mountain Gemeinden (Semmering area in Neunkirchen) with no vineyards, as the law names whole Bezirke.

## Held back / left out

- Style: Neuburger (the main white grape per the specification), Zierfandler and Rotgipfler left out of the grape list: not in the app, and no second source was readable for a grape profile (only Wikipedia; Decanter paywalled, VinoVoss pages empty). They are named in the summary and DAC note.
- Style: grape order follows the specification (Zweigelt named the main red); wine-searcher names Portugieser and Pinot Noir as the most important reds. No area figures by grape were found.
