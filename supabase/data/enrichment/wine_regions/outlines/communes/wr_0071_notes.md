# Bündner Rheintal (official Weinbauregion, alternative drawing for Graubünden)

- Text: Kantonale Weinverordnung Graubünden BR 917.400, art. 2 al. 1 let. a (version in force since
  01.07.2019): "Bündner Rheintal mit den Gemeinden Fläsch, Maienfeld, Jenins, Malans, Landquart,
  Zizers, Trimmis, Chur, Felsberg, Domat/Ems, Bonaduz".
  https://www.gr-lex.gr.ch/app/de/texts_of_law/917.400
- 11 rows. BFS numbers are from the 31.12.2023 snapshot, and every name matches BFS and LAU
  exactly. No mergers needed.
- Status: an administrative wine region (yields and control, art. 4), **not** an appellation. The
  appellation is AOC Graubünden (whole canton, `graubuenden.csv`). This file is offered only as a
  vineyard-focused alternative drawing for the same style entry (`graubuenden` in style.json).
- The narrower "Bündner Herrschaft" (Fläsch, Maienfeld, Jenins, Malans) has no legal definition
  and is held back. See `graubuenden_notes.md`.
