# Madeira (DOP "Madeira" / "Vinho da Madeira", PDO-PT-A0038): source notes

**Boundary source:** eAmbrosia caderno de especificações (IVBAM, 16.3.2012), §4: https://webgate.ec.europa.eu/eambrosia-api/api/v1/attachments/10001. The area is the Região Demarcada da Madeira, "abrangendo as ilhas da Madeira e do Porto Santo", with a table of all 54 freguesias of the 11 municipalities.

## Counts
- 11 whole municipalities (10 on Madeira, DICOFRE 31xx; Porto Santo, 3201): 54 LAU units, matching the 54 freguesias in the spec's table. 54 rows, 54 distinct IDs. Partials: none. Held back: none.
- The text names only the islands of Madeira and Porto Santo. Whether any LAU polygon of these municipalities also includes the uninhabited Desertas or Selvagens was not checked.
- Madeirense (PDO-PT-A0039, table wines) shares the area but is a separate DO and is not included here.

## Style sources
Official: spec §2 (sweetness bands Seco/Sercial, Meio-seco/Verdelho, Meio-doce/Boal, Doce/Malvasia; "elevada frescura, conferida pela acidez típica"), §6 grapes, §8 labelling. Independent: Wines of Portugal Madeira page, Wine Folly Portugal by region.
- All red ratings null: fortified wine; Tinta Negra is not rated by sources as a table red.
- Grapes: Tinta Negra (over 80% of the harvest per Wines of Portugal), Sercial, Verdelho. Boal (Malvasia Fina) and Malvasia are not in the app and were not added.
- Pairing: not sourced, null.
