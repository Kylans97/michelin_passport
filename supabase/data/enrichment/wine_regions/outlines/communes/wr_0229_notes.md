# Monemvasia (GR): boundary notes

**Official text:** PDO Monemvasia - Malvasia, PDO-GR-A1533 (EUGI00000007243), https://ec.europa.eu/geographical-indications-register/eambrosia-public-api/api/v1/attachments/11311, section 5: "την κτηματική περιφέρεια των πρώην Δήμων Μονεμβασίας, Ασωπού, Βοιών και Μολάων του νομού Λακωνίας" (PD 179/2009).

**Resolution:** the four former Kapodistrias municipalities are today's municipal units (ΔΕ) of the same names in the Δήμος Μονεμβασίας: ΔΕ Μονεμβασίας 430505 (10 units), Ασωπού 430502 (4), Βοιών 430503 (13), Μολάων 430501 (6). The Δήμος also has ΔΕ Γερονθρών and Ζάρακα, which are not drawn.

**Judgement call:** "πρώην Δήμων" read as the Kapodistrias municipalities that became these ΔΕ under Kallikratis (the technical file dates from 2011, after Kallikratis). Their borders were not compared with the 2009 borders unit by unit.

**Rows:** 33; partial 0.

**Style:** summary from Wines of Greece (pdo-monemvassia-malvasia, July 2010) and The Wine Society (PDO created 2010, Laconia). label_note from the spec (both names together; age statements 4, 8, 12...). Asproudes and Kydonitsa named in the appellation note only; they are not app grapes and were not researched.
