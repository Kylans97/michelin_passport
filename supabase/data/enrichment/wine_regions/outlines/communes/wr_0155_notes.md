# Amyndeon (GR): boundary notes

**Official text:** technical file PDO-GR-A1395 (eAmbrosia EUGI00000007106, https://webgate.ec.europa.eu/eambrosia-api/api/v1/attachments/11299; europe2/hu_si_gr/raw/gr_amyndeo_ts.txt), area set by Royal Decree 183/1972 as amended in 1995 and 1996: the Δήμος Αμυνταίου and the communities of Αγίου Παντελεήμονα, Βεγόρας, Λακκιάς, Κλειδιού, Αντίγονου, Μανιακίου, Πετρών, Ξυνού Νερού, Φανού, Πεδινού, Αετού, Ροδώνα, Αγραπιδιών, Αναργύρων and Βαρυκού, Florina, "excluding marshy land".

**Units:** 16 GISCO LAU 2024 local communities, all in Dimos Amyntaio (EL_1702xxxx).

**The two that did not resolve before, now resolved from the official EETAA register of administrative changes:**
- Λακκιά: renamed Λεβαία by ΦΕΚ 226Α/10.10.1988 (EETAA settlement record https://www.eetaa.gr/eetaa/metaboles/oikmet_details.php?id=16754, raw/eetaa_16754.html; same chain on Pandektis). Mapped to EL_17020604 Δημοτική Κοινότητα Λεβαίας.
- Αντίγονος: renamed from Κιοσελέρ by ΦΕΚ 193Α/1928; the EETAA record says the settlement was detached in 1997 "από την κοινότητα Αντιγονείας" (https://www.eetaa.gr/eetaa/metaboles/oikmet_details.php?id=16722, raw/eetaa/oik_16722.html). Mapped to EL_17020602 Δημοτική Κοινότητα Αντιγονείας.

**Judgement calls:** "Δήμος Αμυνταίου" in a 1972 text is the old town municipality, mapped to the town's community EL_17020101, not today's much larger Dimos. Genitive and spelling variants: Βεγόρας to Βεγόρων, Αγραπιδιών to Αγραπιδεών, Αγίου Παντελεήμονα to Αγίου Παντελεήμονος. The marsh exclusion is not drawn; no row marked partial for it. Today's communities may hold settlements added after 1972; not checked settlement by settlement.

**Rows:** 16; partial 0.
