## Matching notes
- Qualifiers "(Landkreis Alzey-Worms)", "(Landkreis Mainz-Bingen)" and "(Landkreis Bad Kreuznach)" were used to pick the Kreis for Bermersheim, Bornheim, Bubenheim, Freimersheim, Harxheim, Hillesheim, Sankt Johann, Tiefenthal and Wachenheim.
- Ingelheim am Rhein includes Heidesheim and Wackernheim, merged into it in 2019; the text already lists them under Ingelheim. Bad Kreuznach (Nahe) likewise lists Bad Münster a. Stein-Ebernburg, merged in 2014.
- Every match was made by name inside the Kreise the region lies in (Kreis codes checked against each Kreis seat in the GISCO LAU 2024 index), and each name resolved to exactly one LAU.
