# Querétaro (Mexico): drawn, 1 unit (whole state)

## Official basis
- Mexico has no wine Denominación de Origen. Its first wine indicación geográfica is "Vinos de la Región Vitivinícola de Querétaro": IMPI "Declaración de Protección", oficio DG.2025.045 of 26 Feb 2025, signed 3 Mar 2025 by the IMPI Director General, published in the DOF (https://dof.gob.mx/nota_detalle_popup.php?codigo=5751385; SIDOF 5751385). EU IP Helpdesk (13 Mar 2025) reports Mexico's first wine GI; Mexico News Daily (25 May 2025) names it as Querétaro's IG of March 2025.
- Zone: the declaration defines it as the state of Querétaro ("el estado de Querétaro ubicado entre el paralelo 20° y 21° latitud norte"); it names producing municipios (Tequisquiapan, El Marqués, Ezequiel Montes, San Juan del Río, Colón, Huimilpan, Cadereyta de Montes, Pedro Escobedo) and suitable ones (Querétaro, Corregidora, Amealco de Bonfil, Tolimán) but does not restrict the zone to them. So the whole state is drawn. The accompanying "Reglas" (25 Feb 2025) were not found or read.

## Unit
- MX_ent_22 Querétaro: INEGI Marco Geoestadístico, diciembre de 2025, área geoestadística estatal 22, from INEGI's catalogue service https://gaia.inegi.org.mx/wscatgeo/v2/geo/mgee/22 (EPSG:6365, ITRF2008 geographic, treated as WGS84), saved raw/inegi_mgee_22.json. INEGI Términos de Libre Uso (free use with attribution; not re-read this session).
- Checks: area 11,723 km² (sphere; INEGI publishes about 11,699 km²); Querétaro city and Tequisquiapan inside, San Miguel de Allende (Guanajuato) outside. Simplified 100 m: area change 0.01%.

## Style (sources)
- Summary: high plateau, vineyards about 1,900 m or more (DOF: c. 1,965 m average; Mexico News Daily: 1,900 to 2,300 m; queretaro.travel: 1,950 m); sparkling (DOF: "el primer productor de vino espumoso de calidad del país"; Mexico News Daily: "premier producer of sparkling"; queretaro.travel); first wine GI 2025 (DOF; EU IP Helpdesk).
- Grapes named by both Mexico News Daily and queretaro.travel (state tourism site, a different body from IMPI): Macabeo, Xarel·lo, Chardonnay, Sauvignon Blanc, Merlot, Syrah. No planting figures; order not a ranking. Parellada (MND only), Malbec, Marselan, Tempranillo, Cabernet (travel site only) left out. Marselan is not in the app anyway.
- Label note: DOF (Vitis vinifera only; "está prohibido el uso de edulcorantes y/o saborizantes durante cualquier fase del proceso"; the declaration allows chaptalization only in exceptional weather, not mentioned in the note).
- Red structure: no source; null. Pairing: MND mentions local cheeses as wine's traditional partner, not a sourced wine pairing; null.
