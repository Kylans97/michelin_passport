URL: http://data.europa.eu/eli/C/2026/898/oj (retrieved 2026-11-21).

**Wording:** "die Rebflächen der Gemeinden und deren Ortsteile", with Gemarkung numbers; parcel boundary in the BLE maps. A union of whole Gemeinde polygons therefore overstates the planted area.

## Matching notes
- Qualifiers in the text were used to pick the Kreis: "(Südliche Weinstraße)" for Bornheim, Eschbach, Frankweiler, Oberhausen, Rohrbach; "(Donnersbergkreis)" for Bubenheim; "(Germersheim)" for Winden.
- Two typos in the text were repaired before parsing: "Birkweiler Birkweiler (5393)" and "Colgenstein- Heidesheim".
- "3 Mittelhainger"/"4 Mittelhainger" entries are shares of the jointly owned Mittelhaingeraide forest attached to Edenkoben, Edesheim, Rhodt unter Rietburg and Weyher; they do not change the Gemeinde mapping.
- Every match was made by name inside the Kreise the region lies in (Kreis codes checked against each Kreis seat in the GISCO LAU 2024 index), and each name resolved to exactly one LAU.
