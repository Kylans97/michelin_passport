## Part 1: area
> "Schouwen-Duiveland is een eiland in de provincie Zeeland gelegen aan de Noordzee, tussen de zeearmen De Grevelingen en Oosterschelde."
> "Er is een duidelijke afbakening van het gebied, het eiland Schouwen-Duiveland."
- Soil (§2.1.3): "Op Schouwen-Duiveland zijn de gebieden met gronden met een classificatie die begint met “M” … van toepassing voor de wijngaarden van deze Beschermde Oorsprongsbenaming" (sea clay/zavel, often calcareous).

### Units used
- LAU NL_GM1676 Schouwen-Duiveland, `partial=no`.
- **Judgement call:** the dossier defines the island, not the gemeente. I treat the gemeente as the island, since the municipality is coterminous with the island; this is general knowledge, not stated in the dossier. The municipality polygon includes large water areas (CBS 2024: 22,860 ha land of 48,821 ha total, the rest being parts of the Grevelingen and Oosterschelde, depending on how the LAU geometry draws water). Clip to land, or use the CBS land-water boundary, if the outline should hug the island.
- The "M" soils are a vineyard condition, not a boundary.

## Part 2: content
- Grapes (Tabel 1, white only): Auxerrois, Cabernet Blanc, Gewurztraminer, Pinot Blanc, Pinot Gris, Rivaner, Souvignier Gris.
- Categories: 1 Wijn, 5 Mousserende kwaliteitswijn.
- Types: white dry fruity; white medium-dry/medium-sweet fruity; white sweet fruity; white dry full (wood-aged, at least 6 months for at least 50% of the volume); sparkling white full-fruited (traditional method, more than 12 months on the lees).
- Max yield: 75 hl/ha for all white varieties.
- Soil, climate and style (dossier):
  - Calcareous sea clay and zavel with shell lime. The vineyard (De Kleine Schorre, Dreischor) lies in the former Gouwe stream bed, on Mn35A calcareous polder soil.
  - Maritime climate: more sun hours (about +0.5 h/day versus the NL average), less rain, few spring frosts or hail, salty sea wind.
  - Huglin index 1746 (2018) and 1572 (2019, to end of September).
  - Style: "uitgesproken frisheid en mineraliteit"; notes from peach and pear to gooseberry, saltiness, lime, honey and anise.
- Copy-paste errors in the dossier: §2.5 says "Binnen het afgebakende geografisch gebied Rivierenland" and §3.1 says "(Rivierenland)". These are leftovers from the template; not used.
