# Nahe: source notes

**Source:** Single document published with the approved standard amendment PDO-DE-A1271-AM02, Official Journal C, C/2026/1289, 2.3.2026, section 9 "Kurzbeschreibung der Abgrenzung des geografischen Gebiets". This is the latest approved amendment in eAmbrosia.
URL: http://data.europa.eu/eli/C/2026/1289/oj (retrieved 2026-11-21).

**Wording:** "folgende Gemeinden und deren Ortsteile", with Gemarkung numbers; the exact parcel boundary is in the BLE maps. A union of whole Gemeinde polygons therefore overstates the planted area.

## Counts
- Entries listed in the text: 91
- Rows written: 90, distinct GISCO_IDs: 90
- Rows marked partial: 3

## Partials
- Guldental: text lists only "Heddesheim, Waldhilbersheim"
- Offenbach-Hundheim: text lists only "Offenbach"
- Waldalgesheim: text lists only "Genheim"
- Not marked: entries whose listed Ortsteile include the Gemeinde's own name or every part of a double name (Bad Kreuznach (Bad Kreuznach, Bad Münster a. Stein, Bosenheim, Ebernburg, Ippesheim, Planig, Winzenheim); Bad Sobernheim (Sobernheim); Dielkirchen (Dielkirchen, Steingruben); Mannweiler-Cölln (Cölln, Mannweiler); Warmsroth (Wald-Erbach, Warmsroth)). The text lists them by Ortsteil; whether every Ortsteil is listed was not checked against a register.
- Guldental, a composite-name Gemeinde, is marked because no listed part carries the Gemeinde name; the listed parts may be the whole Gemeinde (not verified).

## Left out
- Bingen am Rhein: Only Ortsteil Bingerbrück is listed for Nahe; the rest of Bingen am Rhein (Bingen, Büdesheim, Dietersheim, Dromersheim, Gaulsheim, Kempten, Sponsheim) belongs to Rheinhessen, so the Gemeinde is assigned to Rheinhessen only.

## Held back
- None.

## Matching notes
- The four Gemeinden written "... bei Bad Kreuznach" (Boos, Roth, Schöneberg, Weinsheim) were matched to the LAU of that base name inside Kreis Bad Kreuznach (07133) only; each name also exists in another Kreis.
- Every match was made by name inside the Kreise the region lies in (Kreis codes checked against each Kreis seat in the GISCO LAU 2024 index), and each name resolved to exactly one LAU.
