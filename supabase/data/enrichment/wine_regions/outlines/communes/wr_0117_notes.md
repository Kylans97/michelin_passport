# Maldonado (Uruguay): drawn, 1 unit

## Official basis
- INAVI regions page (https://www.inavi.com.uy/regiones/): OCEÁNICA "se extiende por los departamentos de Maldonado y Rocha"; Maldonado "es una región emergente en la producción vitivinícola del país". Drawn as the departamento (INAVI's own unit), not the Oceánica region (Rocha has 19 ha of vines, INAVI 2023).
- IG rules as in canelones_notes.md (Decreto 283/993 Arts 15–17). The 2019 MIEM/DNPI bulletin lists Garzón and José Ignacio among wine IGs recognised before INAVI; both are in Maldonado (Decanter coast guide; SevenFifty). Limits unpublished, so not drawn.
- Bodega Garzón, the app's World's 50 Best Vineyards estate, is in Maldonado (Decanter: "by far the Maldonado region's largest"; SevenFifty: "The biggest producer in Maldonado").

## Style (sources)
- Summary: emerging (INAVI; SevenFifty "up-and-coming"; Decanter "newest"); Atlantic coast, Punta del Este (INAVI; Decanter); cooler (SevenFifty "cooler coastal region"; INAVI oceanic climate "temperaturas menores a las continentales"); poor rocky soils, ancient granite (SevenFifty "poorer, rocky soils", "granite soils"; SevenFifty region "poor and granitic"; INAVI crystalline basement 2,500 million years; Decanter soils "Granite, sand, clay and limestone"; Wine Folly "ancient crystalline rocks"); Tannat leads reds (INAVI 2023 predominant-variety map: Tannat 107.8 ha is Maldonado's most planted variety; Merlot 51.17 ha); Albariño leads whites (INAVI 2023 text: Albariño is the white wine variety with the largest area in Maldonado).
- Grapes: Tannat (107.84 ha), Merlot (51.17 ha) from INAVI; Albariño's Maldonado area is not published, so its place relative to Merlot is not established. Decanter lists red Tannat, Syrah, Merlot, Cabernet Sauvignon; white Sauvignon Blanc, Albariño (no areas).
