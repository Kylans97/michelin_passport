# Vipavska dolina (SI) — boundary notes

**Official text:** Specifikacija proizvoda "Vipavska dolina" ali "Vipava" (MKGP, file dated 16.7.2019), "Opredelitev geografskega območja". https://www.gov.si/assets/ministrstva/MKGP/DOKUMENTI/HRANA/VINO/ZOP/S_vipavska-dolina.doc (raw/si_vipava.doc/.txt). eAmbrosia EUGI00000005861 / PDO-SI-A0448 (technical file raw/si_vipava_ts.pdf). Supporting: PISRS Priloga 1 §7 (raw/si_pisrs_351458730.pdf), lists the vinorodni kraji of the two subdistricts.

**The district is defined by a boundary line** (from the Razdrto–Nova Gorica road via contour lines under Nanos and Čaven, the Soča, the state border, the Vipava river, Mirenski grad, Vrtoče, the hills to Dornberk, Branik, Štanjel, the Branica, Štjak, Veliko Polje and Otošče back to the road). Full wording in raw/si_vipava.txt line 36. No unit list exists in the official text.

**Units:** 6 LAU: SI_001 Ajdovščina (partial), SI_136 Vipava (partial), SI_084 Nova Gorica (partial), SI_201 Renče-Vogrsko (partial), SI_183 Šempeter-Vrtojba (whole), SI_075 Miren-Kostanjevica (partial).

**Method:** every settlement named inside the district (Priloga 1 vinorodni kraji, plus "zajame naselje Vrtoče" and "zajame naselje Otošče" in the line) was placed in its municipality with the SURS settlement list (SiStat 05C5006S): Vipava: Gradišče pri Vipavi, Erzelj, Slap, Podraga, Lože, Goče; Ajdovščina: Gaberje, Črniče, Batuje, Selo, Brje, Zavino, Šmarje, Vrtovče, Velike Žablje, Vipavski Križ, Ustje, Planina; Nova Gorica: Branik, Gradišče nad Prvačino, Prvačina, Šempas, Vitovlje, Kromberk, Dornberk, Zalošče; Renče-Vogrsko: Vogrsko; Miren-Kostanjevica: Bilje (Biljenski griči), Vrtoče.

**Judgement calls:**
- Šempeter-Vrtojba is not named, but lies wholly inside the stretch where the line follows the Soča, the state border and the Vipava river; included whole. This is a geographic judgement, not a text match.
- Miren-Kostanjevica: only the Vipava valley side (Bilje, Vrtoče, up to Mirenski grad) is inside; its Karst plateau part belongs to the Kras district. Kept as partial because the named settlements are the municipality's main valley settlements.
- Left out as small parts: Komen (Štanjel, Trebižani: line points; part of the Branica valley), Sežana (Štjak, Veliko Polje: line points), Divača (Otošče is "zajame naselje Otošče", one village; SURS puts Otošče in Divača), Postojna (Razdrto: line start). Kanal not touched.
- Ajdovščina, Vipava and Nova Gorica include large plateau areas (Trnovo, Banjšice, Nanos) above the contour lines; partial.

**Held back:** Komen, Sežana, Divača, Postojna (small parts only, see above).

**Style notes:** red fields null (the spec and vipavskadolina.si describe a mainly white district; no red profile found in two sources). Grapes: Rebula "the foremost white" and Merlot "the predominant variety" for reds (vipavskadolina.si; GuildSomm "Merlot is one of the most popular grapes"); Malvazija and Sauvignon from vipavskadolina.si and the spec's Vipavec/Vrtovčan blends. Zelen and Pinela appear in the summary (GuildSomm, Decanter, vipavskadolina.si) but are not added as grapes: two independent descriptions of their taste were not found (wine-searcher blocks automated reads). The name "burja" is avoided: it appears in Decanter only in a search snippet that could not be read in full.
