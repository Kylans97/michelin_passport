# Heuvellandse wijn (BOB/PDO, Belgium, Flanders)

## Defining text
- EU: OJ C, C/2024/1487, 16.2.2024, single document point 7. http://data.europa.eu/eli/C/2024/1487/oj (raw/oj_1487.pdf)
- Flemish consolidated single document (update July 2024): https://lv.vlaanderen.be/media/9150/download (raw/lv_9150.pdf)
- eAmbrosia EUGI00000003743 (PDO-BE-A1426).

EU text, verbatim: "comprising the municipalities of De Klijte, Dranouter, Kemmel, Loker, Nieuwkerke, Reningelst, Westouter, Wijtschate and Wulvergem. Parcels located in neighbouring municipalities, but whose rows of vines begin within a municipality forming part of the area covered by the designation, are also considered to be part of that area."

## Units: 1 LAU, BE_33039 Heuvelland (West-Vlaanderen), whole, about 95 km2
- The eight names other than Reningelst are the former municipalities that make up Heuvelland. Dutch Wikipedia lists exactly these eight, with areas summing to the municipality's 94.79 km2 (https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heuvelland_(gemeente)), and notes De Klijte belonged to Reningelst before the 1977 mergers. The Flemish government page lists seven, omitting De Klijte (https://www.vlaanderen.be/organisaties/gemeente-heuvelland). With all eight, Heuvelland is whole.

## Left out / conflict
- Reningelst: named in the EU text (2024) but not in the Flemish consolidated text (July 2024). Reningelst is a former municipality now in Poperinge (BE_33021), a minority part of it. Left out as a small part either way; the conflict between the two texts is recorded here, not resolved.
- The row-start rule for parcels in neighbouring municipalities is not drawable.

## Overlaps
- Vlaamse landwijn and Vlaamse mousserende kwaliteitswijn zones include Heuvelland ("Westhoek").
