# Rapsani (GR): boundary notes

**Official text:** technical file PDO-GR-A0116 (EUGI00000003566), https://ec.europa.eu/geographical-indications-register/eambrosia-public-api/api/v1/attachments/10367, section 5; text in raw/ea/att_10367.txt. Area set by RD 539/1971 as amended by PD 45/2001: the Δημοτικά Διαμερίσματα Ραψάνης and Πυργετού of the Δήμος Κάτω Ολύμπου and the Κοινοτικό Διαμέρισμα Αμπελακίων of the Κοινότητα Αμπελακίων, Larisa.

**Resolution:** ELSTAT 2021 settlement file (work/elstat_mon2021.tsv, copied from round5), whose codes are the GISCO ids. The three Kapodistrias units map one to one to today's communities of the same name: EL_22050404 Ραψάνης, EL_22050401 Πυργετού (ΔΕ Κάτω Ολύμπου) and EL_22050201 Αμπελακίων (ΔΕ Αμπελακίων). The former Κοινότητα Αμπελακίων also held the Κ.Δ. Τεμπών (today EL_22050202); the text names only Αμπελακίων, so Τέμπη is not drawn.

**Rows:** 3; partial 0. **Not checked:** whether the settlements Άγιος Κωνσταντίνος and Περαταριά in today's Ραψάνη community were in the 2001 ΔΔ Ραψάνης (assumed by name continuity, not verified).

**Style:** summary from Wines of Greece (pdo-rapsani), The Wine Society Greek guide, Wine Folly map page and GuildSomm (Kelli White, public article). Red body/acidity/tannin left null: tannin conflicts (spec says soft tannins for the blend; Wine Society "ripe yet structured", Wine Folly "firm"), and only The Wine Society rates acidity. label_note from the spec. Krasato and Stavroto are not in the grape list because they were held back as grapes (see held_back_notes.md).
