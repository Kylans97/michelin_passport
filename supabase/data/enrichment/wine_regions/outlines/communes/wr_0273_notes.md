# osaka: GI Osaka (wine), JP_PREF_27

## Official designation
- GI 大阪 (Osaka), wine, designated 30 June 2021; area 大阪府, the whole prefecture (NTA lists, as Yamagata). Specification https://www.nta.go.jp/english/taxes/liquor_administration/02_15.htm (`raw/nta_02_15.htm`); control body: GI "Osaka" Management Committee, c/o Katashimo Wine Foods.

## Boundary
- Same dataset, licence and processing as Yamagata, file `N03-20260101_27_GML.zip`. 157 features (72 municipal units, no unassigned land features). Source 1,905.6 km² in 66 parts; kept 4 parts (mainland plus large reclaimed islands including Kansai Airport); dropped 62 parts totalling 12.69 km² (small reclaimed islands and rocks in Osaka Bay). Output 1,894.0 km², 944 vertices, valid, Hausdorff 0.001°.

## Style evidence
- Summary: Kashiwara and Habikino in Minami Kawachi (NTA spec; Osaka Convention and Tourism Bureau; Wine Anorak on Kawachi Wine and Katashimo Winery); once Japan's largest grape area (NTA "one of the largest" around 1930; Osaka tourism "largest cultivated area" in early Showa; Wine Anorak "number one grape-growing area"); table grape base (NTA; Wine Anorak: 80% of Kawachi's grapes are table grapes).
- Grapes: only Delaware kept: "made mainly from Delaware" (NTA spec), Delaware wines tasted at Kawachi (Wine Anorak); NTA survey table 46 has Osaka third in Japan for Delaware received (70 t). That Delaware leads rests on NTA alone (spec and survey are one publisher); kept because presence is independently confirmed. Logged as a near-miss.
- Held back: Koshu, Muscat Bailey A, Kyoho and others (listed in the spec only); "Kongo Mountains foothills" (NTA only); ratings (NTA spec only: reds "full-bodied", mild acidity).
