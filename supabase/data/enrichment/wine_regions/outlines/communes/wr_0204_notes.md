# Tejo (PT): boundary notes

**Drawn:** DO do Tejo ("DoTejo", PDO-PT-A1544).

**Official text:** Portaria 140/2010, DR 1.ª série n.º 45, 5 March 2010, art. 3 and annex I ("Todo o concelho" for every row), copy at https://www.cvrtejo.pt/Assets/Ficheiros/paginas/portaria-140-2010-do-do-tejo.pdf (raw/pt_area/, txt/portaria_140_2010.txt); restated in the caderno sec. 5, eAmbrosia attachment 3484. Six sub-regions, all whole concelhos: Almeirim (Almeirim, Alpiarça, Salvaterra de Magos), Cartaxo (Azambuja, Cartaxo), Chamusca (Chamusca, Golegã, Abrantes, Constância, Sardoal, Mação), Coruche (Benavente, Coruche), Santarém (Rio Maior, Santarém), Tomar (Tomar, Torres Novas, Alcanena, Entroncamento, Vila Nova da Barquinha, Ferreira do Zêzere).

**Conflict found and resolved:** the caderno's sec. 8 (link with the area) still describes Chamusca as Chamusca, Golegã plus only the freguesias of Tramagal and Santa Margarida da Coutada, and Tomar as Tomar, Torres Novas plus only Chãos and Praia do Ribatejo. The Portaria's own preamble says it widened ("alterar a área de produção das sub-regiões da Chamusca e Tomar"), so sec. 8 is the older wording. The legal delimitation (art. 3, annex I) is drawn, and the Chamusca and Tomar appellation notes in style.json were corrected to match it (the style agent had used sec. 8).

**Units:** 21 concelhos, 135 GISCO LAU 2024 freguesias, concelho codes from DGT CAOP. Rows 135; partial 0. Azambuja is in Lisboa district (it is the one Lisboa concelho left out of IG Lisboa, so the two regions meet but do not overlap).

**Not checked:** whether the Portaria was amended after 2010 (no consolidated text read).
