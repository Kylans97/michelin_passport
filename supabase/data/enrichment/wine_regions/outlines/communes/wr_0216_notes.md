## Held back / left out (style)
- Murfatlar red_body / red_tannin / red_acidity: Single document says reds are full bodied with velvety tannins; winetourism.com only says 'ripe reds' / 'fruity reds'. No second source for a level.
- Murfatlar Black Sea breeze moderating climate: winetourism.com says Dobrogea is moderated by Black Sea breezes; the single document speaks of the Danube Black Sea Canal and sea as thermal regulator in autumn. Close but not the same claim; left out of the summary to be safe.
- Murfatlar Băbească Neagră, Merlot, Pinot Noir and others: Listed on winetourism.com's Dobrogea page (region wide) but Băbească Neagră is not in the Murfatlar single document; others lack a Murfatlar specific second source.
