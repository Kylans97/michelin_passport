## Context: why press disagrees

MICHELIN does label some other selections announced in autumn 2026 with 2027. The same English article feed shows "MICHELIN Guide Beijing & Tianjin 2027" and "The MICHELIN Guide Athens, Thessaloniki, Santorini 2027". So "2027" is a real MICHELIN convention for some destinations, but MICHELIN does not use it for the Netherlands. The Netherlands keeps the edition year of its ceremony: 2025 was announced 6 Oct 2025 and 2026 was announced 5 Oct 2026. Any press source calling this the "2027" Netherlands guide is wrong by MICHELIN's own wording.
