# Guide year for the Belgium & Luxembourg selection

**Finding: MICHELIN labels the current selection "MICHELIN Gids België en Luxemburg 2026" (Guide MICHELIN Belgique et Luxembourg 2026).** It was announced on Monday 4 May 2026 at the Handelsbeurs in Antwerp. The previous edition is "MICHELIN Gids België en Luxemburg 2025", announced 28 April 2025. I checked guide.michelin.com's ceremony article index (be/nl and be/fr, pages 1-2) on 2026-10-28 and found no later BE/LU selection.

The headline, URL, publication date and the 151 total all say 2025, and the list itself parses to 2 + 22 + 127 = 151.

## Notes

- Belgium & Luxembourg keeps the year of its spring ceremony: 2025 was announced in April 2025 and 2026 in May 2026. Nothing on MICHELIN's site calls this selection "2027". The next edition would be expected around spring 2027, but MICHELIN has not announced it.
- The live site no longer matches the article exactly. The 2026 article lists 139 starred restaurants; the live all-starred listing on 2026-10-28 shows 136. La Paix (2 stars), Kommilfoo (1 star) and La Table de Manon (1 star) now return "Restaurant not found", and MICHELIN gives no reason.
