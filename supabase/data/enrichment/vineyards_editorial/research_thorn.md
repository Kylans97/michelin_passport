## Recognition (re-checked 2026-11-20)
- **Rijks (1★, Amsterdam), wine list July 2026: re-confirmed.** I downloaded the PDF again (HTTP 200, Last-Modified 22 Jul 2026), and its text is identical to the earlier copy. It has three entries for this estate. "Wijngoed Thorn" is on the Dutch estates map. "Wijngaard Thorn, Thorn, Limburg" lists **Pinot Noir 2022 at €75**. Under sweet wines, "Wijngoed Thorn, Maasvallei" lists **Late Harvest 2024, 0.375 l, €65**. https://www.rijksrestaurant.nl/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/RIJKS_WINEMENU_2026-1.pdf
- **Kassa/BNNVARA test, 19 Sep 2020: confirmed, with one correction (claim b).** The article is "Test: witte wijn van Nederlandse bodem", dated 19-09-2020 (`raw/kassa.html`). Wijngoed Thorn won **both** categories: Pinot Gris 2018 (€12.95) scored **8**, first of 5, and Riesling 2013 (€13.95) scored **8.6**, first of 5. The panel was three people: wine journalist Esmee Langereis, sommelier Ronnie Brouwer (Kromme Dissel, Sommelier of the Year 2017) and BNNVARA presenter Astrid Joosten. **Correction: the article never says the tasting was blind.** It says the ten wines were bought from online shops. Don't call it a "blind test". Also, each category had only five wines (the Riesling field included a Jumbo supermarket wine), so it's a consumer TV test, not a competition. https://www.bnnvara.nl/kassa/artikelen/test-witte-wijn-van-nederlandse-bodem

## Other facts seen on the estate's site (context)
- Started growing grapes and making wine in 2001; 9 ha of vineyard. The site says the vines sit on an old arm of the Maas (clay, sand, gravel), and that Thorn is among the driest areas of the Benelux (680 mm/year, about 1,600 sun hours). Both are the estate's own figures. Source: homepage and technical sheets
- Phone +31 (0)475 56 10 02; info@wijngoed-thorn.nl. Source: /contact/
- It appeared in the ANWB "Reisgids voor levensgenieters" video with Rik Felderhof and Hans Melissen. Source: /proeflokaal/

## Things to watch
- **There are no coordinates for this record.** This is the only one of the four estates where the published address has no exact BAG point. Possible reasons: BAG may record the shop under 12 or another number, or "12A" may be a postal suffix that BAG doesn't carry. Resolving it needs a human, for example by asking the estate or checking the BAG viewer by hand. Do not snap to Bogenstraat 12.
- **The shop is not the vineyard.** The address is a town-centre wine shop. Do not describe it as a vineyard visit, and do not put a "vineyard" pin there. The site states plainly that there are no tours.
- **Rijks uses two names.** It writes "Wijngaard Thorn" in one place and "Wijngoed Thorn" in two. The estate writes **Wijngoed Thorn**.
- **Vintage mismatch.** Rijks lists Late Harvest **2024**, while the estate's own technical sheet shows **2021**. The site's sheets look stale (the footer reads "© 2019"). This doesn't affect the record, but don't quote vintages from the estate site as current.
- The International Wine Challenge medals for "The King's Thorn" Pinot Gris belong to a New Zealand wine, not this estate (noted in candidates.md, still true).
