## Recognition (re-checked 2026-11-20)
- **Rijks (1★, Amsterdam), wine list July 2026: St. Martinus is NOT on it.** I downloaded the PDF again (text identical to the earlier copy) and searched for "Martinus": no hits. "Vijlen" appears only as a BOB region label on the map page. candidates.md never claimed Rijks for this estate. Recording it here so no one adds it later. https://www.rijksrestaurant.nl/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/RIJKS_WINEMENU_2026-1.pdf
- **Oxford Companion to Wine, "Netherlands" entry: the page opened this time.** It reads: "The small village of Vijlen houses the biggest vineyard so far, St. Martinus, with 11 ha." This is the 4th edition (copyright 2015), with data from 2014. **It is stale.** The estate's own history page reports 11.3 ha in 2010 and 12 ha in 2014, and its philosophy page now says 28 ha across several Zuid-Limburg plots. Don't quote "biggest vineyard, 11 ha" as current. https://app.ckbk.com/reference/wine05383c14s001e033
- Wijnkeuring van de Lage Landen 2025, 10 golds (candidates.md): **not rechecked on this pass.** The estate's own history says it was named best vineyard of the Wijnkeuring van de Lage Landen in 2012, 2015 and 2016. That is the estate's claim and I did not check it against the competition.

## Other facts seen on the estate's site (context)
- Hans Beurskens started a hobby vineyard in 1980 and moved to the Rott in Vijlen in 1986. The vineyard was named St. Martinus in 1988. Stan Beurskens took over young. The history page says 1995, "nog net geen 18"; the RVO dossier says 1996, at 18. The estate runs a viticulture advisory business (since 2007) and a wine knowledge centre (since 2014), and has held the FAIR'N GREEN sustainability certificate. Its gravity-fed cellar opened in 2014, 18 m underground. Source: /wijndomein/historie/, homepage
- Phone 043 455 2027; info@wijngaardmartinus.nl. LÖSS: 043 303 0093.

## Things to watch
- **Opening hours conflict within the site.** The footer on every page, and the homepage, give: shop Apr–Oct Mon–Tue 10–17 and Wed–Sun 10–18; tasting room Apr–Oct Wed–Sun and Nov–Mar Fri–Sun. The contact page *body* gives: shop every day 10–18 (Apr–Oct) and 10–17 (Nov–Mar); tasting room Tue–Sun all year. Both versions agree only that the shop is open daily 10:00–17:00 from November to March. Don't store exact tasting-room hours without confirming them with the estate.
- **Founding year.** The homepage says "sinds 1986" and "de eerste rode wijnmakers in Nederland" (the estate's own claim, unchecked). The history page dates the name to 1988, and the RVO dossier dates the first vines to 1988. "First red wine makers in NL" should not go into editorial copy without independent support.
- **Two names in use, and Rijks never names it.** Prefer **Wijngaard St. Martinus** (title, copyright, contact), and keep "Wijndomein St. Martinus" as an alias. The address is "Rott 21a". The site writes "6294 Nl" in one place; the postcode is 6294 NL.
- **The hotel is a different address.** If the app ever lists LÖSS, it needs its own record (Vijlenberg 76). Do not merge it into this estate's pin.
- **The BOB field is blank on purpose.** Fill it only when the estate, a label image or an official register entry ties St. Martinus to BOB Vijlen.
