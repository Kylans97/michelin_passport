## Near-misses and inconsistencies
- **Name.** The brief says "Wijngoed / Hoeve Apostelhoeve". The estate never uses either form. It writes "De Apostelhoeve" (also "de Apostelhoeve Maastricht" in the homepage title, and "Wijndomein de Apostelhoeve" in running text). Use "De Apostelhoeve".
- **"Oldest" claim.** The site is inconsistent: the homepage says "Nederlands oudste" and the over-ons page says "Limburgs oudste". Hoeve Nekum's own site calls itself "op één na oudste wijngoed in Nederland". Do not put "oldest in the Netherlands" into editorial copy unless an independent source confirms it.
- **BOB "Maasvallei Limburg"** is a different BOB, used by Wijngoed Thorn, among others. Nothing on Apostelhoeve's site claims it.
- All wines were listed as "Uitverkocht" (sold out) except Cuvée St. Pieter Brut when I checked on 2026-11-20. That is stock, not closure.
