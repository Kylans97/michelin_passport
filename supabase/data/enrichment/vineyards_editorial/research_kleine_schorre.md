## Recognition (re-checked 2026-11-20)
- **Rijks (1★, Amsterdam), wine list July 2026: re-confirmed.** I downloaded the PDF again (HTTP 200, Last-Modified 22 Jul 2026). Its extracted text is identical to the earlier copy. The map page "Nederlandse wijndomeinen op deze wijnkaart" names "Wijnhoeve de Kleine Schorre", with Schouwen-Duiveland as its region. **No Kleine Schorre wine is listed by name or price** on the Dutch pages (only Apostelhoeve, Wijngaardsberg and Thorn are). So this shows the estate on the map, not a wine on the list. https://www.rijksrestaurant.nl/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/RIJKS_WINEMENU_2026-1.pdf (`raw/rijks_recheck.pdf`, `raw/rijks_recheck.txt`)
- **Oxford Companion to Wine, "Netherlands" entry: confirmed (claim a).** The ckbk page opened this time (HTTP 200; `raw/ocw_ckbk.html`). The text reads: "Zeeland, in the sunny south west, is home to one of the best Dutch producers: Kleine Schorre." The copyright line on the page is "© Jancis Robinson and Oxford University Press 1994, 1999, 2006, 2015", so this is the **4th edition (2015)**. The paragraph's data dates from 2014 ("In 2014 there were about 180 active vine-growers"). Cite it as OCW 4th ed., not as a current verdict. https://app.ckbk.com/reference/wine05383c14s001e033
- Kassa/BNNVARA test, 19 Sep 2020: Kleine Schorre Pinot Gris 2019 finished **3rd of 5** (score 7.3, tied on points with Apostelhoeve, which placed 2nd as the cheaper wine). https://www.bnnvara.nl/kassa/artikelen/test-witte-wijn-van-nederlandse-bodem

- Founded 2001, working with the Luxembourg wine house Cep d'Or. The site calls Cep d'Or "ons leerbedrijf" and also sells its wines. 63,000 vines on 14 ha, "een van de grootste wijngaarden van Nederland" (the estate's own claim). The tarred barn dates from 1735. Source: /geschiedenis

## Things to watch
- **Name casing.** The estate uses both "De Kleine Schorre" and "de Kleine Schorre". Rijks writes "Wijnhoeve de Kleine Schorre", and the Oxford Companion writes just "Kleine Schorre". Use **Wijnhoeve De Kleine Schorre** (the contact-block form) unless a house style says otherwise.
- **"Schouwen-D®uiveland" is a trademark, not the BOB name.** The wine labels on /assortiment read "Schouwen-D®uiveland Auxerrois" and so on. The ® wordplay is a brand. Do not read it as proof that a wine carries the BOB, and do not copy the ® into the BOB field.
- **Zuiddijk 4 vs 4a.** BAG has both: **4** (industry + residential function) and **4a** (industrial, 1,380 m², status "Verbouwing verblijfsobject", meaning under renovation). The estate publishes **4**, and PDOK ranked 4a *first* for the query, so a careless "take the top hit" would have picked the wrong point. I used only the exact match for 4. Which building the shop or tasting room actually occupies is not stated.
- **The tasting room's winter closure starts tomorrow (Sat 21 Nov 2026).** The site writes "dinsdag 7 december", but 7 Dec 2026 is a Monday, so the end date is uncertain by a day.
- The tasting room is run by a separate couple (Guus and Sitare de Jong) and has its own Instagram account. If the app shows one handle, use the estate's @dekleineschorre, not the tasting room's.
- The old page `/bezoek` (raw `ks_bezoek.html`, from the earlier pass) is a 404. The live page is `/bezoek-de-wijnhoeve`.
