The MICHELIN Guide Restaurant Ceremony Nordic Countries 2026 took place on Monday 1st June at the Tivoli Concert Hall, set within Tivoli Gardens in Copenhagen, Denmark. Not only were the new MICHELIN Stars, Bib Gourmands and Special Awards announced, but this event also marked the unveiling of the full 2026 edition of the Guide, where a total of 23 new restaurants were added across Denmark, Finland, Iceland, Norway and Sweden. Eleven entered the Guide with new awards in the form of Stars or Bib Gourmands, while 12 others joined the Main Selection. Here, we take a look at all of those added to the Guide this year.For the hottest news from the Ceremony, check out our other articles: All the Award Winners / New MICHELIN Stars / New Bib Gourmands / Every MICHELIN Star in the Nordic Countries / Dine with a MICHELIN Inspector at Our New Three Star
