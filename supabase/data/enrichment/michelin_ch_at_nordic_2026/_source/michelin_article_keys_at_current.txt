On 18 September 2026, the MICHELIN Guide reveals its selection of the world’s most outstanding hotels for the second time. It is the culmination of countless journeys and careful evaluations by the MICHELIN Guide Inspectors.Here is everything you need to know about the MICHELIN Keys.In total, the MICHELIN Guide’s 2026 hotel selection in Austria includes two Three Key hotels, 42 Two Key hotels – including three new additions – and 49 One Key hotels, of which seven have been newly awarded One Key.
Beneath high ceilings and historic arches, THEO’s Lounge & Bar blends Viennese coffeehouse culture with contemporary bar culture. © Anantara Palais Hansen Vienna

From the rooms of Rosewood Vienna, in the heart of Vienna’s Old Town, views open onto the historic façades and landmarks around Petersplatz – here, the city is quite literally part of the interior. © Rosewood Vienna
23 Keys Go to ViennaVienna has two new Two-Key hotels, bringing the Austrian capital’s total to 23 Key hotels. The Anantara Palais Hansen Vienna Hotel is one of this year’s winners following an extensive renovation and rebranding. The second new Two-Key hotel is the Mandarin Oriental Vienna, which occupies the city’s landmark former commercial court, built in 1908 and meticulously restored and transformed into a hotel.Vienna’s Key hotels stand out for their variety. Alongside elegant luxury hotels are a number of younger, hip and contemporary properties, including Hotel Josefine, Hollmann Beletage Design & Boutique Hotel and Hotel MOTTO.See more: 2 Days in Vienna
At 2,090 metres, Hotel Schöne Aussicht lives up to its name: From its panoramic rooms, views stretch across the ski slopes of Hochsölden to the surrounding 3,000-metre peaks. © Hotel Schöne Aussicht
Reaching New Heights: The Ski Resorts Continue to Rise
Tyrol in western Austria is known for its breathtaking Alpine landscapes, authentic cuisine — from Kaspressknödel to Schlutzkrapfen — and warm hospitality. In fact, the warmth of the Tyrolean welcome is legendary. This year’s newly awarded hotels offer another reason to visit.The Schlosshotel in Fiss is newly awarded Two Keys this year. Sölden welcomes the new One-Key Hotel Schöne Aussicht, while Ullrhaus - St. Anton brings another new One-Key hotel to St. Anton am Arlberg.Elsewhere in western Austria, Hotel Nesslerhoff in Grossarl, Salzburger Land, and Hotel & Chalet Aurelio in Lech am Arlberg, Vorarlberg, have each been newly awarded One MICHELIN Key. 
The centrepiece of the Living Garden at Nesslerhof is an 800-square-metre natural swimming pond, which is entirely chemical-free. Surrounding sun terraces and lounging areas invite guests to relax while taking in views of the Grossarl mountains. © Hotel Nesslerhof
