The MICHELIN Guide Croatia 2026 Is Unveiled!
The new and highly anticipated 2026 edition of the MICHELIN Guide Croatia was officially presented today.

During their visits throughout the country, the inspectors noted a strong growth in the gastronomic scene, with 20 new restaurants completing a selection that reconfirms the
 two-star 
Agli Amici
 restaurant in Rovinj and adds a 
new restaurant with a star
 in Istria, 
Harry's Piccolo Porec
, to the 
12 reconfirmed stars
 for this year. 
This is also a year of exceptional growth, with 
three new Bib Gourmands
 and four "
Special Michelin
" awards for talented professionals.
Agli Amici Rovinj
: Excellence Confirmed with 2 Stars
For three years, this extraordinary restaurant has maintained an absolute level of excellence, a merit widely recognized by the prestigious confirmation of its
 2 Michelin stars
. Chef 
Emanuele Scarello  
(formerly also at the helm of the historic
 Agli Amici 
in Udine) and resident chef 
Simone de Lucca 
demonstrate an extraordinary ability to manage and create a high-profile gastronomic project. 
The end result, already excellent in itself, is further enhanced by a splendid location that offers guests a breathtaking view, transforming every dinner into an unforgettable moment.
An elegant composition of delicately arranged bites, enhanced by a theatrical veil of aromatic smoke and finished with vibrant yellow accents. © Agli Amici Rovinj
A new star shines at 
Harry's Piccolo Poreč
On the first floor of a modern hotel complex lies the restaurant led by 
Goran Hrastovčak
, a local chef with extensive experience. The elegant and contemporary spaces also include a pleasant terrace ideal for cocktails and aperitifs, served with professionalism and passion by a young and exceptionally friendly team.
The gastronomic offering includes two tasting menus and an à la carte selection, in a journey that blends sea and land, Italy and Istria, tradition and contemporary sensibility. The dishes are striking for their colors, aromas, and harmonious flavors, such as lobster with béarnaise sauce and kruškovac, black truffle, and green pepper.
A refined green composition featuring a smooth quenelle, delicate textures, and precise plating in a striking, contemporary setting. © Harry’s Piccolo Poreč
3 new restaurants have been awarded the Bib Gourmand
Bistro Loora - Dakovo
On a lively pedestrian street in the city center leading to the evocative Cathedral of St. Peter, this small and welcoming bistro is distinguished by its pleasant outdoor seating nestled in a shaded courtyard. The contemporary interior offers a relaxed atmosphere where you can enjoy creatively reinterpreted local cuisine with a modern twist. Don't miss their version of "Beef Wellington," the chef's signature dish.
A beautifully plated composition of tender sliced meat, complemented by vibrant purées, earthy textures, and refined garnishes in a harmonious presentation. © Bistro Loora
Lulu Fusion Bistro - Osijek
Just steps from the historic center, this small and welcoming bistro (with just a few tables, so reservations are recommended) welcomes guests into a contemporary setting, complete with a convivial bar where you can sip a cocktail while waiting for your meal. The cuisine is creative and fun, with dishes reminiscent of Asian flavors, expertly crafted by the chef with balance and harmony using local ingredients. During the warmer months, a few tables are also available outside.
A plate of delicate dumplings filled and folded with care, topped with fresh herbs and spices, served alongside a flavorful dipping sauce. © Lulu Fusion Bistro
Nuré - Zagreb
Tucked away on a narrow street in the city center, it's one of those places that surprises. A tiny bistro with just four tables, welcoming and with a relaxed atmosphere: a little gem made special by the cuisine, which reflects the international flair of a Korean family who arrived in Zagreb after significant fine dining experiences in Europe and Asia. The dishes, only slightly influenced by their origins, express a distinct and creative personality. Among the most popular are the unmissable hummus with broccoli, scamorza cheese, crispy onion, and paprika powder, or the excellent rack of lamb with smoked mashed potatoes, harissa sauce, roasted bell peppers, and green goddess salsa. At lunch, the menu is simpler.
Finely chopped tartare served on crisp textures, elevated with colorful garnishes and delicate strips in a playful, refined presentation. © Nuré.
16 new restaurants join the main selection
7 Seas by Matija Bogdan – Njivice
The location directly on the sea is already a great reason to choose it: the view of the water accompanies practically every table, becoming even more evocative with outdoor service.
Cantilly Garden – Samobor
The young and talented chef Luka Dzamarija, originally from Dubrovnik, offers Mediterranean cuisine with an Italian touch, based on the rigorous use of local products, enriched with aromatic herbs and vegetables from his own garden.
Dida – Crikvenica
The kitchen offers rich and colorful dishes, built on numerous ingredients and inspired by local specialties, reinterpreted with contemporary taste and carefully presented.
Foša– Zadar
Located at the foot of Zadar's historic walls, right across from the small port, the restaurant celebrates seafood with classic recipes reinterpreted with a modern twist.
Jaz by Ana Roš Poreč – Poreč
Following its success in Ljubljana, the second Jaz by Ana Roš restaurant has opened along Poreč's charming waterfront, housed in the elegant, contemporary spaces of the Jadran Heritage Hotel. The celebrated three-Michelin-starred chef brings her more informal and lively interpretation of her cuisine to Croatia.
Ješka Fine Bistro - Makarska
This small and cozy tavern, with a rustic yet modern feel, is run by the couple Ela Ožić and Ljudevit Blažević. Their cuisine is based on seasonal ingredients and carefully selected raw materials, distinguished by their freshness and quality.
Kiyomi – Zagreb
Drawing on his experience in Japan and China, talented chef Vid Nikolić offers a rich and dynamic cuisine, ranging from dim sum to robata grill, from tempura to sashimi, gunkan, temari, and hand rolls, all prepared with authentic techniques and in full respect of Asian tradition.
Konoba Campanelo – Mirlović Zagora
Konoba Campanelo isn't easy to find: from the center of Šibenik, it takes about thirty minutes to reach it, winding, and winding roads, and a short path leading to a quiet village surrounded by nature. But the journey will be worth it with its youthful and down-to-earth cuisine, based on raw ingredients, technique, and a modern approach that's respectful of tradition.
LABRAX - Zadar
Located in the heart of the historic center, LABRAX stands out for its modern, welcoming style and delicious cuisine that reinterprets tradition with a contemporary twist. One example is the scorpion fish and mussel brudet (škrpina i dagnje brudet), served authentically with whole fish, including mantis shrimp and red scorpion fish.
Maguro – Zadar
Located in the historic part of the city, Maguro restaurant offers modern Asian fusion cuisine, featuring colorful and distinctive dishes. Guests can choose between the intimate dining room with an open kitchen or the pleasant outdoor terrace overlooking the promenade.
Nico’s Bar & Bistro – Zagreb
A modern and dynamic venue, featuring a cocktail bar and a bistro with contemporary, welcoming furnishings. The spacious outdoor terrace is ideal for enjoying a pleasantly relaxed atmosphere. The cuisine offers traditional Croatian and Mediterranean dishes, reinterpreted with a creative and contemporary twist.
Plavi Podrum – Opatija
A restaurant with over a century of history, the longest-running on the Opatija coast, splendidly overlooking the small port of Volosko, a charming town on the road to Rijeka. For over thirty years, it has been run by Daniela Kramarić, an award-winning sommelier, who welcomes guests with warmth and, thanks to her profound expertise, helps them choose the dishes and wines.
SOL - Zagreb
This is the new venture from renowned chef Mate Janković, located just steps from the vibrant Ban Jelačić Square and the evocative Cathedral of the Assumption of the Blessed Virgin Mary. The contemporary interior design stands out for its street art-style walls, which contribute to a casual and creative atmosphere.
Stancija Kovačići - Opatija
Less than a ten-minute drive from Opatija, nestled in the greenery at the foot of the Učka mountains, a historic late-19th-century family farmhouse has been transformed into a restaurant with a few guest rooms. The atmosphere is simple and authentic, with family management led by chef-owner Vinko Frian.
The Lubenice Restaurant – Cres
You'll find yourself on an elegant veranda (which becomes a fully open terrace in summer) overlooking the sea and the natural surroundings of the modern and luxurious The Isolano hotel. In this idyllic setting, you can enjoy colorful and generous Mediterranean cuisine—not just seafood—that emphasizes local and seasonal ingredients to showcase the finest ingredients.
The Restaurant - Hotel Supetar – Cavtat
The restaurant occupies a historic building and features a pleasant terrace overlooking the Cavtat waterfront. The à la carte menu offers seafood and meat dishes prepared with local ingredients, reinterpreting Croatian tradition with a Mediterranean twist. A quiet and relaxed atmosphere completes the experience.
A minimalist composition of cocoa-dusted forms set against a soft, creamy base, exploring contrast, repetition, and texture in a quietly radical expression. © SOL
The four "Michelin Special Awards" for professional talent
The MICHELIN Special Awards are given to exceptional individuals who contribute to making the guest experience unforgettable through their passion and skills.
Michelin Award for the young chef
Marin Pleše – 
Restorant Vila Rova
, Malinska 
This young chef exudes overwhelming energy and surprising maturity in the kitchen. Tasting his many dishes immediately suggests he's talent has still a long way forward. The secret to his success lies in his ability to showcase rare and authentic local products, while treating them with refined, cutting-edge technique. Each dish on the menu tells a story of local tradition, innovation, and pinpoint precision. A dynamic and constantly growing talent.
Michelin Award for the sommelier of the year
Branimir Vukšić – Restaurant 
Pelegrini
, One Michelin Star, Šibenik  
The friendliness and skill of Sommelier Branimir Vukšić at the Pelegrini restaurant in Šibenik   is the guarantee of a moment of true passion and sharing. The predominantly Croatian wine list will take you on a journey around Dalmatia and its finest wine-producing regions, with Branimir's flair for sharing his emotions with you. This sensorial and psychological skill makes pairing wine with food a truly memorable experience.
Michelin Award for the new opening of the year
Nure’ 
– Bib Gourmand, Zagreb
This small but splendid project presents itself as a contemporary fusion restaurant. The cuisine is based on solid French techniques, but stands out for its decidedly modern approach and unique interpretation of the dishes. The menu offers a fresh and creative vision of cuisine, all complemented by welcoming and unpretentious service. Behind the project is a young management team - chef Hwan Woong Kim - with extensive experience in the kitchens of some of the most important restaurants in Seoul and France.
Michelin Award for service
Monte 
– One Michelin Star,  Rovinj
Behind the extraordinary gastronomic experience at the Monte restaurant in Rovinj—famous for being Croatia's first Michelin star—is a perfect synergy, expertly led by Tjitske Dekic-Brusse, the hospitality and service deserve due praise: an impeccable combination of precision and punctuality and superb coordination of the team.
Mindful Voices  
Mindful Voices New for 2026, the Mindful Voices platform brings together inspirational voices from across the hospitality industry – an editorial space where chefs, hoteliers and winemakers can share their stories and pioneering practices, both with one another and the rest of the world.  
Gwendal Poullennec, International Director of the MICHELIN Guide, says, “Mindful Voices will give a platform to all those who are rewriting the rules in their respective fields. This new framework draws directly from what our inspection teams witness firsthand: encounters and experiences that are transforming how things are done and that deserve to be shared. Staying true to its identity and values, the MICHELIN Guide will amplify and give full resonance to these voices from the worlds of gastronomy, hospitality and wine so that they can be heard, wherever they may be.”  
Traditional composition of seasonal vegetables, gently cooked and paired with creamy elements and fresh herbs, evoking simple, timeless flavors. © Korak.
