        On August 26, 2026, the very first Selection for the MICHELIN Guide American Southwest was revealed, spotlighting a total of 5 Starred restaurants. Below, you can delve deeper into the culinary trends that are defining the region's gastronomy right now or simply browse every single Star. 
        Recognizable Names Remain ImpressiveIt may come as no surprise that Las Vegas, a place known for its over-the-top personality, is full of restaurants with big names. In fact, it often seems you can’t turn a corner without seeing another recognizable, celebrity chef-backed (and named) spot. While some are more flash-in-the-pan, several stood out to the MICHELIN Guide Inspectors for truly delivering Star-worthy meals.  Indeed, four of the five Starred restaurants in the inaugural selection are eponymous spots in Las Vegas (see below), but it’s not the only spot in the American Southwest with well-known chefs. Cities like Phoenix and Santa Fe are home to restaurants that continue to be heralded as flagships, including Recommended restaurants Christopher Gross at Wrigley Mansion in Phoenix, Arizona, and Restaurant Martin in Santa Fe, New Mexico.  
                Recommended restaurants Christopher Gross at Wrigley Mansion in Phoenix, Arizona, and Restaurant Martin in Santa Fe, New Mexico. © Paul Moore/Christopher's at Wrigley Mansion | © Gabriella Marks/Restaurant Martin
        Diverse Dining Scenes Offer a Wide Variety Across the Southwest  Our Inspectors discovered the Southwest teeming with restaurants offering everything from ramen and Indian to Chinese noodles, as well as standout regional cuisine at longstanding restaurants. The Bib Gourmand-Awarded restaurants provide fantastic insight into local cuisines. In Phoenix, Pizzeria Bianco run by Chris Bianco is a flagship that stands the test of time. Also in Phoenix, Valentine is a Swiss army knife-style spot, featuring a Mexican coffee program, pastries and savory dishes, plus a vintage furniture store in the back. In Las Vegas, where it’s a bit more difficult to stretch a dollar, YEN Viet Kitchen, run by a mother and daughter, cooks from the heart while delivering great value.
