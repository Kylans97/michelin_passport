        With the winter months fast approaching, there's hardly a better time to put the spotlight on the best hotels in Mexico. After a global reveal in September that announced hundreds of new Keys around the world, Mexico counts 98 Key hotels in 2026, including 12 brand-new additions, 74 One-Keys, 21 Two-Keys and three superlative Three-Key hotels. Like the MICHELIN Stars for restaurants, the MICHELIN Keys are our most outstanding hotels. Go straight to the list or check out some of the highlights of the 2026 Mexico Key hotels, below.

                For the third year in a row, Xinalani earns Three Keys on Mexico's Pacific coast. Pictured: a jungle-immersed sauna. © Xinalani
        The Three-Key Club in Mexico The country’s three top-rated stays retain their honors in 2026: One&Only Mandarina, Hotel Esencia and Xinalani. Perhaps unsurprisingly in this country blessed with so much tropical coastline, each represents the best of the country's many beachside accommodations, with architecture that takes full advantage of the Mexican landscape and rooms hidden among the trees. Mandarina and Xinalani make their homes on the Pacific, while Esencia represents the country's Caribbean coast. 
                The entrance to Rosewood San Miguel de Allende mimics a typical 19th-century hacienda. © Rosewood San Miguel de Allende
        A New Two-Key in San Miguel de Allende 
 This year, Rosewood San Miguel de Allende joins Casa de Sierra Nevada, A Belmond Hotel as the only Two-Key hotels located in San Miguel de Allende, a historic, cobblestoned town tucked in Mexico's central highlands. The Rosewood is a modern hacienda with 19th-century flair; Casa de Sierra Nevada, meanwhile, incorporates six colonial houses that date back to the 16th century. Both put you in easy walking distance to San Miguel's striking, pink parish church in the central square.  
                Once a 16th-century private residence, Hotel Sevilla is now one of Merida's trendiest hotels. © Hotel Sevilla / Rodrigo Hermida
        The Beauty of a Mexican MansionThroughout the country, Mexico sports a unique abundance of residential-style accommodations, beautiful houses filled with the kind of character that can only come from a lifetime of personal care and passion. For decades, these spaces were used as private family homes and, as a result, they retain a particular sense of warmth and intimacy.A handful of these mansion hotels have earned Inspector recognition in Mexico, from Grana B&B in Oaxaca City, to Pug Seal Allan Poe in the capital, to Merida's brutalist masterpiece, Hotel Sevilla.
