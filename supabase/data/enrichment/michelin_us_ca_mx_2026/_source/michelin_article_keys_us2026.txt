        New Key Hotels from Sea to Shining SeaThere is no single way to sum up the new U.S. Keys in 2026. They span every nook and cranny of the country and cover new builds like One&Only Moonlight Basin in Montana (a nominee for the 2026 Opening of the Year Award) and restored gems, among them Park Avenue icon Waldorf Astoria in NYC and the historic Douglas in Savannah. 
Some of the most under-the-radar finds are the tiny, cozy boutiques. Richmond’s Boulevard Inn boasts just five guest rooms and nightly wine-and-cheese gatherings. Sophie’s Gasthaus, just outside San Antonio, offers eight suites, in a 1906 mansion with a distinctly German style.
And if it wasn’t obvious last year, it is now: travelers are craving nature. Mountain and nature properties — see Prospect Berkshires in Massachusetts and the Swag in the Smoky Mountains, Tennessee — dominate a wide swath of the 2026 class, alongside a growing interest in working farms like upstate New York’s Wildflower Farms, now a Two-Key hotel. 
Head here for a deep dive into all 65 new Keys and upgrades by destination and style. These new additions bring the total number of Keys to 359 in the United States, broken down to 267 One-Key hotels, 78 Two-Keys and 44 Three-Keys. 
                Amangiri in Canyon Point, Utah, retains its Three-Key distinction in 2026. © Amangiri
        The Three-Key Club in the United StatesThe highest hotel honor in the MICHLEIN Guide, every existing Three-Key hotel in the United States retained its distinction in 2026 (with the exception of Canyon Ranch Woodside —  which closed its doors permanently in late 2025). The majority of these 15 hotels make their homes in New York and California, with one each in Arizona, Florida, Hawaii, Montana and Utah.
The Three-Keys in New York include the Aman New York and Casa Cipriani, both of which make use of glamorous Beaux Arts buildings — the former a gold-crowned skyscraper in Midtown, the latter a glamorously restored ferry terminal at the very southern tip of Manhattan. The Three-Keys in California include the landscape-immersed Post Ranch Inn, as well as one of the only Three-Key hotels with a Three-Key restaurant in the world: the sublime SingleThread Inn.
The Three-Key in Arizona, Canyon Ranch Tucson, represents one of the world’s great wellness hotels, while the Pray, Montana location of Three-Key Sage Lodge is the luxurious neighbor of Yellowstone National Park.
