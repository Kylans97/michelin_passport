  MOUNTAIN AND NATURE RETREATS
  A major trend in the 2026 Keys in the United States: a thriving world of outdoor immersion.
        Quercus, Georgia: Four European-inspired cottage suites, an extensive (and personalized) wellness program, and a restaurant led by Chef Ryan Smith of Atlanta’s Staplehouse at this new Georgian One-Key. The property’s working farm, fascinatingly, dates back to an Italian Duke and Duchess. Horses, yoga, fishing and rucking keep guests busy before nights in front of the fireplace.
Prospect Berkshires, Massachusetts: At this One-Key on Prospect Lake, 49 hand-built, Scandinavian-style cabins promise privacy and fresh air; paddle on the lake in summer, ice skate in winter, submerge in a heated saltwater pool surrounded by Finnish saunas all year long. Expect to dine on the Cliff House’s suspended porch with views of the mountains.
One&Only Moonlight Basin, Montana: The One&Only group’s first mountain resort resides alongside the landscape of Greater Yellowstone, with all the natural beauty that name implies. Standalone cabins, chic suites and spacious rooms feature mountain views; beyond are hiking trails, tennis courts and an extensive spa program, plus a guest-exclusive heated gondola for ski season. This is one of the United States' newest Two-Key hotels, as well as a nominee for the 2026 Opening of the Year Award.
Cataloochee Ranch, North Carolina: At the edge of the Great Smoky Mountains National Park, a near-century-old working ranch features hiking trails and horseback riding. Thirty modern-rustic cabins and suites combine antique décor with modern comforts at this Two-Key gem, and along with the natural experiences come certain classic games like cornhole and ping pong, plus a heated pool with mountain views.
The Swag, North Carolina: Reclaimed wood — including logs from a Tennessee church, dating to 1785 — make up part of this 18-room, Two-Key hideaway in the Smoky Mountains (the sister property of the Cataloochee Ranch above). Higher room categories come with a mix of outdoor soaking tubs, stone fireplaces and private saunas. An all-inclusive rate includes three daily meals that change according to that day’s produce haul. Come before the dinner bell rings for hors d’oeuvres in the Swag House.
                The Missing Hotel in Texas features accommodations in the midst of the forest. © Missing Hotel
        Tu Tu’ Tun Lodge, Oregon: The ideal do-nothing escape by the Siskiyou National Forest, a One-Key with reflective glass cabins overlooking Rogue River or the creek. Main lodge guest rooms feature cedar beams and river rock fireplaces; houses offer full kitchens and wrap-around decks. Dining revolves around seasonal ingredients from local farms.
Missing Hotel, Texas: Just outside Austin, a One-Key that consists of a collection of geodesic domes and bohemian villas built on platforms amid the treetops, with private decks featuring plunge pools or hot tubs. Here you’re off grid without losing Wifi completely, with the added benefit of air conditioning in summer. Don't forget to visit the mini Highlander cows that live in the resort’s lower pasture.
Goldener Hirsch, Auberge Collection, Utah: A family-owned, One-Key icon features a Bavarian-style chalet and a contemporary addition with a rooftop infinity pool overlooking the Wasatch Mountains. Two restaurants serve hearty meals after a day in Deer Valley; a full calendar of events makes it a year-round destination.
The Lodge at Blue Sky, Auberge Collection, Utah: Upgraded from One to Two Keys, at this 3,500-acre private sanctuary outside Park City days consist of horseback rides and high-alpine hikes. Cliffside suites, creekside cabins and expansive Sky Lodge rooms are designed with natural materials around panoramic views.
The Lodge at Primland, Auberge Collection, Virginia: A sprawling, wellness-focused Two-Key estate in the Blue Ridge Mountains with a main lodge, chalet-style suites, cozy cottages and treehouses. Pampering goes beyond Biologique Recherche spa treatments — think hiking, kayaking and outdoor yoga; close out the night with stargazing in the property’s conservatory.
  city standouts 
  From Los Angeles to New York and Raleigh to Oklahoma City, Inspectors continue to add gems across the country's major and mid-size metropolises. 
        The Sun Rose, California: As glamorous as its West Hollywood address is this One-Key's amenities: several restaurants, a members’ club, music venue and bowling alley. A rooftop pool is the crown jewel, where dazzling sunsets reflect Art-Deco hues. Forty-nine rooms and suites offer four choices of views, from the LA skyline to Hollywood Hills.
Fairmont Washington DC Gold Experience, District of Columbia: Heightened luxury at this One-Key just a short walk from DC’s famous monuments. A massive spa with three floors of fitness classes, steam room, sauna and saltwater pool; bites are available from breakfast through evening hors d’oeuvres, alongside an honor bar. A dedicated concierge assists in securing access to Georgetown’s lively calendar.
Nobu Hotel Atlanta, Georgia: A crisp, contemporary and hip One-Key, with a rooftop pool, cocktail lounge and Japanese design nods throughout. A privately accessed 2,000-foot villa includes a grand piano; Porsche house cars are available to all.
The Ritz-Carlton Atlanta, Georgia: Everything one expects from a Ritz-Carlton — classic elegance, elevated design, lavish guest rooms — at this One-Key, plus contemporary art and live music, Peloton bikes, a kid’s program and culinary experiences in the vibrant heart of Downtown Atlanta.
Four Seasons Hotel Chicago, Illinois: Towering above the Magnificent Mile, views at this One-Key encompass Lake Michigan to the east and the city skyline to the west. Generously sized suites serve business travelers and families; resident musicians and DJs attract locals. A waterfall-edge indoor pool takes care of the rest.
Park Hyatt Chicago, Illinois: The renovation of the world’s first Park Hyatt tapped into the brand’s artsy side. At this One-Key, coveted rooms feature floor-to-ceiling windows overlooking Lake Michigan; wellness-themed rooms offer Peloton bikes and fresh-pressed juices. Just across from the Museum of Contemporary Art.
                Think afternoon tea with blackcurrant scones at the Windsor Court in New Orleans. © The Windsor Court
        The Windsor Court, Louisiana: British sensibility meets Southern hospitality at this One-Key, steps from the New Orleans French Quarter. Ornate European-style rooms come with Italian marble baths, and a museum-quality art collection of mostly British origin lines the halls. Join a proper afternoon tea, harpist included — on site since 1984.
Heights House Hotel, North Carolina: One of the few pre-Civil War houses remaining in Raleigh, a nine-room, One-Key, Modern Victorian boutique with four-poster beds, working fireplaces, high ceilings and original parquet floors. Walkable to downtown, an ideal spot for adults celebrating something special or simply taking a weekend away.
Faena New York, New York: True indulgence at this glittering One-Key, where soaring ceilings, a cosmic mural and a gold-trimmed spiral staircase collide — and that’s just the lobby. In the Tierra Santa Healing House, find a marble hammam and South American-inspired treatments; in The Living Room, there’s nightly live music. Select suites overlook the High Line and Empire State Building.
The Ritz-Carlton New York, NoMad, New York: A 50-story tower near Madison Square Park, opened in 2022 with 250 rooms dressed in bespoke furnishings and floral accents (a nod to the historic flower district below). Promoted from One to Two Keys, every dining venue is under the leadership of internationally beloved Chef José Andrés, from mezze to cocktails at the rooftop bar.
W New York – Union Square, New York: A marriage of Art Deco and mid-century Modernism with the social atmosphere expected of any W. In Union Square, this One-Key sports coworking spaces that serve coffee by day and cocktails by night; spacious suites and a rooftop bar show off priceless Manhattan views.
Citizen House Hotel, Oklahoma: Sixteen residential-style rooms sit beneath a swanky members club in downtown Oklahoma City at this One-Key, where art, culture and social engagements intersect. A hidden speakeasy serves late night sips and karaoke; for the day, an executive workspace and game room.
Hotel Daphne, Texas: A bohemian Bunkhouse hideaway and One-Key in Houston’s The Heights, with 49 rooms, a library, and a restaurant serving house-made pasta. Select suites come with a balcony; unexpected details include stitched floral headboards and funky soap dishes. A lobby shop sells locally produced jewelry and home décor.
  COASTAL AND WATERFRONT DESTINATIONS
  From Hawaii to Florida to the Jersey Shore and Long Island, splashy beaches and tranquil seasides.
        Fairmont Breakers Long Beach, California: A One-Key beach resort with urban flair, where 185 bright guest rooms blend gold accents and modern technology, plus views over the harbor and Long Beach. A rooftop bar and restaurant, an outdoor pool, and an impressive spa uphold an always-luscious Fairmont experience, within a glamorous mid-century California context.
La Bahia Hotel & Spa, California: A luxurious One-Key in Santa Cruz that occupies a 1926 apartment complex and landmark bell tower. Spanish revival on the outside, clean, contemporary rooms on the inside (some with sea views). Gatherings take place on the lively roof deck; introverts settle in private poolside cabanas. A Mediterranean spa is among the best in town.
The Vineta Hotel, Florida: Classic Palm Beach marries global eclecticism in this Two-Key, 1926 Mediterranean Revival building, where 41 state-of-the-art rooms and suites sit two blocks from Worth Avenue. At the retro-glam Pool House, sit beneath bright bougainvillea and scalloped sun umbrellas — mind the camera, though, photoshoots are strictly forbidden.
The Ritz-Carlton Residences, Waikiki Beach, Hawaii: The eighth-floor lobby and restaurants (including a 10-seat omakase counter) soar above Waikiki’s pedestrian traffic at this One-Key; 552 ocean view units are spacious — the smallest features a kitchenette, living area and laundry. Find two separate pools for families and adults, lined by Smart TV-equipped cabanas, and the beach just a short walk away.
Four Seasons Resort Oahu at Ko Olina, Hawaii: Meaning "place of joy" in Hawaiian, this One-Key Leeward Coast resort is a quiet departure from Waikiki, facing a lagoon and the Wai'anae Mountains. Find four pools, 14 spa treatment rooms and multiple restaurants, including an outpost by Chef Michael Mina. Premium suites are ideal for romantic getaways or family vacations.
                A new One-Key in Santa Cruz set on the self-explanatory Beach Street. © La Bahia Hotel & Spa
        Hotel Albatross, New Jersey: The Victorian village of Ocean Grove on the Jersey Shore may be dry when it comes to alcohol, but not when it comes to charm; this One-Key, 27-room historic boutique (home to the town’s first elevator) reveals a reception-slash-espresso bar and old-meets-new interiors. Select rooms feature writing desks, mood lighting and eat-in kitchens. BYO-wine is encouraged; a complimentary golf cart gets you to the beach.
Canoe Place Inn & Cottages, New York: A 17th-century Native American portage site is now home to the 25 historic inn rooms, garden cottages and waterfront residences at this One-Key in Hampton Bays on Long Island, each blending bright spaces with modern amenities. Outdoor fire pits complement cozy interiors adorned with colorful art; fresh seafood is often prepared over a Cherrywood grill.
Faraway Sag Harbor, New York: An expansive One-Key estate with 67 rooms and suites ranging from urban-style lofts to garden escapes. Here, find tennis courts, a heated saltwater pool and bikes for pedaling downtown Sag Harbor. Return to the stylish hotel bar for champagne and oysters before an Amalfi-inspired dinner.
The Nickel Hotel, South Carolina: The Wes Anderson-style façade is a surprise departure from Victorian interiors, where even the most basic room features French doors or a wrought-iron balcony. This One-Key's rooftop lounge serves up in-season produce and fresh seafood dishes; outside is Cannonborough, a locally beloved neighborhood and culinary hotspot.
  SPA AND RESORT DESTINATIONS 
  New One and Two-Key getaways with sprawling spas, luxe amenities or beloved on-site activities. 
        Rosewood Sand Hill, California: A Silicon Valley One-Key retreat with nature as the focus — the Santa Cruz mountains offer plenty of outdoor recreation — and even standard rooms come with balconies. Come for the extensive pool deck and luxury spa, curated speakers and weekend happy hours.
The Parker Palm Springs, California: A modern and lively One-Key, with old-school throwbacks like bubblegum-pink bellhop uniforms and backyard croquet sets. Find indoor and outdoor pools, golf, a yacht club and a nautical-themed spa. 144 rooms with eclectic adornments range from four-poster beds to a giant silver elephant sculpture.
El Encanto, California: A One-Key with dozens of posh suites and bungalows that unfold over a sleepy Santa Barbara hillside, some with their own fireplaces. A resident dairy cow supplies the restaurant with fresh cheese; terrace views sweep over the glittering Pacific. A full-service spa beckons for days spent on property.
Solage, Auberge Collection, California: A series of studios and suites at this new Two-Key in Napa wine country, full of natural materials and, in a departure from the usual in this part of the state, a distinctly urban flair. The restaurant offers a global menu crafted with local produce; a thermal pool circuit at the spa pulls from Calistoga’s natural hot springs.
The Broadmoor, Colorado: A Colorado Springs institution since 1891, this One-Key Italian neoclassical compound comes with European-style antiques and chandeliers, and plenty of bathrooms with luxe soaking tubs. Nearly 800 rooms range from standard to an actual brownstone apartment with a three-car garage. Dine at several restaurants, golf at three surrounding courses.
The Breakers, Florida: Palm Beach’s legendary resort, upgraded from One to Two Keys in 2026, boasts more than 100 years of history and 140 oceanfront acres. Expect a Mediterranean-style beach club with bungalows for lounging, two 18-hole golf courses, a tennis complex and a massive fitness center. Four pools and a complimentary chauffeur — an electric Mercedes-Maybach SUV, if you please — still don’t complete the long list of amenities.
                Attention to detail defines the redesign at this Palm Springs One-Key. © The Parker Palm Springs
        The Colony Hotel, Florida: Another Palm Beach landmark, but make it pink. Between the beach and Worth Avenue, this One-Key comes with beach cruisers and open-air buggies for exploration, plus a signature vintage Land Rover Defender for complimentary rides along the island. A full calendar of fitness classes, tennis, golf, boating and surfing is available between poolside cocktails.
The St. Regis Atlanta, Georgia: Private club vibes with hardwood floors, marble bathrooms and Juliette balconies at this One-Key; the Empire Suite has its own kitchen and Peloton gym. Dine at MICHELIN-Starred Atlas — alongside original Picassos —  after a Champagne sabrage (for the uninitiated, the art of opening a bottle with a saber). Worth a mention is the sprawling Pool Piazza, exclusive for guests.
Four Seasons Hotel New Orleans, Louisiana: Housed in the former World Trade Center tower at the foot of Canal Street, this One-Key rises above the Mississippi with riverfront views throughout. Rooms open to floor-to-ceiling water vistas; below, a resort-style pool and Louisiana cuisine. A Vue Orleans observation deck offers 360-degree views of the city.
The Frances Modern Inn, Texas: A 14-room One-Key boutique in Central East Austin. No two rooms are alike: the smallest are cozy singles with reading nooks, the largest are two-story lofts with clawfoot tubs. Communal lounges and a rooftop remain open around the clock; dine in the restaurant downstairs.
The Ritz-Carlton Dallas, Las Colinas, Texas: Spread across a former cattle ranch, several hundred guest rooms, suites and poolside villas all but blend into a hilly Las Colinas landscape at this One-Key spot. Golf is the draw, though tennis and pickleball courts, three pools and a sprawling spa keep guests busy off the course.
Four Seasons Hotel Austin, Texas: Set on three lakeside acres along Lady Bird Lake, this downtown One-Key trades city bustle for a resort-like calm. The 294 rooms lean into lake house design, with soft blue-and-gray palettes and views of the water or skyline. Expect a saltwater pool, full spa, and Texan touches like an on-demand margarita cart.
  COUNTRYSIDE AND FARM STAYS
  Beyond the major cities, where cottage stays and farm-to-table dining reign supreme.
        Le Petit Pali St Helena, California: Rooms, suites and five cozy cottages unfold at this One-Key in Napa Valley wine country; when not exploring, lounge in vintage-style green-and-white-striped poolside chaise lounges. No restaurant, but daily breakfast with champagne and local pastries, plus social hours with regional wine and canapes.
The Cottages at Little Saint, California: Star designer Ken Fulk transformed four cozy cottages and named them after classic rock songs at this One-Key, throwing in surprises like wallpapered ceilings and golden dog statues. Highlights include garden walking paths, a heated pool and fresh flower delivery upon request. Just a short walk to downtown Healdsburg for dinner.
San Ysidro Ranch, California: John and Jackie Kennedy honeymooned here; today, a continued commitment to secrecy makes the 38 California-rustic cottages at this One-Key a celebrity favorite. Complimentary farm-to-table dining, a 1920s-inspired speakeasy, plus a well-stocked wine cellar and extensive spa outside Santa Barbara.
The Inn at Mattei’s Tavern, Auberge Collection, California: An unpretentious and historically charming One-Key, with accommodations ranging from restored 19th-century cottages to more modern rooms and suites. Beyond a full spa and wellness center are vineyards and horse ranches, plus an outdoor restaurant and pool. Nights are meant for s’mores around the fire pit.
                A scene from Two-Key Wildflower Farms in New York State. © Wildflower Farms, Auberge Collection
        Wildflower Farms, Auberge Collection, New York: Upgraded from One Key and now the only Two-Key hotel in New York outside of NYC, this secluded, working farm in Hudson Valley is made up of 65 luxurious, stylish cabins with floor-to-ceiling windows that frame the meadows. The spa offers hot tubs, an outdoor pool and an indoor saltwater option, while staff guide art tours and offer painting classes. Dining take place in the farm-to-table restaurant, Clay.
The Fearrington House Inn, North Carolina: A One-Key and onetime dairy farm, still home to rare Belted Galloway cows, that offers 32 English countryside-inspired guest rooms with pillow-top featherbeds, Boca Terry robes and freshly cut flowers from the property’s gardens. Don’t miss the restaurant’s signature chocolate soufflé, a crowd pleaser for nearly half a century.
Carversville Inn, Pennsylvania: Just one hour outside of Philadelphia, Carversville’s first tavern is now a One-Key boutique hotel with six plush suites clad in jungle wallpaper under crystal chandeliers. Receive a glass of complimentary wine upon check in; an onsite restaurant serves French fare. Scenic trails with covered bridges and local farms surround the inn.
  HISTORIC HOMES, INNS AND DEEPLY STORIED HOTELS 
  Story and pedigree, felt in the architecture, design and personality of every aspect of the property. 
        Beverly Wilshire, Beverly Hills, California: A Two-Key hotel that's been a popular Hollywood film backdrop and Rodeo Drive institution since 1928. Known for its highly regarded spa and Italian-style pool. Beyond the lively pool bars and lounges, dine at Wolfgang Puck’s MICHELIN-selected CUT.
The Mining Exchange, Colorado: Originally the Colorado Springs Mining Stock Exchange, authentic steel vaults remain on every floor of this One-Key, while a Victorian-style lobby boasts gold-toned furnishings and an antique Franz Wirth piano. Each high-ceilinged room showcases a locally commissioned artwork. Stick around for happy hour on weekdays and live jazz on Thursdays, with the rest of downtown Colorado Springs just right outside the door.
The Hay-Adams Hotel, District of Columbia: A 1928 Italian Renaissance monument famous for its discretion and coveted address: neighbors include the White House and National Mall. Upgraded from One to Two-Keys, white rooms come with plush furnishings and suites come with Juliet balconies. A subterranean bar adorned by irreverent political caricatures complements a sophisticated dining scene.
The Douglas, Georgia: Savannah’s Historic District is the perfect setting for this adults-only, 22-room, One-Key luxury boutique in a restored 1850s townhouse, where no two rooms are alike and retro elements prevail. A guests-only bar offers complimentary canapés every afternoon, a host of restaurants within walking distance.
The Ritz-Carlton, New Orleans, Louisiana: A French Quarter landmark inside the 1908 Beaux Arts Maison Blanche building, once a grand department store. Rooms at this One-Key are meant to echo Garden District mansions in their residential touches, and a spa, among the largest in New Orleans, offers more than 100 treatments. Nightly, jazz musician Jeremy Davenport headlines the lounge that bears his name.
XV Beacon, Massachusetts: An ultra-discrete pre-Revolutionary mansion one block north of Boston Common that’s listed on the National Registry of Historic Places. Elegant guest rooms at this One-Key hotel come with Italian marble, cashmere throws and heated towel racks; downstairs, find a modern steakhouse and wine cellar. An around-the-clock concierge secures everything from reservations to Red Sox tickets.
The Townsend Hotel, Michigan: Classic luxury in the Detroit suburbs at this One-Key, with swanky guest rooms including nine individual penthouses that, legend has it, once hosted Michael Jackson. A fine-dining restaurant, cocktail bar and afternoon tea brings a Downton Abbey vibe. Shopping and parks abound; downtown Detroit is a 20-minute drive.
                Eight suites, One Key at Sophie's Gasthaus outside San Antonio. © Sophie’s Gasthaus
        Waldorf Astoria New York, New York: Conrad Hilton called it “The Greatest of them All” and after a seven-year restoration, the Park Avenue legend re-emerges fully restored. 375 elegant rooms at this Two-Key offer high-quality amenities, and the suites are among the most coveted for their Park Avenue views. In the Waldorf Astoria Suite, there’s even a grand piano.
The Lowell, New York: A dignified, family-owned hotel in Upper East Side has been upgraded from One to Two Keys, where rooms come with woodburning fireplaces and landscaped terraces that show off city views. Rooms and suites are classy and singular, martinis are perfect, and the concierge service is famously well-connected and discrete.
The Surrey, A Corinthia Hotel, New York: Dating to 1926 and refreshed in recent years, this discreet Upper East Side sanctuary features four signature suites with crown moldings and bespoke details inspired by the bridges of Central Park. Upgraded from One to Two Keys, it is one of New York’s most storied hotels, its Art Deco edifice having welcomed everyone from John F. Kennedy to Bette Davis.
Hotel 1928, Texas: A 1928 Moorish Revival landmark, once known for its dances and banquets, now reimagined with an Art Deco flair. This One-Key sports original arched windows and hardwood floors layered with contemporary tiles and sparkling chandeliers. Find stylish rooms — the largest suite sleeps 12 — a café, a two-story library and rooftop dining.
Sophie’s Gasthaus, Texas: In San Antonio’s German enclave of New Braunfels on the fringes of San Antonio, European soul meets Texas hospitality in a One-Key, eight-suite, 1906 mansion. Rooms come with white-noise sleep machines; beyond, a pool, café, and art thanks to a partnership with Austin’s West Chelsea Contemporary. Walkability to town center makes it an ideal weekend getaway.
The Boulevard Inn, Virginia: Richmond’s Fan District is home to this charming, One-Key, 1914 Colonial Revival townhouse, where five rooms with high ceilings, brick walls and antique touches blend with modern amenities. Guests enjoy a continental breakfast, plus wine and cheese happy hours in the common areas.
Hotel Burg, Virginia: In a town that sheltered the National Archives during the Civil War and later became a retreat for the D.C. political class, an 1885-vintage, One-Key estate with classic rooms, condos and multiple suites — the Rooftop Suite includes a private outdoor terrace with its own fireplace — surrounded by vineyards and horse farms.
