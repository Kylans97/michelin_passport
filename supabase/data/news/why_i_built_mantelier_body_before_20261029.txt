I studied hotel management in Maastricht, but the thing that stuck came later — during an internship at Moments, the restaurant inside the Mandarin Oriental in Barcelona. I worked front of house, in a room that held two stars at the time. That is where you learn what an evening is actually made of: the timing, the room, the small decisions nobody notices when they go right. I have never stopped caring about it since.

What I wanted, and never found, was somewhere to keep track of it. Where I had been. Where I still wanted to go. And above all: what was actually happening out there — the one-off dinners, the visiting chefs, the nights that exist once and then don't.

Those evenings are the ones I remember. A four-hands dinner announced on a Tuesday. A cellar opening its doors for a single night. They are easy to miss, and almost impossible to find unless you already know where to look.

So I started building the place I was looking for. Mantelier is that place, and you are seeing it early.

Thank you for testing. It is rough in places and some things will break — tell me when they do. Everything you send me shapes what this becomes.

What's next: more guides, more events beyond the Netherlands, and a way for chefs and restaurants to announce their own nights directly.

— Kylan
