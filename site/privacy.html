Privacy

What Mantelier collects, why, and what you can ask us to do about it. Written to be read, not to be survived.

Who is responsible

Mantelier is run by Kylan Scheepstra, based in the Netherlands. For anything in this policy — including access, correction or deletion requests — write to hello@mantelier.app and you'll get a reply from a person.

Under the GDPR, we are the data controller for the personal data described below.

What we collect

When you join the waiting list

Your email address, and the date you submitted it. Nothing else — no name, no tracking pixel, no profiling. We use it to tell you about the app.

When you create an account

Your email address and a password (stored hashed — we never see it). Sign-in is handled by Supabase Auth. Your profile holds a username, a display name, a member number shown on your Passport, and optionally your home country and a profile photo. It also records whether other members may find you by searching; that is on by default, and you can switch it off under Privacy in the app.

What you record in the app

  • Visits and stays: the date, your ratings, the menu you had, your notes and your photos
  • Your wishlist, the venues you follow, your trips and the visits you plan
  • Events you mark Interested, Going or attended, and any rating you give them
  • Friend requests, friendships and blocks, and the dinner invitations you send friends, with any note
  • Reports and corrections you send us about content or a venue

We don't ask for the location data inside your photos, and the app resizes each photo before it is uploaded.

If you claim a venue

If you are a chef, restaurateur or hotelier and claim a listing, we record your role, a business email address and phone number, any notes, and what you submit for the venue — descriptions, photos, events, and the names and short biographies of its team. Every submission is reviewed by hand before it appears. When you claim a venue or submit something, we email ourselves a notification containing your name, username, account email and the contact details you gave.

How you use the app

Linked to your account, we record when you open a venue, event or news page; when you tap through from a venue to its website, booking page, phone number or directions; which main screens you open; and that you searched or changed a filter in Explore — never what you searched for. This tells us which parts of the product are useful. We do not follow you to other sites, we do not know whether you booked anything, and we use no third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting tools.

Push notifications

If you allow them, we store your device's push token, its app version and when it was last seen, so we can tell you about friend requests and new events at venues you follow. You can switch each kind off in the app, or all of them in your device settings.

Location

If you allow it, the app uses your device's location to show what is near you. It is used on your device and not sent to us. Permission is asked at the moment it is needed, and you can refuse or withdraw it in your device settings without losing access to the rest of the app.

Technical data

Our hosting providers process standard technical data — IP address, device type, timestamps — as part of delivering the service and keeping it secure.

Why we are allowed to hold it

Account data, the content you create, venue claims and push notifications: because we need them to provide the service you asked for (contract). Waiting-list emails: because you gave them to us for that purpose (consent — withdraw any time by replying to any message). Usage data, security logs and the notification emails about claims and reports: because we have a legitimate interest in knowing whether the product works and in protecting it from abuse. Location and push notifications are also subject to your consent, which you give and withdraw in your device settings.

Who else sees it

Our processors

  • Supabase — database, authentication and file storage, in the European Union
  • Resend — the notification emails we send ourselves about venue claims, submissions and reports
  • Apple — App Store and TestFlight distribution, and delivery of push notifications
  • MapTiler — map images when you choose a location on a map
  • Cloudflare — hosting and delivery of this website
  • Google Fonts — typefaces in the app and on this website, which your device fetches from Google

These providers act on our instructions and may not use your data for their own purposes. Some of them operate outside the European Economic Area; where that happens, transfers rely on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. If you tap Directions, the venue opens in your maps app, which handles your data under its own policy.

Other people in the app

  • Members who search. Unless you switch it off, members can find you by name or username and see your display name, username and profile photo.
  • Friends, and people with a pending friend request with you, see your profile photo.
  • Friends see the visits, stays and attended events you mark as visible to friends — with their date, ratings, notes and photos — and the events you mark Interested or Going, which are shared with friends by default. Your wishlist is visible to your friends. Everything else stays private.
  • Trip members see the trips you add them to and the visits planned in them.

Removing a friend or blocking someone takes effect immediately, on their next request — there is no cached access. A block hides you from each other entirely.

Community rankings

Every rating you give a visit, stay or attended event — including private ones — counts towards that venue's average in Rankings. A venue only appears once at least three members have rated it, and only the average and the number of members are shown: never who rated it, or how.

Venue pages

Descriptions, photos, events and team names that a venue's manager submits and we approve are public on that venue's page.

Restaurants, hotels and partners

We intend to share aggregated statistics with venues and partners — for example, how many people looked at a listing or tapped through to a booking page in a given month.

This is built so that individual people cannot be identified. Aggregates are suppressed entirely below five unique users, individual identifiers and precise timestamps are never included, and the underlying records are not readable by anyone outside Mantelier. We do not sell personal data, and we do not share data that can be traced back to you.

When the law requires it

If we receive a valid legal order, we comply. If we are permitted to tell you, we will.

How long we keep it

Account data, the content you create and your usage records: for as long as your account exists. Push tokens: until you sign out on that device or delete your account. Waiting-list emails: until you ask us to remove you. Notification emails about claims and reports: as long as we need them to handle the claim or report.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app, under Profile. This removes your profile and profile photo, your visits, stays, photos and ratings, your wishlist, follows, trips and event attendance, your friendships, blocks and invitations, your usage records and push tokens, and your venue claims and permissions. Your ratings stop counting towards Rankings.

Some things stay: content a venue has already published — approved photos and events — remains on that venue's page without your name attached, and notification emails we have already received about your claims or reports remain. Our providers' backups expire on their own schedule.

If you would rather we did it for you, write to hello@mantelier.app.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, ask us to restrict or stop a particular use, and ask for it in a portable format. You can also object to processing we base on legitimate interest.

Write to hello@mantelier.app and we will respond within one month. If you are unhappy with how we handle it, you can complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Children

Mantelier is not intended for children under 16. We do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, write to us and we will remove it.

Changes

If we change how we use your data in a way that matters, we will update this page and change the date at the top. If the change is significant, we will tell you in the app or by email rather than expecting you to notice.
