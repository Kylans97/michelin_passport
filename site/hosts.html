For venue hosts

Welcome to Mantelier

Your restaurant is part of a community that travels for exceptional gastronomy. Here is how to make the most of it.

Write for a world of guests

Mantelier is read by members across Europe and beyond. Please write your about text, photo captions and event descriptions in English, so every member can discover what you offer.

Evenings that happen once

Events on Mantelier are not your regular menu. They are the nights guests plan a journey around:

  • A special tasting, from a rare vintage to a single-ingredient menu
  • A collaboration with another kitchen, four hands or a guest chef
  • An exceptional setting, away from your usual dining room

Unique for guests, visible for you

Every event gives the Mantelier community the chance to experience something they could not find anywhere else. In return, we bring your evening to members who follow you, plan trips nearby and travel for moments like this.

A stamp of your own

Members keep every visit in their Passport. Soon you can design your venue's stamp with us, so each guest leaves with a keepsake that is unmistakably yours.

Getting started

Own or run a restaurant, hotel or private-chef practice that is on Mantelier? In the app, go to Profile and choose "Claim your venue". Once your claim is approved, go to Profile, then My venues, to manage your page and create your first event.

Questions? Write to claimedvenues@mantelier.app.
