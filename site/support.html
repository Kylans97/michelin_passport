Support

Help with Mantelier, from a person.

Contact

Write to hello@mantelier.app with anything — a question, a problem, a correction to a venue — and you'll get a reply from a person, usually within a day.

Common questions

I didn't receive the confirmation email

Check your spam folder first. If it isn't there, write to us from the address you signed up with and we will help.

I forgot my password

Tap "Forgot password?" on the sign-in screen and follow the link we email you.

A venue's details are wrong, or a venue is missing

Write to us with what you noticed. A missing restaurant, hotel or event can also be reported from Explore when a search finds nothing. Venue owners can claim their page and keep it up to date themselves.

Reporting content or a person

You can report a photo, a visit or a profile from where you see it, and block anyone from their profile. We review reports within 24 hours.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Profile and choose "Delete account". This removes your account and what you recorded in it — see the privacy policy for exactly what. You can also ask us to do it for you at hello@mantelier.app.
