## 1. La Liste — Top 1000 World's Best Restaurants

### Publisher & independence
- Operated by Société de la Tournelle de Béthune, 22 Quai de Béthune, 75004 Paris (from the site's T&C page). Founded 2015 by Philippe Faure (former French ambassador to Japan, ex-CEO of Gault&Millau, ex-head of the French tourist board). It launched with French government support. It was set up partly as a French answer to World's 50 Best (Wikipedia; Toyo Keizai, **unverified** detail).
- Method: an algorithm that aggregates scores from guides (MICHELIN, Gault&Millau, others), press reviews and consumer review sites (Wikipedia cites more than 700 guides and publications plus TripAdvisor/Yelp-type sources). There are no inspectors or voting panel of its own. Since 2025–2026 it also includes chef votes, per its 10th-anniversary coverage (**unverified**).
- Corporate sponsors named on Wikipedia include Moët Hennessy, American Express, AB InBev, Rungis and Accor (**unverified** for the current edition). I found no evidence that restaurants pay to be listed.
- Criticisms: it is a meta-aggregation, so it largely restates MICHELIN and Gault&Millau. Scores are heavily tied at the top (9–10 restaurants at 99.5 in 2026). It has also been accused of a French bias. I found no credible pay-to-play allegation.

### Scale
- A score out of 100 (top 99.5). The restaurant pages show a score and a "Top 1000 Restaurants" badge but **no numeric rank**, and the list pages show scores, not position numbers. Ties are common.

### Coverage & count
- The Top 1000 is titled "1,000", but the destination breakdown on laliste.com/en/restaurants shows **1,273 restaurants in 54 countries / 80 cities** (fetched 2026-11-16). The overflow is presumably from ties at the cut-off; this is not verified. The wider app database claims 35,000+ restaurants in 200 countries, but that is a directory, not the ranking.

### Cadence & latest edition
- Annual, announced in Paris in late November. 2026 edition: late November 2025 (exact date not verified; press coverage from 25–29 Nov 2025).
- **The 2027 edition is announced on the homepage for "November 23"**. The page is © 2026, so this is almost certainly **23 November 2026, one week from today**. The year is inferred, not stated on the page.

### Openness & format
- Free web pages, with no login or paywall seen, plus a free iOS/Android app. Each venue has a per-venue page.

### Terms of use
- T&C (laliste.com/legal/terms-conditions): content is "owned or provided by the Owner or its licensors"; users may not "copy, download, modify, publish, sell" or make derivative works; commercial exploitation needs "express prior written permission". There is no specific database-right clause and no scraping clause.
- Implication: showing a fact with a link ("La Liste 2026: 98.5/100") is low risk. Bulk extraction of a substantial part of the list would likely engage EU sui generis database right; La Liste is an EU (French) maker, so that right clearly applies. Hand-verified per-venue facts with a link are the defensible pattern; scraping the full table is not. **Recommend asking La Liste for written permission** before showing it at scale, since it explicitly reserves commercial use.

### Matching
- **Strong.** Each venue page has a stable slug URL (e.g. `/en/places/guy-savoy-paris-fr`), a full street address, coordinates (in the Directions link: 48.8567046, 2.3391491 for Guy Savoy), the official website, phone and price level. A human can confirm against our row's address, coordinates or website without ever relying on the name.

### Recommendation
- **Hold now, but take the 2027 edition** (due 23 Nov 2026), not the 2026 one. It is the most credible global list after MICHELIN and 50 Best, overlaps heavily with our high-end catalogue, and is the easiest to match. Store the score and edition year, not a rank, because the publisher shows none. Caveats: it adds little signal beyond MICHELIN and Gault&Millau because it aggregates them, and permission for commercial display should be requested.

- https://www.laliste.com/en/restaurants (1,273 restaurants / 54 countries)
- https://www.laliste.com/en/places/guy-savoy-paris-fr (per-venue fields)

## 3. OAD — Opinionated About Dining (Top Restaurants Europe / North America / Japan / Asia / Latin America; Classical, Casual, Cheap Eats, New)

### Publisher & independence
- Founded by Steve Plotnicki (New York), around 2004–2007. Rankings come from an online survey of registered diners whose votes are **weighted by experience**: how many restaurants they have visited and how highly those restaurants rank. Panel size per secondary sources: about 8,500 for Europe 2026 (vinaria.at, 22 June 2026); the Cision profile says "nearly 5,000 members". All **unverified**.
- There are no inspectors and no paid inclusion found. OAD runs events and dinners; Plotnicki has said the company "doesn't make any money" (The Week, dated). Criticisms: an opaque weighting algorithm; a self-selected panel of avid "destination diners", which skews toward cult tasting-menu places; and one person's influence over the method.

### Scale
- Numbered ranks for the top tier (e.g. Europe top ~150), then unranked tiers: "Highly Recommended" and "Recommended". Separate lists cover Classical, Casual, Cheap Eats and Top New restaurants.

### Coverage & count
- Regions: Europe, North America, Japan, Asia, Latin America. Europe 2026 "Top Restaurants Ranked" has **144 entries numbered 1–150 with gaps**, per the En Primeur Club mirror (**unverified**; rank 3 is missing in the mirror). Total across tiers is several hundred per region (**unverified**). 2026 Europe No. 1 Asador Etxebarri, No. 2 Bagá (secondary, **unverified**).

### Cadence & latest edition
- Annual per region, staggered. **Europe 2026: published about June 2026** (vinaria.at article dated 22 June 2026). North America 2026 and Asia 2026 exist according to aggregators (**unverified** dates). For Japan, 2025 is the latest seen on aggregators (**not verified** whether a 2026 Japan list exists).

### Openness & format
- **Could not verify on the primary source.** On 2026-11-16, `opinionatedaboutdining.com` timed out (HTTPS and HTTP) and `www.` returned an SSL certificate mismatch. `oad.com` is a **parked domain**. Historically the lists were free web pages on OAD's site plus an app. Today the lists are visible mainly through third-party aggregators (En Primeur Club, Pearl), and Pearl's data contains visible errors (wrong-year rank citations, a Zürich restaurant placed in Porto). **Aggregator copies must not be used as a source.**

### Terms of use
- **Not verified**: the site was unreachable. OAD is US-based, so US law has no EU database right, but republication into an EU product of data extracted from it is still governed by contract (site terms), which we could not read.

### Matching
- **Not verified on primary.** The aggregator mirrors carry only name, city and country. Historically OAD's own pages gave name, city and country with a link to a restaurant page; whether that page held an address or website is not verified. Expect weak-to-moderate matchability: city and country plus a human check of the venue's own website.

### Recommendation
- **Hold later.** It is credible among serious diners and overlaps strongly with our European high-end catalogue (Etxebarri, De Librije, JAN, Schauenstein and others). But the primary source is currently unreachable or unstable, the parked `oad.com` and certificate errors raise a maintainability concern, terms could not be read, and the per-venue identifiers are weak. Revisit once the primary site is confirmed live, and ask OAD for permission and a data feed. Never source from Pearl or En Primeur Club.

## 4. Asia's 50 Best Restaurants (The 50)

### Publisher & independence
- William Reed Ltd (UK), the same publisher as the held World's 50 Best, now branded **The 50**. The voting Academy covers the region's sub-regions; members cast ranked votes, and the process is adjudicated by a third party (Deloitte historically; it now says "third-party adjudication", firm not re-verified). Sponsored (S.Pellegrino/Acqua Panna historically, plus regional tourism-board host partners). The same criticisms apply as to the global list: sponsor and tourism-board influence on host cities, a lobbying-prone Academy, and opacity. The publisher states venues cannot pay to be listed.

### Scale
- Rank 1–100, plus Individual Awards (Best Restaurant in [country], Highest New Entry and others).

### Coverage & count
- 100 ranked restaurants across Asia (17 cities in the top 50 per the 2026 press release). 2026 No. 1: The Chairman, Hong Kong.

### Cadence & latest edition
- Annual. **2026 edition announced 25 March 2026**, Kerry Hotel, Hong Kong.

### Openness & format
- Free web pages: `the50.com/restaurants/best-in-asia/list/1-50` and `/51-100`. Ranks 1–50 link to per-restaurant pages. **Ranks 51–100 show only rank, name and city, with no per-venue page.**

### Terms of use
- Footer of the new site: no terms link (checked on a list page and a venue page, 2026-11-16). Probed `/terms-and-conditions`, `/terms`, `/legal` and `/privacy-policy` on the50.com: all 404. The old site's footer read "© William Reed Ltd … all rights reserved" and linked "Website Terms" (secondary). **Current website terms not verified.** William Reed is a UK maker, and the UK has its own database right. Whatever basis is already used for the held global list should be extended to cover the regional lists rather than assumed.

### Matching
- **Strong for 1–50.** Each venue page (e.g. `/restaurants/best-in-asia/the-list/jl-studio.html`) carries a full street address, the official website and phone, but no coordinates. **Weak for 51–100**: name and city only, so a human must find the venue's own site to confirm.

### Recommendation
- **Hold now (1–50); hold 51–100 only with manual confirmation.** Same publisher and method as the list we already hold, so one data model and one review process. Overlap with our catalogue depends on how far it extends into Asia (as a global audience product it is relevant).

## 9. The Best Chef Awards

### Publisher & independence
- An independent company founded in Poland by Joanna Slusarczyk and Cristian Gadau (founders from prior knowledge; **not re-verified** on primary). It is funded by sponsors (the site shows about 25 sponsor logos) and **host-city/tourism partners** (Dubai 2024, Milan 2025, Mexico 2023). It is a **peer vote**: in 2025, 972 voters (572 chefs from 64 countries plus 400 industry professionals), per secondary reporting. Criticism: nominated chefs vote for each other, and the awards are very inclusive (126 chefs at "Three Knives" in 2025), which dilutes the signal.

### Scale
- Since 2024, a **Knives** tier system: One Knife (Excellent), Two Knives (World Class), Three Knives (The Best). There is still a "#1 The Best Chef" and top positions (e.g. Rasmus Munk #1 in 2024 and 2025). Before 2024 it was a Top 100 ranking (2017–2023).

### Coverage & count
- Global. Several hundred chefs across tiers (exact 2025 count not verified; 126 at Three Knives, secondary).

### Cadence & latest edition
- Annual since 2017. **Latest: 2025, Milan, 1–2 October 2025.** **As of 2026-11-16 I found no 2026 edition**: no announcement or results, and the official site still shows "Milan 2025" as the latest event and guide. This suggests a pause or slip, not verified either way.

### Openness & format
- Free web guide with map and list views and per-chef pages.

### Terms of use
- No terms link visible on the homepage (not verified further).

### Matching
- **Chef-keyed, not venue-keyed.** A chef page (e.g. `/chefs/rasmus-munk/`) carries the restaurant name, full street address, city, country and website; no coordinates. The award attaches to a person, who can move between restaurants.

### Recommendation
- **Leave out for now; possibly hold later at the chef level.** The peer-vote method and broad knife tiers are weak signals for a luxury register. No 2026 edition exists to show. If we want chef accolades on chef profiles later, this is the only global candidate, but it is not a venue award.

## 10. James Beard Foundation Restaurant and Chef Awards

### Publisher & independence
- The James Beard Foundation, a US non-profit. Process: open call for entries, then semifinalists, nominees and winners, voted by **volunteer subcommittees and judges**. There is a Code of Ethics and an independent Ethics Committee (reformed after 2021–2023). **Presented by American Express and Resy**; category sponsors include Acqua Panna (Outstanding Restaurant) and S.Pellegrino (Emerging Chef). Controversies: 2020 awards cancelled, the 2023 ethics disqualification and judge resignations, and a 2026 HR-investigation story (Colorado Sun, Feb 2026).

### Scale
- Win or nomination per category. No score or rank.

### Coverage & count
- **US only.** 2026: 13 national restaurant/beverage categories plus 12 regional "Best Chef" categories, so 25 winners. Semifinalists and nominees number in the hundreds (not counted).

### Cadence & latest edition
- Annual. **2026 winners announced 15 June 2026**, Lyric Opera of Chicago (Chicago hosts through 2028). Entries for 2027 are open.

### Openness & format
- Free web article listing winners (jamesbeard.org/stories/james-beard-award-winners-2026). The format is plain text: chef name, business, city and state. No per-winner links.

### Terms of use
- No terms-of-use page found; the footer links only Privacy and has "© … James Beard Foundation. All rights reserved"; "James Beard Awards®" is a registered mark. US law has no database right. Stating facts ("2026 James Beard Award winner, Outstanding Chef") with a link is low risk; use of the logo or mark beyond nominative use would need care. **Terms not verified.**

### Matching
- **Weak-to-moderate.** Business name plus city and state only, with no URL, ID or address. Winners must be matched by a human against our row's website and address.

### Recommendation
- **Hold later**, winners only (not semifinalists), and only for national categories plus Best Chef where the venue is already in our catalogue. Credible and well known, but US-only, the categories mix casual and fine dining, and the awards are chef-centric and weakly identified. Low priority for a Benelux-first product.
