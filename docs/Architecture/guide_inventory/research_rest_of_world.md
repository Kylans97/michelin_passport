# C — Rest of world: national/regional restaurant guides (Asia, Pacific, Americas, Africa, Middle East)

Researched 2026-11-16. Read-only; nothing written to any database.

How to read this file:
- Primary sources were opened where possible. Where a figure comes only from press or an aggregator (joinpearl.co, enprimeurclub.com, regional newspapers), it is marked **unverified**.
- Several primary sites refused automated fetches (HTTP 403): bluer.co.kr (Blue Ribbon), agfg.com.au (AGFG), the ACM regional mirrors of the Good Food Guide 2027 list. Their state is marked "not verified" rather than guessed.
- "Terms of use" notes are a reading of what the sites say. They are not legal advice. A general point applies to all of them: that a restaurant holds an award is a fact, and a short factual statement with a link ("Black Pearl ◆◆◆ 2026") is low risk. Copying a full list, including tiers and the publisher's own descriptions, into our database is the act that copyright/database-right terms are aimed at. EU database right (we are EU-based) protects the *publisher's* substantial investment in a database; extracting a substantial part of a list is the risk, not mentioning one entry. Ask for written permission before importing a full list from any publisher with an explicit reproduction ban (Tabelog first).

## 1. Tabelog Award (食べログアワード) — Japan

### Publisher & independence
Kakaku.com, Inc. (Tokyo-listed; also runs the price-comparison site Kakaku.com). The Tabelog Award is derived from **user ratings and user votes** on the Tabelog review platform, not from inspectors: Gold/Silver/Bronze are decided by user votes among restaurants that rated highly during the year; a separate "Chefs' Gold" is voted by the chefs of nominated restaurants. Tabelog sells paid restaurant memberships (listing enhancements) — the platform states paid status does not affect scores; I did not re-verify that statement this session. From memory and **not re-verified this session**: a Tokyo District Court ruling in 2022 ordered Kakaku.com to pay damages to a yakiniku chain over a scoring-algorithm change, later overturned on appeal — worth a check before relying on it, but it is the main public credibility controversy.

### Scale
Award tiers: Gold / Silver / Bronze, plus Chefs' Gold, Best New Entry, Best Regional Restaurants. Underlying Tabelog score is 0–5 (top restaurants sit ~4.0–4.7); the award, not the score, is the citable credential.

### Coverage & count
Japan only. 2026 edition: 36 Gold, 160 Silver, 537 Bronze (733 total); 13 Chefs' Gold, 12 Best New Entry, 6 Best Regional. Verified on the official award page (fractions 36/733, 160/733, 537/733) and in press.

### Cadence & latest edition
Annual, announced late January. Tabelog Award 2026 announced **26 January 2026** (press; the official page shows no date). Next (2027) expected late January 2027 — not yet out.

### Openness & format
Open web. award.tabelog.com shows all 36 Gold entries; Silver and Bronze are previewed with "More" links to full lists. English mirror of "Best Restaurants in Japan" (Award + Hyakumeiten winners, 3,833 entries) at s.tabelog.com/en/TopRestaurants/. No paywall seen.

### Terms of use
Strict. Tabelog terms (https://tabelog.com/help/rules/): Art. 8(1) all content and information belong to Kakaku.com or rights holders; Art. 8(3) without permission users may not reproduce, edit, post, repost, publicly transmit, distribute, sell, provide or translate it ("複製､編集､改変､掲載､転載､公衆送信…"); Art. 9(1)(1) bans reproducing Tabelog-provided information by copying or other means without prior consent; Art. 6(1)(3) sets a ¥10,000-per-review penalty for commercial reuse of reviews. Scraping is not named explicitly but falls under the catch-alls (Art. 9(1)(7) interfering with operations, 9(1)(12) anything deemed inappropriate). A single factual badge "Tabelog Award 2026 Gold" with a link is a factual statement, but building our list from their pages is exactly what Art. 8(3)/9(1)(1) prohibit. **Get written permission before importing the list.**

### Matching
Good for verification. Every award entry links to a stable Tabelog restaurant URL with a numeric ID, e.g. https://tabelog.com/aichi/A2301/A230104/23080047/ (prefecture/area/sub-area/ID). The award page itself shows only name (Japanese + romanised), genre and prefecture — no street address; the per-restaurant page carries the address and map. Romanisation is inconsistent (e.g. 橦木町 しみず → "Shimizu"); many sushi/kaiseki restaurants share short names (several "Sushi Saito"-like names), so a human must confirm via the Tabelog ID page's address against our coordinates. Storing the Tabelog ID per venue would make this reliable.

### Recommendation
**Hold later.** It is the most-used credential inside Japan and its Gold/Silver tiers overlap heavily with a high-end catalogue, but (a) it is user-vote-driven, not inspected, and (b) the terms explicitly forbid reproduction without consent. Approach Kakaku.com for permission; if granted, hold Gold and Silver (Bronze is broad). Store as a factual badge with link to the Tabelog page.

- https://tabelog.com/help/rules/ (opened)
- https://s.tabelog.com/en/TopRestaurants/ (opened)
- https://gourmet.watch.impress.co.jp/docs/news/2081169.html (press, 2026 results)
- https://www.advertimes.com/?p=532403 (press)

## 3. Black Pearl Restaurant Guide (黑珍珠餐厅指南) — China + Greater China + overseas

### Publisher & independence
Meituan (owner of Dianping, China's largest restaurant-review app), launched January 2018. Selection described by the publisher and press as anonymous judge visits plus "data-driven insights" from the Meituan/Dianping platform and cross-verification. Launch-era reports: ~200 anonymous judges, about a third chefs, each nominated restaurant reviewed by at least three judges; later reports: majority "foodies", 30% annual rotation, five-year maximum term, no industry-tied judges (all **unverified** for 2026). Independence concerns: the publisher is a commercial platform that also sells services to restaurants, and the exact method is not published; the guide is widely framed as "China's Michelin" and a soft-power project. Not a pay-to-play guide on the evidence found.

### Scale
One, two or three diamonds (◆ special-occasion, ◆◆ memorable celebrations, ◆◆◆ highest). Plus "Annual Dish" and individual chef honours.

### Coverage & count
Two lists per year:
- Mainland China 2026: 263 restaurants in 32 cities (China Daily). Reported 6 three-diamond restaurants nationally (**unverified**, Chinese regional press).
- Hong Kong, Macau, Taiwan & overseas 2026: 135 restaurants including Singapore, Tokyo, Bangkok and (new) Seoul. Singapore three-diamonds: Les Amis, Odette; Macau: Jade Dragon, Robuchon au Dôme (press).
Total ~398 (one outlet says 377 — conflict **unresolved**).

### Cadence & latest edition
Annual. 2026 mainland list released in Quanzhou, Fujian, on **27 January 2026** (Chinese press; China Daily article dated 29 Jan). 2026 Greater China/overseas list unveiled **23 March 2026** at Marina Bay Sands, Singapore. 2027 not yet out.

### Openness & format
The full list lives in the Dianping/Meituan app and dianping.com, in Simplified Chinese (per secondary sources; I could not open a list page — blackpearl.meituan.com returned only a title). Press in English (Time Out SG, HungryGoWhere, Macau outlets) publishes market sub-lists. No paywall reported, but app-first and Chinese-language.

### Terms of use
**Not verified.** Meituan/Dianping terms were not read this session. Chinese platforms generally claim rights in their data, and Dianping has litigated scraping of its reviews (Dianping v. Baidu, 2016, unfair-competition ruling — from memory, not re-verified). A factual badge with link is low risk; bulk extraction from the app is not advisable without permission.

### Matching
Hard for mainland entries: names are Chinese, romanisation varies, and many restaurants sit in hotels with brand-name restaurants repeated across cities. Each Dianping listing has a numeric shop ID with address and map, which is the reliable key — but requires reading the Chinese app. Overseas/HK/Macau/SG entries are reported with English names and hotel affiliation (e.g. MGM, Melco, Galaxy press releases), which a human can confirm against our hotel_restaurants links.

### Recommendation
**Hold now — start with the Greater China/overseas list**, mainland later. It is the most credible Chinese-language guide, its three tiers map cleanly, and three-diamond/two-diamond venues overlap strongly with a high-end catalogue (many in our Key hotels). The overseas list is small (135), English-reported and easy to verify; the mainland list needs a Chinese-reading reviewer and Dianping IDs.

### Publisher & independence
Tatler Asia (Tatler Asia Group; licensed Tatler brand, separate from Condé Nast's UK Tatler). ### Scale
Best 20 Restaurants per market (unranked list), Best-in-Class category awards (Restaurant of the Year, Best New, Best Service…), and the regional Tatler Best 100 Restaurants Asia-Pacific (third edition in 2026).

### Coverage & count
Markets: Hong Kong, Macau, Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand, Philippines, Indonesia (and Taiwan/China, not verified). ~20 per market; 100 regionally. HK & Macau 2026: 141 awards incl. hotels and bars.

### Cadence & latest edition
Annual per market. Tatler Best HK & Macau 2026 ceremony **11 May 2026**; Singapore Best 20 published **13 April 2026**; Tatler Best Asia Pacific 2026 ceremony in Shanghai, **22 October 2026** (Wing, Hong Kong = Restaurant of the Year).

### Openness & format
Open web pages on tatlerasia.com, with a per-restaurant Tatler page. No paywall seen; digital magazine also on Zinio.

### Terms of use
Not read in full. Standard magazine copyright; no explicit database clause seen.

### Matching

### Recommendation

Revisit once the Tatler Best format has run unchanged for another cycle; the regional Best 100 would be the one to hold.

## 12. AAA Diamonds (restaurants and hotels) — USA, Canada, Mexico, Caribbean, Costa Rica

### Publisher & independence
AAA (American Automobile Association, a not-for-profit federation of motor clubs). Professional inspectors visit unannounced, sometimes anonymously, for nearly 90 years; Five Diamond hotels get an overnight stay. Properties must first be "AAA Approved" (meet baseline requirements); a property can decline participation. No payment for ratings (long-standing policy; not re-verified).

### Scale
Approved, Three, Four, Five Diamonds (One/Two were retired); plus a "Top 10" selection inside Five Diamond.

### Coverage & count
28,000+ Diamond-designated properties (hotels + restaurants). ~65 Five Diamond restaurants and ~145 Five Diamond hotels as of February 2026 (press — **unverified**).

### Cadence & latest edition
Continuous designations, with an annual list published early in the year (2026 list = designations from 2025 inspections), mid-year additions (e.g. Atomix NYC Feb 2026; Chez Philippe Memphis Mar 2026; hotels May 2026) and Top 10 lists released **12 June 2026**.

### Openness & format
Open: annual Five Diamond restaurant and hotel PDFs on newsroom.aaa.com (e.g. AAA-Five-Diamond-Restaurants-2026-3.pdf, listing restaurant, city and year first awarded); full directory on AAA TripCanvas.

### Terms of use
Not read. Newsroom PDFs are press material intended for publication.

### Matching
PDF lists name + city + state/province + year first awarded; hotel restaurants named with their hotel (e.g. Chez Philippe at The Peabody). Good enough for human verification.

### Recommendation
**Hold now — Five Diamond only, restaurants and hotels.** Inspector-led, transparent tiers, press-ready PDFs, and it is one of the few credible ratings that covers **hotels** (useful for our `hotels` table in North America/Mexico, where MICHELIN Keys are recent). Four Diamond is too broad for our register.
