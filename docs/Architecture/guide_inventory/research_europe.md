# B — European national/regional restaurant guides

Researched 2026-11-16. Web checks done today with WebSearch and WebFetch. Some sites refused automated fetches (gamberorosso.it 403, falstaff.com 403, ratedtrips.com 403, whiteguide.com classification page 500). Where that happened, the finding is marked **not verified**. Any count taken from press or an aggregator rather than the publisher is marked **(unverified, secondary)**.

None of this is legal advice. The terms notes say what each site's own terms page states. The EU database right (Directive 96/9/EC) protects against extracting or re-using a *substantial part* of a database. Showing a single award fact per venue, with attribution and a link, is a very different act from mirroring a whole list. Everything below is assessed on that basis.

## 1. Gault&Millau (selected markets: NL, BE/LU, FR, DE, AT, CH)

### Publisher & independence
- The brand belongs to Gault&Millau International SA, which holds the trademark ([FR CGU](https://fr.gaultmillau.com/fr/pages/cgu)). Each country runs under a **national licensee**:
  - NL: Gault&Millau Nederland bv, Naarden ([legal notice](https://www.gault-millau.nl/en/legal-notice)).
  - BE/LU: GaultMillau Benelux nv, Hasselt ([wettelijke info](https://www.gaultmillau.be/nl/wettelijke-informatie)).
  - CH: Ringier AG / Ringier Medien Schweiz ([gaultmillau.ch](https://www.gaultmillau.ch/)).
  - AT: the site shows only "© Gault & Millau". The licensee entity was **not verified** (site Impressum at /impressum).
- The guide is inspector-based, scoring out of 20. Licensees earn money from book sales, events, partner sponsorship and logo/plaque sales. Independence therefore varies by licensee and is not uniform. No paid-inclusion evidence was found in this pass.
- **Germany: the guide has no current edition.** The licence with Henris Edition ended after the 2025 guide, and there was **no 2026 German Gault&Millau** ([tageskarte](https://www.tageskarte.io/gastronomie/detail/verlag-henris-edition-verabschiedet-sich-vom-gault-millau-was-wird-aus-den-hauben-in-deutschland.html), [hogapage](https://www.hogapage.de/nachrichten/wirtschaft/gastronomie/aus-f%C3%BCr-gaultmillau-deutschland/)). Henris has pivoted to a pan-European **aggregator** platform, "Henris", with its own "Henris Diamonds". It combines Michelin, G&M, Gusto, Gambero Rosso and others, which is essentially the same idea as this task, so it is a competitor rather than a source.
- **Italy:** a Gault&Millau Italia guide with scores now exists ([gamberorosso.it report](https://www.gamberorosso.it/notizie/attualita/guida-ristoranti-gault-millau-italia-punteggi/)). Its date and size were not verified.

### Scale

### Coverage & count

### Openness & format
- **NL: open.** Per-restaurant pages at stable URLs show the score, full address, postcode, phone, website, hours, menus and prices, with no login seen ([example](https://www.gault-millau.nl/en/restaurants/de-librije-zwolle)). There is also a free app.
- **BE: open.** Same platform (Flying Elephant build) with per-restaurant pages ([example](https://www.gaultmillau.be/nl/restaurants/boury-roeselare)). The listing page shows scores and filters. Address fields on BE pages were not explicitly confirmed but are presumably the same as NL.
- **FR: open (free).** The CGU state the site offers "un accès gratuit" to search and consult establishments. Per-restaurant pages exist (`/fr/restaurants/<slug>`), and listings show the score and city. Full address on the detail page was not confirmed.
- **AT: open.** Restaurant list and per-restaurant pages (`/restaurant/<slug>`) with Hauben icons and no paywall seen. The listing shows region/city only; detail-page address not confirmed.
- **CH:** a restaurant list (/restaurants) and a map exist with no visible paywall. Whether the points are fully free was **not verified**. Ringier historically put some content behind subscription.
- **Print:** all markets sell books.

### Terms of use
- **NL:** the legal notice contains only "© 2026. All rights reserved". It has no explicit reuse, database or scraping clause.
- **BE:** name, logo and marks may not be used without prior written authorisation, and reproduction for merchandising or advertising is prohibited. It has no database or scraping clause.
- **FR (most explicit):**
  - Content is the property of G&M and its affiliates.
  - Reproduction, publication or copying needs prior permission.
  - Any commercial or advertising use is "strictement interdite".
  - Unauthorised reproduction is called "contrefaçon" under European IP and **database protection** law.
  - Anyone reproducing content "doit citer l'auteur et sa source".
- **Implication:** stating a fact ("Gault&Millau 2027: 17/20") with attribution and a link is the kind of use commonly treated as non-substantial. Using the **logo or toque iconography** clearly needs written permission. Bulk-copying a full market list into our database is the risk area under the database right.
- **Recommendation for G&M:** ask each licensee in writing for permission to show scores and the wordmark. NL and BE are the easiest, since both are Benelux and both have commercial "partner" programmes.

### Matching
- Strong for NL. Stable slug URL plus street address, postcode, phone and website on the page, so a human can confirm the exact venue.
- BE, FR and AT also use stable per-venue slug URLs. The address is visible in at least NL; the others were not confirmed.
- No public IDs or coordinates were seen beyond a Google Maps route link.

### Recommendation
- **Hold now** (continue) for **NL, BE/LU, FR, AT, CH**. These are credible, inspector-based, openly browsable, and have per-venue URLs.
- Store the edition year and the per-venue G&M URL as the verification key.
- **Germany: stop treating G&M DE as current.** Mark existing DE scores as "Gault&Millau 2025 (last German edition)" or retire them. Do not render them as current.
- Seek written permission before using logos or toques.

## 2. Gambero Rosso — Ristoranti d'Italia (Forchette)

### Publisher & independence
- Gambero Rosso S.p.A. (Rome), a media company that has also run a wine/food academy, TV and events for 40 years ([Milano Finanza](https://www.milanofinanza.it/news/gambero-rosso-festeggia-40-anni-2721907)).
- More than 100 contributors, coordinated by named editors ([scattidigusto](https://www.scattidigusto.it/guida-ristoranti-2027-gambero-rosso)). Whether visits are anonymous was not verified.
- It is a commercial publisher with events and sponsorship. No paid-inclusion evidence was found.

### Scale
- Score out of 100. From the 2027 edition this is a single overall score; the separate kitchen/cellar/service sub-scores were dropped.
- Symbols: **Forchette** (1–3) for restaurants, **Gamberi** for trattorie, **Bottiglie** for wine bars. "Mappamondi" was dropped ([scattidigusto](https://www.scattidigusto.it/guida-ristoranti-2027-gambero-rosso)).

### Coverage & count
- Italy only.
- 2027 edition: **2,573 addresses**, 316 new, **56 Tre Forchette**, top score Reale at 99/100 (unverified, secondary: [Il Sole 24 Ore](https://en.ilsole24ore.com/art/gambero-rosso-reale-niko-romito-conquista-guida-ristoranti-d-italia-2027-AJvdqauB), [l'Adige](https://www.ladige.it/cronaca/40-ristoranti-forchettati-tra-trentino-e-alto-adige-niederkofler-quarto-in-italia-wl0jdxte)).
- 2026 edition: 2,580 restaurants and 55 Tre Forchette.

### Cadence & latest edition
- Annual, in October.
- **2027 edition (38th) presented in Rome in late October 2026.** One source gives 26 Oct 2026 at La Nuvola; the exact date was **not verified** on the primary source.
- The 2026 edition was presented 20 Oct 2025 ([gamberorosso.it calendar](https://www.gamberorosso.it/notizie/attualita/date-presentazioni-guide-2026-gambero-rosso/)).

### Openness & format
- **Paywalled.** Restaurant listings on gamberorosso.it ask readers to choose a subscription (Premium Base / Plus, roughly €50–70 a year; prices not verified) ([abbonamenti](https://www.gamberorosso.it/abbonamenti)).
- Also sold as a print book, and through the Gambero Rosso+ app.
- The Tre Forchette list is published openly as news articles each year ([e.g. 2026](https://www.gamberorosso.it/notizie/rubriche/migliori-ristoranti-tre-forchette-2026/)).
- The site returned 403 to automated fetches, so this was not opened directly.

### Terms of use
- **Not verified**: the site blocked fetches. Expect the standard Italian "tutti i diritti riservati". Because the full guide is a paid product, re-using more than headline awards carries real database-right risk.

### Matching
- Per-venue pages exist on gamberorosso.it behind the paywall; their fields were not verified.
- The open Tre Forchette news lists carry name and town only.
- Workable for the 56 top venues by human check (town plus chef), but there is no open stable ID.

### Recommendation
- **Hold later, top tier only.** Tre Forchette is credible, and for top Italian venues it largely overlaps our catalogue.
- Show only "Tre Forchette 2027" (optionally Due) for venues already in our catalogue, verified by a human from the open annual list. Do not ingest scores out of 100.
- Ask for permission first.

- **Hold now** for Spain, Portugal later. Credible, open, stable IDs, a small high-end tier (46 and 173 at the top two levels) that overlaps strongly with our catalogue, and annual and easy to maintain.

## Cross-cutting notes
- **Henris (ex-G&M Germany licensee) is building an aggregator of all these guides.** It is a direct conceptual competitor; worth knowing, not a data source.
- **Never match on name.** The guides with stable per-venue IDs or URLs plus address are:
  - G&M NL, BE, FR and AT (slug URLs; NL shows full address).
  - Repsol (numeric ID in the URL).
  - Feinschmecker (full address in the list).

  Record that URL in the award row as the verification key. All the others offer name and town only, which is acceptable for small top lists checked by a human.
- **Permissions.** The FR G&M CGU and the GFG terms explicitly bar commercial reuse without permission. Treat every guide's logo and iconography as permission-only. Plain-text facts ("Tre Forchette 2027", linked) are lower-risk but not risk-free.
