# Permission request to La Liste — draft, not sent

2026-11-16. Drafted for the product owner to send; nothing was sent.

**To:** contact@laliste.com — the address La Liste's own terms page gives
(https://www.laliste.com/legal/terms-conditions). No dedicated press or
partnership address was found on the site.

**Why we ask:** La Liste's terms say users may not "reproduce, duplicate,
copy, sell, resell or exploit any portion" of the service "without the
Owner's express prior written permission". Showing a restaurant's La Liste
score on our pages is commercial use, so we ask before building it
(`docs/Architecture/GUIDE_INVENTORY.md`).

**Timing:** the 2027 ranking is announced on 23 November 2026; sending
before then lets La Liste answer with the new edition in mind.

**Subject:** Request to reference La Liste scores on Mantelier

Dear La Liste team,

Mantelier is an app and website for discovering and recording exceptional
restaurants and hotels, built in the Netherlands for a global audience.
Each restaurant page shows the recognition that restaurant holds from the
major guides side by side — the MICHELIN Guide, The World's 50 Best
Restaurants and Gault&Millau today — with each guide named and linked.

We would like to add La Liste, and we are asking for your written
permission first. What we would show:

- For restaurants already in our catalogue only, and listed in La Liste's
  Top 1000: the score and edition, in plain text (for example
  "La Liste 2027 · 98.5"), credited to La Liste, with a link to that
  restaurant's page on laliste.com.
- No La Liste logo or artwork unless you provide one for this use.
- No copy of your descriptions, reviews or rankings beyond that one fact,
  and no copy of the full list: we enter each score by hand for the
  restaurants we hold, starting with the 2027 edition.

If you prefer a different form of attribution, a data feed, or a
partnership arrangement, we are glad to follow it. We would also welcome a
contact for corrections, so that a score on our pages never differs from
yours.

Kind regards,

[name]
Mantelier
