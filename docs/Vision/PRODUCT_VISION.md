# Our Mission

Michelin Passport exists to preserve the world's most memorable culinary experiences.

Rather than helping users collect restaurants, Michelin Passport helps them collect memories.

Every exceptional meal, every remarkable hotel stay and every unforgettable journey deserves to be remembered exactly as it happened.

Michelin Passport is designed to become the personal archive of those experiences.

---

# Our Vision

We believe extraordinary hospitality deserves more than a checklist.

Michelin Passport aims to become the world's most elegant and personal culinary journal.

A place where Michelin-starred restaurants, Michelin Key hotels and exceptional dining experiences become part of a lifelong story.

Years from now, users should not only remember where they travelled, but also who they were with, how they felt and why those moments mattered.

---

# Why Michelin Passport Exists

Dining at an exceptional restaurant is rarely just about the food.

It is often connected to a celebration.

A honeymoon.

A birthday.

A business achievement.

A dream destination.

A special trip with family or friends.

These moments deserve to be preserved.

Michelin Passport exists to capture not only where those experiences happened, but the memories surrounding them.

---

# Who We Build For

Michelin Passport is built for people who intentionally seek exceptional hospitality.

Our users appreciate craftsmanship.

They value outstanding restaurants.

They travel for remarkable hotels.

They plan journeys around food.

They enjoy discovering the world's finest dining experiences.

Most importantly, they want to remember those experiences for years to come.

---

# What Michelin Passport Is

Michelin Passport is:

- A personal culinary journal.
- A premium travel companion.
- A lifelong collection of memories.
- A beautifully designed archive.
- A celebration of exceptional hospitality.

---

# What Michelin Passport Is Not

Michelin Passport is not:

- A restaurant review platform.
- A booking platform.
- A discount platform.
- A social media platform.
- A generic travel app.

The focus always remains on preserving personal experiences.

---

# Our Philosophy

Restaurants evolve.

Hotels evolve.

Awards change.

Rankings change.

Memories do not.

Michelin Passport should preserve those memories exactly as they happened.

History should never be rewritten.

---

# Core Principles

## Every Visit Tells a Story

Restaurants and hotels are destinations.

Visits are experiences.

The visit—not the location—is the centre of Michelin Passport.

Every visit represents a unique memory.

---

## History Is Sacred

Historical information should never be lost.

Michelin stars change.

Michelin Keys change.

World rankings change.

The user's memories remain permanent.

The past should always remain accessible.

---

## Personal Before Public

Objective information exists to support the user's personal experience.

The application should always prioritise personal memories over public ratings or popularity.

---

## Quality Over Quantity

Every feature must earn its place.

Michelin Passport should remain focused, refined and intentional.

We prefer fewer exceptional features over many average ones.

---

## Timeless Design

Design trends come and go.

Timeless products remain.

Michelin Passport should feel as elegant in ten years as it does today.

Simplicity creates longevity.

---

## Premium By Default

Every interaction should feel effortless.

Every animation should feel intentional.

Every screen should communicate quality.

Luxury is found in simplicity, clarity and attention to detail.

---

## Build For Decades

Michelin Passport should not only be enjoyable today.

It should still be meaningful twenty years from now.

The application should become more valuable as more memories are added over time.

---

# Product Pillars

Everything within Michelin Passport should strengthen one of these four pillars.

## Discover

Discover exceptional restaurants and hotels around the world.

Curated destinations inspire future journeys.

---

## Experience

Capture every visit with personal notes, photographs, ratings and memories.

The experience is always more important than the destination.

---

## Remember

Relive journeys through timelines, maps, historical awards and personal stories.

The application should become richer with every visit.

---

## Inspire

Encourage future adventures by helping users dream, plan and return to remarkable destinations.

Michelin Passport should inspire the next journey while preserving the last one.

---

# Design Philosophy

Michelin Passport should never feel overwhelming.

Instead, it should feel calm.

Elegant.

Premium.

Intentional.

The interface should disappear into the background, allowing memories, photography and storytelling to take centre stage.

Every screen should communicate quality over complexity.

---

# Long-Term Vision

Michelin Passport is not being built to become the largest restaurant database.

It is being built to become the most meaningful personal archive of exceptional hospitality.

Over time, users will not simply build a collection of restaurants.

They will build the story of a lifetime of travel, discovery and unforgettable experiences.

The application should grow alongside its users, becoming more valuable with every journey they make.

---

# The Michelin Passport Standard

Every decision made during the development of Michelin Passport should answer one simple question:

> **Does this help preserve or relive an exceptional experience?**

If the answer is no, it probably does not belong in Michelin Passport.
